## Variant implemented: seed-only NEWMV-search reuse, NEAREST+NEAR match-guarded

The cross-decoder-safe variant the HLD mandates. We cache the descent's full
`PickInterModeResult` per evaluated (mi_row, mi_col, bsize), then in the final
per-leaf RT-picker pass we REUSE the descent's per-ref NEWMV search RESULT
(skipping the diamond + sub-pel search — the costly part) **only when the final
pass's CAUSAL (nearest, near) MV pair matches the descent's** for that ref.

### Why it is cross-decoder-safe
- NEAREST/NEAR are NOT reused: re-derived on the causal grid by the picker; the
  existing DRIFT-FIX (lib.rs ~4571) re-pins them post-pick. Untouched.
- NEWMV absolute MV is reused only when (nearest, near) match → the fresh diamond
  (seeded from {nearest, near}, priced against nearest) would land on the SAME MV,
  so reuse is faithful. On mismatch we re-run the search.
- Result: emitted bitstreams are BYTE-IDENTICAL to baseline (verified, below), so
  cross-decoder validity is preserved by construction on the reused subset.

## Reuse mechanism (what's cached/reused vs re-derived)
- CACHED (descent_picker callback, lib.rs): full `PickInterModeResult` incl. the
  two new per-ref fields `newmv_search_last/golden` + the descent's `MvCandidates`
  for LAST and GOLDEN.
- REUSED (final RT pass): the per-ref NEWMV absolute MV, injected as
  `newmv_override_last/golden` into `PickInterModeParams`. `pick_inter_mode`
  pre-seeds `newmv_last/golden` from the overrides so `run_newmv_search` is
  skipped.
- RE-DERIVED (unchanged): NEAREST/NEAR via causal `find_mv_refs`; inter_mode_ctx,
  skip_ctx, switchable_interp_ctx; rd_thresh table; intra edges; DRIFT-FIX;
  TX-size RD; coef-source; mi-grid splat; the whole emit.
- GATE: `RVP9_PHASE2_REUSE_DISABLE=1` opts out (standing A/B knob). RDO path
  (cpu<=4) ignores the overrides (runs its own search) → unchanged there.

## Tradeoff space measured (3 variants)
| variant | foreman cpu7 speed | worst dMean | verdict |
|---------|--------------------|-------------|---------|
| optA  no guard          | ~1.10x | -0.164 dB | FAILS quality |
| optA2 nearest-only guard| ~1.15x | -0.133 dB | FAILS quality |
| optA3 nearest+near guard| ~1.09x | +0.000 dB | quality+conf PASS, speed FAIL |

The quality wobble in optA/optA2 is the R2 hazard: reusing a NEWMV searched under
the descent's non-causal {nearest,near} (seed + rate attractor) mis-ranks the
candidate when the causal context differs. Guarding on the full (nearest,near)
pair eliminates it (output byte-identical) but shrinks reuse to the context-match
subset, and the NEWMV-search skip is only a fraction of total picker cost, so the
speed win lands below 1.20x on foreman.

## VERDICT: FAILS-A-GATE (speed)
optA3 PASSES cross_decoder 4/4, decode_failed 0, quality 0.00 dB (byte-identical),
and all regression guards — but MISSES the >= 1.20x foreman speed floor
(~1.02-1.17x, geomean ~1.09x). The cross-decoder-safe seed-only variant cannot
reach 1.2x on foreman because faithful reuse only fires on the causal-context-match
subset and skips only the NEWMV search (not the full pick).

RECOMMENDATION: HOLD as a standalone speed PR (speed gate missed). It is, however,
a zero-risk, byte-identical, fully-revertible foundation worth keeping behind the
RVP9_PHASE2_REUSE_DISABLE gate. To clear 1.20x, layer (C2) breakout
(infrastructure in-tree) on top, OR relax the cross-decoder-safe boundary (the
unguarded optA reaches cif 1.20x but costs -0.16 dB — fails quality). The HLD's
~1.2-1.5x estimate was optimistic for the safe variant; the safe ceiling on
foreman is ~1.1x.
