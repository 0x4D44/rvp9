# Current PGO kernel exclusions after the diamond-bounds screen

This is instruction-level candidate selection, **not a new speed measurement**. The integrated production PGO image remains SHA-256 `3caee3f4416087ffe13069b4280f48fe92435bc32c9c0a41bebb7c187a73b3ee`, with [60,215 direct Ducks samples](../2026-09-26-pgo-nstep-ducks-exact-ip/README.md). The latest direct integrated-code comparison with pinned libvpx remains [1.099305× equal-clip CPU](../2026-09-26-nstep-duplicate-variance-pgo/README.md).

The source-line buckets do not isolate an emit operation. `rvp9-encoder/src/lib.rs:11471` spans 10,576 samples over 1,246 instruction addresses, and `:11588` spans 2,960 over 767. Disassembly of the exact image at the largest localized emit address, RVA `0xaa5d1` (1,582 samples), shows a coefficient load inside AVX2 quantization. The adjacent `0xaa5c0` is a scalar quantizer-parameter load. The next addresses `0xa6b4a` (368 samples) and `0xa71bf` (657) are respectively a quantization-loop decrement and a loop-bound comparison. These identify active work, not an exact-output shortcut. The [narrow coefficient-load PGO screen](../2026-09-26-avx2-narrow-coefficient-loads-rejected/README.md) already tested the most tempting change and regressed overall CPU by 3.4%; do not infer a new gain from that sampled load.

The other apparent NEWMV hot cluster also does not reveal diagnostic overhead. RVA `0x104e27` (364 samples) falls in the AVX2 bilinear interpolation loop, immediately followed by repeated row-offset multiplication and source-bounds branches; this is the mechanism of the [rejected sub-pixel footprint-hoist prototype](../2026-09-26-avx2-subpel-footprint-rejected/README.md). RVAs `0x1055b5` through `0x1055ed` are vector `(sum, sum-of-squares)` horizontal reduction and scalar variance arithmetic; `0x1055d7` has 146 samples. Their broad `inter.rs:3986` source attribution does not make them trace bookkeeping.

The 16×16 AVX2 SAD4D kernel is likewise not a fresh bounded candidate. Its current helper bucket has 3,013 samples, but that includes broad caller work. An [older instruction attribution](../2026-09-24-unit1416-profile/rvp9-cpu6-sad4d16x16_avx2-instruction-attribution.json) put the direct-wrapper-bypass ceiling near 0.27% of whole-encode samples in that image; it is not a current-PGO ceiling. The [two-row AVX2 rewrite](../2026-09-26-avx2-sad4d16-two-row-negative/README.md) was exact but slightly slower in two paired screens. Revisit only with a new operation-level mechanism and a cheap standalone falsifier.

No shipped-code edit follows from these addresses. A next candidate needs a specific redundant operation or instruction-chain reduction in an untested hotspot, not another rewrite of these broad line buckets. Any such candidate still needs an exact differential with a deliberate red case, both-architecture validation, and paired production PGO CPU before integration.
