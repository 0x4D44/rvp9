The accepted phase-specific sub-pel work moved that family below 7% of the
profile. The next sensible Tesco-sized screens are therefore picker/quantizer
or transform changes, but prior production-PGO screens already rejected the
obvious quantizer packing, fixed-shape variance, fused sub-pel, and fdct16
second-pass candidates.

A fresh direct timing screen compared this exact integrated binary with the
pinned libvpx `vpxenc` reference. It used the same CPU-used-6 CBR settings as
the local harness, ten alternating pairs per clip, and median-of-middle-two
timings. Output hashes remained deterministic for both encoders.
