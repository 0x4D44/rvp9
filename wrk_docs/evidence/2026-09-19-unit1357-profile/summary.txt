This profile makes the next decision explicit. Prediction search still owns
the largest required work, while the AVX2 sub-pel kernel is the largest exact
kernel surface. Picker policy shortcuts remain unsuitable because they change
strict decisions. The earlier mixed DC/AC quantizer screen is retained as a
negative result; its three-run PGO geomean was 0.996991x with one regression.
The earlier fused sub-pel screen was also rejected at 1.004094x paired corpus
on plain release. A future sub-pel change therefore needs a materially deeper
rewrite or new line-level evidence, not a repeat of either rejected patch.
