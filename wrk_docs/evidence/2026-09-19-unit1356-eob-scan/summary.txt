Unit 1356 — x86 AVX2 quantizer EOB scan
Date: 2026-09-19
Branch commit: 8d079130

Scope

The AVX2 fixed-point quantizer already writes qcoeff in raster order, then
walks the scan permutation backwards to find EOB.  This candidate passes the
complete ScanOrder inverse table into the production x86 dispatch.  For 8x8
and 16x16 blocks it computes the maximum iscan value with AVX2 loads; a
16-entry reverse scan probe preserves the cheap dense-block case.  4x4 and
the existing direct wrapper retain the scalar reverse scan.

Focused ruler

The synthetic `dsp-microbench quantize_fp` is not uniformly positive.  Final
candidate versus integrated control dispatch times were:

| block | candidate | control |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 4x4 | 17.6 ns | 15.5 ns |
| 8x8 | 19.1 ns | 17.9 ns |
| 16x16 | 37.5 ns | 38.7 ns |

This is why the end-to-end and production-PGO screens are required; the
candidate trades a small 8x8 reduction overhead for a larger-block win.

Decision

This is an integration-quality performance candidate.  The production-PGO
gain is repeatable across three screens and all output hashes are stable.  The
branch is committed but not integrated; keep it ready for an explicit landing
decision.  It does not by itself reach the parity goal: the current PGO corpus
ratio is about 0.955x versus the integrated control and 1.221x versus pinned
libvpx.
