Candidate: 8117b1cc (perf(x86): streamline subpel narrow tails)
Host: Crucible, Windows x86-64, Ryzen 9 9950X3D, 32 logical processors

Change

`sub_pixel_variance_avx2` already used checked SIMD loads for full 16-byte
chunks. The 4- and 8-pixel tails still copied through stack buffers. This
candidate uses exact-width checked loads for 4/8-byte source tails, direct
4/8-lane i16 loads and stores for the vertical pass, and direct 4/8-byte
stores for the packed u8 result. Other narrow widths and malformed callers
keep the checked copy fallback. Arithmetic, dispatch, and public signatures
are unchanged.
