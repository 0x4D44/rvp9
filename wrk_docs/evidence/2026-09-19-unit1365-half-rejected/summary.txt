RVP9 Unit 1365 rejected half-pel bilinear specialization

The experiment replaced the generic `[64, 64]` bilinear multiply-and-shift
with AVX2 `avg_epu16` for half-pel phases in the generic and identity-axis
helpers. It was evaluated against the committed Unit 1364 binary.

The short ducks-only screen looked promising (`0.985645x` paired,
`0.985767x` separate), but the full three-clip screen regressed:

| Clip | Separate | Paired | Candidate ms/frame | Unit 1364 ms/frame | Hashes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| ducks_take_off | 1.003004x | 1.013379x | 26.484 | 26.405 | identical |
| in_to_tree | 1.021697x | 1.010956x | 15.505 | 15.176 | identical |
| old_town_cross | 1.032482x | 1.006037x | 19.220 | 18.616 | identical |
| corpus | 1.018988x | 1.010119x | — | — | all identical |

The full protocol was one-thread CPU-used-6 CBR, 1280×720@30, 1260 kbps, 60
frames, one warmup, 15 paired samples, two rotations, and three clips. Crucible
was busy during this run (28.30 busy logical cores at preflight), but both
paired and separate corpus ratios regressed, so the specialization is not kept.

The experiment was removed; the task worktree is back at committed Unit 1364
`ecfb17ff`. Raw manifest, rows, and summary CSV remain beside this file.
