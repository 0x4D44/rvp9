RVP9 Unit 1364 x86 AVX2 zero-vertical-phase sub-pel shortcut

Candidate: Unit 1363 source (including the integer-centre and zero-horizontal
shortcuts) plus `horizontal_bilinear_to_u8_temp` in
`crates/rvp9-core/src/dsp/x86_64/subpel_variance_avx2.rs`.

When `yoffset == 0`, the vertical bilinear taps are `[128, 0]`, so the second
pass is the identity. The candidate computes the horizontal bilinear result
directly into the byte temp and skips the extra widened row and vertical pass.
