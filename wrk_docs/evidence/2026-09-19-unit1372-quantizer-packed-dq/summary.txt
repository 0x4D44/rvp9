RVP9 Unit 1372 — packed AVX2 dqcoeff multiply (rejected)

Base: integrated trunk commit b72f42db37957e7badba3341d5fdc83ac3b2097d.

Candidate: pack the eight qcoeff lanes once, then compute dqcoeff with
`_mm_mullo_epi16`. The low 16 bits of a 16-bit product match the scalar
`(qcoeff * dequant) as i16` cast, including intentional wrap. This removes
the vector qcoeff sign-extension and 32-bit dequant multiply from the AC
loop while retaining exact qcoeff packing.
