# x86 frame-HF Laplacian AVX2 candidate

The change at `b4ad2591` vectorizes the frame-HF Laplacian on x86-64 with runtime AVX2 dispatch. It retains the scalar fallback and the existing AArch64 NEON implementation. The candidate was built on Crucible from source SHA-256 `505489f04fea3c732180db294cf6c629bfa9dc5b8553f17f5742034a347ce221`; the executable SHA-256 is `1825fdcf69abe466115327d9530a96c4f1653c92e866b004643497723850dc4a`. The integrated baseline executable is `c371d472690baba94dc1374b9ce4b65dddd3f3ac2381feca362efa25e55f0d98`. Each run's `identity.csv` verifies the executable, source, and three 60-frame inputs.

With CPU6 CBR1260, one thread, and otherwise matched pinned settings, two independent 15-pair-per-clip screens gave candidate/current process-CPU geometric-mean ratios of **0.984277** and **0.979543**. All 90 pairs were retained; the combined equal-weight ratio is about **0.9819**. The candidate's IVF hashes matched current for all three clips and every run. The x86 scalar differential failed under a deliberate below-row mutation and passed after restoration. The AArch64 differential and focused checks also passed.

A fresh 15-pair-per-clip comparison with pinned libvpx v1.14.1 gave candidate/libvpx process-CPU ratio **1.182520** (ducks 1.316331, tree 1.202904, town 1.044310). The earlier integrated baseline/libvpx comparison was 1.213193, but these are separate sessions and their ratio is not a direct speedup measurement; the paired candidate/current screens above are the speedup evidence. The candidate remains short of the 1.05 parity target. Wall-time ratios and host-load samples are retained as diagnostics, not the decision metric; fluctuating load affected some runs.

Each subdirectory retains the exact runner, manifest, raw timings, summary, output-stability hashes, identity hashes, and load samples. Encoded IVF files were not checked in because their hashes and byte counts are recorded in `raw.csv` and `output-stability.csv`.
