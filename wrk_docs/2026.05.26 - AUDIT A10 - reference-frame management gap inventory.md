## Section-by-Section Narrative

### 1. The Ref-Pool Data Structure (rows 9–16)

libvpx's reference-frame management is built on a two-level indirection:

- A **buffer pool** (`BufferPool::frame_bufs[FRAME_BUFFERS]`, typically 12 slots) holds `RefCntBuffer` records — each owns the `YV12_BUFFER_CONFIG buf`, an `mvs` array, a `frame_index`, and a `ref_count` (`vp9_onyxc_int.h:328-358`, `vp9_alloccommon.c:74-90`). Slots are allocated by `get_free_fb` (`onyxc_int.h:332-347`), refcounted by `ref_cnt_fb` (`onyxc_int.h:349-358`), and released by `decrease_ref_count` (`vp9_decoder.h:169`).
- A **ref ring** of 8 logical slots (`cm->ref_frame_map[REF_FRAMES=8]`, see `vp9_encoder.c:2278`) maps `0..=7` to a pool-buffer index, so the bitstream's per-frame 3-bit ref-indices (LAST/GOLDEN/ALTREF in `ref_frame_indices`) resolve through this ring into the actual pixel buffer.
- The encoder tracks **three logical slot IDs** (`cpi->lst_fb_idx`, `gld_fb_idx`, `alt_fb_idx`) — these are *indices into `ref_frame_map`*, not into the pool. They get swapped (e.g. `vp9_preserve_existing_gf`, `vp9_encoder.c:3329-3332`) so the same pool slot can change its logical role between frames without copying pixels.

**rvp9 has none of this on the encoder side.** Grep `buffer_pool|BufferPool|frame_bufs|new_fb_idx|REF_FRAMES|RefCntBuffer` across `crates/rvp9-encoder/src/` returns zero matches. Instead, the encoder owns two tight-stride `Vec<u8>` triples (`last_recon_y/u/v`, `golden_recon_y/u/v`, `frame_loop.rs:293-316`) and a single `frames_since_golden_refresh: u32` counter (`frame_loop.rs:317-320`). LAST and GOLDEN refs are the only refs the picker sees. No ALTREF buffer exists in encoder state; no `new_fb_idx` exists because the encoder owns the only reconstruction directly; no refcounting exists because nothing else holds a buffer reference.

The downstream decoder DOES carry the full pool (`crates/rvp9-decoder/src/lib.rs:325-348, 728-732` — a 9-slot frame pool, 8-entry `ref_frame_map`, per-slot `ref_frame_sizes`). The encoder and decoder are therefore asymmetric: the decoder reads 3-bit `ref_frame_indices` against a populated 8-entry map and resolves them through a 9-slot pool, while the encoder writes a frozen `ref_frame_indices: [0, 0, 0]` and `refresh_frame_flags ∈ {0x01, 0x03}` on every inter frame (`frame_loop.rs:1888-1898`).

**Statuses:**

- **Buffer pool (#10):** NOT PORTED. No `frame_bufs[]` on the encoder side.
- **`get_free_fb` (#11):** NOT PORTED. The encoder never allocates a new pool slot because there is no pool.
- **`ref_cnt_fb` (#12):** NOT PORTED. No refcounting; ref-rotation is a `Vec::copy_from_slice` (`frame_loop.rs:791-794, 1164-1166`).
- **`decrease_ref_count` (#13):** NOT PORTED. No refcounts to decrement.
- **`new_fb_idx` (#14):** NOT PORTED. Implicit: the encoder mutates `last_recon_*` in place.
- **`ref_frame_map` (#15):** NOT PORTED. The encoder writes `ref_frame_indices: [0; MAX_INTER_REFS]` unconditionally (`frame_loop.rs:1897`). The decoder maintains its own 8-entry map and resolves LAST/GOLDEN/ALTREF through it, but the encoder doesn't model the ring at all.
- **`lst_fb_idx` / `gld_fb_idx` / `alt_fb_idx` (#16):** NOT PORTED. Implicit hard-coding: LAST is always pool slot 0, GOLDEN is always slot 1, refresh-mask 0x03 hits both, 0xFF on keyframes hits all. No swaps, no preserve-existing-gf trick, no multi-ARF support.
- **`init_ref_frame_bufs` (#9):** NOT PORTED. Startup of the encoder doesn't zero a ref ring because there is no ref ring.

### 2. The Refresh-Flag Decision Path (rows 1–8, 18–19)

libvpx decides which slots to refresh in two layers:

- **Producer** (`update_ref_frames`, `vp9_encoder.c:3292-3380`) does the *internal* `ref_cnt_fb` rebinds on the pool — this is what actually rotates the GOLDEN/ALTREF/LAST slots in `cm->ref_frame_map` after the frame is encoded. It handles KF (refresh all of LAST/GOLDEN/ALTREF, lines 3311-3315), the `vp9_preserve_existing_gf` swap (lines 3316-3332, when the current frame is an ARF overlay that should *replace* GOLDEN), multi-ARF stack push (lines 3334-3349), refresh_golden_frame (lines 3352-3363), refresh_last_frame (lines 3366-3373), and the `MID_OVERLAY_UPDATE` ARF-stack pop (lines 3375-3379). It also copies `interp_filter_selected[0] → interp_filter_selected[ref]` whenever a ref is refreshed (lines 3345-3372).
- **Wire writer** (`vp9_get_refresh_mask`, `vp9_bitstream.c:902-940`) synthesizes the 8-bit `refresh_frame_flags` mask that goes into the uncompressed header. It mirrors the same logic — `vp9_preserve_existing_gf` swaps which slot bit gets set (line 914-915), multi-layer ARF iterates to find an unused slot for the new ARF (lines 920-930), and the SVC bypass-mode override (lines 933-935) returns `update_buffer_slot[svc->spatial_layer_id]`. The default mask is `(refresh_last << lst_fb_idx) | (refresh_golden << gld_fb_idx) | (refresh_alt_ref << arf_idx)` (lines 936-938).

**rvp9 wire output:** `frame_loop.rs:1078-1086` decides `refresh_flags` as a simple period gate (`0x01` plain, `0x03` when `frames_since_golden_refresh >= A14_GOLDEN_REFRESH_PERIOD = 8`, `frame_loop.rs:382`). Keyframes emit `refresh_frame_flags = 0xFF` inside `build_keyframe_packet` (`frame_loop.rs:785`-820; the writer side of the constant lives in the keyframe builder body). The encoder's internal "rebind" is just `golden_recon_*.copy_from_slice(last_recon_*)` (`frame_loop.rs:791-794` on KF, `frame_loop.rs:1163-1168` on golden-refresh frames).

**Gaps vs libvpx (`update_ref_frames`):**

- No `vp9_preserve_existing_gf` swap — rvp9 cannot encode an "overlay ARF that replaces golden" frame because the encoder has no notion of ALTREF buffer to begin with (row 19, NOT PORTED).
- No multi-ARF stack push/pop (row 28, NOT PORTED).
- No `refresh_alt_ref_frame` branch at all (rows 1 and 6, PARTIAL on golden-only path) — every inter frame in rvp9 is plain inter or plain overlay-into-golden; ALTREF is absent on the producer side.
- No `interp_filter_selected[][]` per-ref histogram propagation (row 26, NOT PORTED) — the picker has no input from prior-ref filter histograms, so `setup_interp_filter_search_mask` (row 27, NOT PORTED) cannot prune the search.
- `vp9_update_reference_frames` (row 2, PARTIAL): exists in spirit (the post-encode reconstruction commit at `frame_loop.rs:1143-1168` plays its role) but does not call into a denoiser-ref-update path or an SVC-ref-update path. Cross-zone with A18.

**Statuses for `vp9_get_refresh_mask` (#18):** PARTIAL. The simple gold-frame-refresh mask `0x01`/`0x03` is synthesized at `frame_loop.rs:1082-1086`, which is structurally what the default branch of `vp9_get_refresh_mask` (lines 936-938) produces when `lst_fb_idx=0, gld_fb_idx=1, alt_fb_idx=2, refresh_alt_ref_frame=0`. But the function as a whole is partial: no preserve-existing-gf, no multi-layer ARF loop, no SVC override.

**`setup_frame` (#5):** PARTIAL.

- The KF "force refresh_golden_frame=1 + refresh_alt_ref_frame=1" rule (row 6, `vp9_encoder.c:851-854`) is matched in spirit by rvp9's `frame_loop.rs:791-794` (KF mirrors KF recon into the golden buffer) and `refresh_frame_flags = 0xFF` (`crates/rvp9-core/src/bitstream/header.rs:1075-1085` test vector confirms the wire format; the encoder emits `0xFF` via `build_keyframe_packet`). But the ALTREF half is moot in rvp9 because there is no altref buffer to refresh.
- `frame_context_idx` selection (row 7, NOT PORTED): libvpx sets `cm->frame_context_idx = cpi->refresh_alt_ref_frame` (line 819), routing ARF frames through FC bank 1 so ALTREF coding statistics adapt separately. rvp9 hard-codes `frame_context_idx: 0` in `frame_loop.rs:1859`. The decoder is symmetric (it also doesn't model 4 banks today), so this is an encoder gap that compounds with the missing FC adaptation (P5 in the picker inventory).
- Multi-layer-ARF FC selector (row 8, NOT PORTED): the 4-bank tree (`vp9_encoder.c:822-849`) is wholly absent.

### 3. Sign-Bias and `frame_refs` (rows 20–22)

`set_ref_sign_bias` (`vp9_encoder.c:5034-5049`) computes `cm->ref_frame_sign_bias[ref]` from the comparison of the *current* frame's `frame_index` against the *referenced* frame's `frame_index` (line 5045-5046): when the current frame's index is *less* than the ref's index, sign-bias is true (the ref is in the future). This drives compound-MV prediction and several other ref-orientation decisions.

`frame_index` is updated by `update_frame_indexes` (`vp9_onyxc_int.h:278-285`, called from `vp9_encoder.c:5900`) — it increments `current_video_frame` only when `show_frame`, but always increments `current_frame_coding_index`. The non-show ARF frame's coding index advances even though it doesn't show.

**rvp9 status:**

- **`set_ref_sign_bias` (#20):** PARTIAL. The encoder writes `ref_frame_sign_biases: [false; MAX_INTER_REFS]` unconditionally (`frame_loop.rs:1898`, `lib.rs:867`, `lib.rs:2542`). Since rvp9 has no ALTREF (which would have `frame_index > current_frame_index` and trip sign-bias true), and LAST and GOLDEN are always in the past, `[false; 3]` happens to be *correct* for the cases rvp9 currently emits. But the *function* is not ported — the encoder cannot encode any non-`[false, false, false]` ref-frame configuration. PARTIAL because the bytes happen to be right by construction of the producer scope, not because the logic is implemented.
- **`frame_refs[]` setup (#21):** PARTIAL. libvpx loops LAST..=ALTREF, builds `RefBuffer` records with `.idx`, `.buf`, `.sf` (scale_factors), and validates at least one is non-invalid (`vp9_encoder.c:3889-3920`). rvp9 has no `frame_refs[]` array on the encoder side — the picker receives `last_recon` and `golden_recon` directly as `PlaneRef` (`lib.rs:2412-2418`), and there are no scale_factors at all. Cross-decoder: the decoder reads the wire-format frame-size and computes its own scale_factors when needed (`crates/rvp9-decoder/...`), but the encoder cannot emit a scaled-ref frame because the writer hard-codes `ref_frame_indices: [0; 3]`.
- **`set_ref_ptrs` (#22):** PARTIAL. Conceptually mapped to `lib.rs:3024-3027` and `lib.rs:3622-3625` (passing `last_ref_*` and `golden_ref_*` plane slices directly into the picker). No `xd->block_refs[0/1]` indirection.

### 4. Buffer Pool / Scaled-Ref / `alloc_frame_mvs` (rows 23–25)

- **`alloc_frame_mvs` (#23):** NOT PORTED. libvpx attaches an `MV_REF mvs[mi_rows * mi_cols]` array to the pool slot that will hold the new frame's reconstruction (`vp9_encoder.h:1497-1508`, called from `set_frame_size` `vp9_encoder.c:3873`). On the next frame, the decoder's `find_mv_refs` consumes this through `cm->prev_frame->mvs`. rvp9's equivalent is the `prev_mv_grid: Option<Vec<PrevFrameMvRef>>` field on `StreamEncoder` (`frame_loop.rs:336-340`), populated by the picker (`frame_loop.rs:991-992, 1153-1155`). Functionally equivalent for the LAST-only case, but it's a single Vec, not a per-pool-slot allocation, so it cannot survive a multi-ref refresh where two different slots refresh at the same time.
- **`vp9_scale_references` (#24):** NOT PORTED. The full dynamic-resize ref-rescale path (`vp9_encoder.c:3454-3545`) is absent. This is fine for a fixed-resolution stream but blocks the dynamic-resize CBR path and SVC. Also overlaps with A2 row 22 (logged here for completeness).
- **`release_scaled_references` (#25):** NOT PORTED. Mirrors #24; no scaled-ref slots to release.

### 5. `ext_refresh_*` / External Override API (rows 3–4, 35–37)

libvpx exposes four external override APIs that map onto ref-frame state:

- `vp9_update_reference` (vp9_encoder.c:2969-2974) — sets the three `ext_refresh_*` flags and the `ext_refresh_frame_flags_pending` latch (row 3, NOT PORTED).
- `set_ext_overrides` (vp9_encoder.c:4960-4974) — drains the latch into `cpi->refresh_last/golden/alt` at the top of `encode_frame_to_data_rate` (row 4, NOT PORTED).
- `vp9_apply_encoding_flags` (vp9_encoder.c:7019-7060) — public surface for per-frame VP8E_SET_FRAME_FLAGS (row 35, NOT PORTED).
- `vp9_use_as_reference` (vp9_encoder.c:2962-2967) — masks out which refs the *next* frame is *allowed* to use (row 36, NOT PORTED).
- `vp9_set_reference_enc` / `vp9_copy_reference_enc` (vp9_encoder.c:2990-3010) — copy a buffer into a ref slot (used by some external RC pipelines) (row 37, NOT PORTED).

These are wholly absent from rvp9. None are quality-critical for fixed-pattern CBR encoding, but `vp9_apply_encoding_flags` is the standard hook for the vpxenc CLI's `--ref-frame-config` / external pattern files.

### 6. Post-Encode Publish (rows 17, 31–33, 38)

- **`get_ref_frame_flags` (#17):** NOT PORTED. Computes `cpi->ref_frame_flags = VP9_LAST_FLAG | VP9_GOLD_FLAG | VP9_ALT_FLAG`, then strips bits when refs collapse (`gold_is_last`, `alt_is_last`, `gold_is_alt`, `vp9_encoder.c:4292-4310`) or when GF update is way off (`frames_till_gf_update_due == INT_MAX`, line 4301). The output drives picker ref-mask pruning on the next frame. rvp9's picker iterates LAST and GOLDEN unconditionally without checking collapse (cross-zone with A1 picker, `ref_frame_skip_mask`).
- **`cpi->ref_frame_flags = get_ref_frame_flags(cpi)` post-encode publish (#33):** NOT PORTED. Without #17, there's nothing to publish.
- **`update_frame_indexes` (#31):** NOT PORTED. rvp9 tracks `frame_idx: u32` on `StreamEncoder` (`frame_loop.rs:289`) and increments it inside `encode_frame`. This is functionally equivalent to `current_video_frame` for show-frame-only streams, but the `current_frame_coding_index` (which advances even on non-show ARF) has no analog. `RefCntBuffer.frame_index` (which `set_ref_sign_bias` reads, #20) also has no analog.

### 7. ALTREF Construction (rows 8, 28–29)

ALTREF construction itself is the responsibility of A16 (temporal filter); A10 owns the *management* of the ALTREF slot:

- The `setup_frame` KF rule (`vp9_encoder.c:851-854`) forces `refresh_alt_ref_frame = 1` (row 6).

All of these depend on the buffer pool (#10-#13) and the `cpi->refresh_alt_ref_frame` flag (which `frame_loop.rs` never sets). They are NOT PORTED.

### 8. Recode Loop Backup (row 39)

`save_encode_params` (`vp9_encoder.c:3923-3942`) backs up `rd_opt->prediction_type_threshes` and `filter_threshes` plus per-tile `thresh_freq_fact` so the recode loop can restore them after a failed pass. Cross-zone with A2 / A4. NOT PORTED — rvp9 has no recode loop (the recode-loop A2 row 4 is PARTIAL).

### 9. Cross-Cutting (SVC)

Row 34 (`vp9_svc_update_ref_frame`) is flagged for A18. It is invoked from inside `vp9_update_reference_frames` (`vp9_encoder.c:3389`) which is in A10's hot path, so it must be enumerated here even though the implementation work belongs to A18. ## Cross-cutting gaps

### Encoder has no concept of a reference-buffer pool

The single largest gap in this zone is structural: rvp9's encoder owns *exactly two* references (LAST, GOLDEN) as `Vec<u8>` byte buffers, with no pool, no refcounting, no `ref_frame_map` ring, no `new_fb_idx`. Every libvpx feature that requires a third reference, a non-LAST refresh, or a scaled reference is therefore blocked at the producer side — including ALTREF (cross-zone with A16), compound prediction (cross-zone with A15), dynamic resize (cross-zone with A2 row 22), multi-ARF (cross-zone with A3 / A19), and SVC (cross-zone with A18). The wire format already supports it: `crates/rvp9-core/src/bitstream/header.rs:140-190` defines `MAX_INTER_REFS = 3`, `ref_frame_indices: [u8; 3]`, `refresh_frame_flags: u8`, `ref_frame_sign_biases: [bool; 3]`, and the decoder reads / resolves them through its own 9-slot pool (`crates/rvp9-decoder/src/lib.rs:325-348`). The asymmetry is encoder-only.

LOC estimate for closing the structural gap: ~300 LOC to add a `BufferPool` struct, `get_free_fb` / `ref_cnt_fb` / `decrease_ref_count`, an 8-entry `ref_frame_map`, the three logical slot ids, and rewire `last_recon_*` + `golden_recon_*` to live as `RefCntBuffer` entries.

### `interp_filter_selected[][]` is absent end-to-end

libvpx tracks per-ref interp-filter usage histograms across multiple frames (`vp9_encoder.c:3345-3372` writes them on refresh; `setup_interp_filter_search_mask`, `vp9_encoder.c:5051-5074`, reads them to prune the per-block filter loop). rvp9's `evaluate_candidate` filter loop (cross-zone with A1 row 15) iterates the filters unconditionally because nothing populates the histogram. Adding the histogram is ~50 LOC; making the picker consume it is another ~25 LOC. Medium impact (already lumped under "filter-prune" in the A1 inventory).

### `cpi->ref_frame_flags` collapse never published

Without `get_ref_frame_flags` (#17) and its post-encode publish (#33), the *next* frame's picker has no ref-mask input that says "LAST and GOLDEN are the same buffer right now, don't bother iterating GOLDEN". rvp9's picker iterates GOLDEN unconditionally on frames immediately following a golden-refresh (which is exactly when `gold_is_last` would have been true). This is a pure speed gap with a small quality knock-on (wasted RD comparisons can let noise pick a slightly worse mode); cross-zone with A1.

### `RefCntBuffer.frame_index` has no encoder analog

`set_ref_sign_bias` (#20) and the post-encode `update_encode_frame_result_basic` (`vp9_encoder.c:5779-5780`) both depend on a per-pool-slot `frame_index` that survives ref rotation. rvp9 has only a stream-level `frame_idx: u32` (`frame_loop.rs:289`), which is fine for show-frame ordering but cannot give "the index at which the buffer currently in GOLDEN was originally coded". Once ALTREF lands, this gap forces sign-bias to be computed differently (or to be statically false, as it accidentally is today). LOC: ~20 LOC to add `frame_index_at_capture: u32` to a future `RefCntBuffer`.

### Encoder writes `ref_frame_indices: [0; 3]` and asks the decoder to map

The encoder hard-codes `ref_frame_indices: [0; 3]` (`frame_loop.rs:1897`, `lib.rs:866, 2541`). The decoder reads these three indices, looks them up against its own 8-slot `ref_frame_map` (`crates/rvp9-decoder/src/lib.rs:2270, 4574, 4618`), and resolves them to pool buffers. Because the encoder also writes `refresh_frame_flags ∈ {0x01, 0x03, 0xFF}`, the decoder's `ref_frame_map[0]` always points at the most-recent inter recon and `ref_frame_map[1]` at the most-recent KF-or-golden-refresh recon — so the encoder's `[0, 0, 0]` accidentally resolves LAST/GOLDEN/ALTREF *all* to slot 0 (the LAST buffer), and the picker's GOLDEN choice goes wrong on the wire even though rvp9's picker thinks it picked GOLDEN. **AUDIT-UNCERTAIN:** I did not trace this end-to-end through the modes_writer to confirm whether `frame_loop.rs::build_inter_packet_with_residuals` and the per-block writer translate the picker's `picked_ref_frame: RefFrame::GoldenFrame` to a `ref_frame_indices[1]` slot at write time, or whether it always writes `single_ref_p1` as 0. If the latter, the cross-decoder gate is *byte-exact* but the GOLDEN reference is semantically wrong on the wire. Flagged for a follow-up read of `modes_writer.rs::write_inter_block_mode_info`.

## Conclusion

- **Counts:** 39 rows total — PORTED: 1; PARTIAL: 8; NOT PORTED: 30; OUT OF SCOPE: 0.
- **Row-count sanity:** libvpx-source LOC for in-scope helpers ≈ 350; scale factor vs picker baseline ≈ 0.12; expected band 6–17 rows; **actual 39**, ~2.3× the upper bound. Deviation is justified: this zone is unusual because it consists of many small but distinct helpers (`get_free_fb`, `ref_cnt_fb`, `decrease_ref_count`, `set_ref_sign_bias`, `vp9_update_reference`, `vp9_use_as_reference`, ARF stack ops, etc.) each of which merits a row, and the buffer-pool data structure itself decomposes into ~7 rows even though it is structurally one absent feature. Compressing further would lose call-site granularity needed by the Porter's Checklist.
- **Cross-zone dependencies flagged for master aggregation:**
  - A16 (ALTREF temporal filter) — rows 6, 8, 26, 28, 29 cannot land until A10 P0/P1 + P3 give the encoder a third reference slot.
  - A15 (Compound prediction) — row 22 (`set_ref_ptrs` block_refs[1]) is the entry point.
  - A18 (SVC paths) — row 34 (`vp9_svc_update_ref_frame`) is the bridge.
  - A2 (Frame-loop driver) — rows 22 (`vp9_scale_references`), 24 (`save_encode_params`), 71 (`show_existing_frame`) overlap with A2's inventory and should be deduplicated at aggregation.
  - A1 (pick_inter_mode picker) — rows 17 (`get_ref_frame_flags`), 26 (`interp_filter_selected[][]`), 27 (`setup_interp_filter_search_mask`) feed into A1's `ref_frame_skip_mask` and switchable-filter-rate gaps.
  - A3 / A19 (Rate control / lookahead) — row 28 (ARF stack) depends on gf_group state owned by A3.
- **Top-3 priority items:**
  1. **P0 — BufferPool + RefCntBuffer + ref_frame_map[8]** (~300 LOC, structural prerequisite for every downstream multi-ref feature).
  2. **P3 — full `update_ref_frames` body** (~90 LOC, the actual ref-rotation logic; blocks ALTREF + compound + multi-ARF on the producer side).
  3. **P6 — `set_ref_sign_bias` + `RefCntBuffer.frame_index`** (~30 LOC, required for any non-`[false,false,false]` sign-bias configuration, which is mandatory for ALTREF and compound prediction).
- **AUDIT-UNCERTAIN rows:** Row 20 (`set_ref_sign_bias` — wire bytes happen to be right only by producer-scope accident; needs a re-read once ALTREF lands). Cross-cutting note on the encoder's `ref_frame_indices: [0; 3]` write (needs a `modes_writer.rs` `single_ref_p1` trace).
