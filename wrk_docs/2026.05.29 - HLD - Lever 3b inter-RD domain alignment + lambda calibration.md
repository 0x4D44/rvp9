## 0. TL;DR

S2 (the inter-RD skip gate, `RVP9_S2_GATE=1`) is a **content-split**: **+~3 dB on
foreman** (BD-rate −43.5%) but **−0.9 .. −1.1 dB on testsrc** (strictly
dominated — more bytes *and* less PSNR). It is therefore landed default-OFF and
cannot ship as the headline win. The regression is **not** a bug: it is the
**RD-domain mismatch** S2 un-masks. The picker prices a **coefficient-domain**
rate (`coef_rate`, Hadamard SATD `<< 11`) against a **pixel-domain** distortion
(`dist` = SSE-of-recon). Once skip is honest, that mismatch mis-ranks modes on
synthetic high-frequency content (testsrc): `coef_rate` is over-stated, so the
picker flees to ZEROMV and over-spends mode/MV overhead.

**Lever 3b's job: make S2 a UNIVERSAL win** — keep foreman's gain AND remove
testsrc's regression — so S2 can ship default-ON. The two land as **one
shippable unit** (the calibration is meaningless without flipping the gate;
the gate regresses without the calibration).

**The honest complication:** the obvious fix — switch `dist → coef_dist` so rate
and distortion share a domain (libvpx's pairing) — **already exists** behind
`RVP9_S3_DOMAIN` and was **measured to REGRESS** (+21.6% BD-rate on top of S2;
foreman −0.71 dB @250k). A skip-shift sweep `{0,2,4,6,8}` could not recover it.
So this HLD is **not** "turn on S3." It is: *diagnose why the existing
domain-switch regresses, and find the rate/distortion/lambda triple — by
measurement — that makes S2 universal, or honestly conclude none exists.*

## 1. Problem

### 1.1 The measured content-split (the thing we are fixing)

From the campaign journal (foreman_320x256_60f and testsrc 1280×704, cpu7 CBR,
`RVP9_S2_GATE` toggled; gate-off is byte-identical baseline, so the deltas are
the S2 path, not a build artifact):

| Corpus | Point | HEAD (gate off) | S2 (gate on) | Δ |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| foreman | 100k | 26.14 dB | **29.25** | **+3.11** |
| foreman | 250k | 30.71 | **33.64** | **+2.93** |
| foreman | 1000k | — | — | +0.50 (still positive) |
| testsrc | 1000k | (baseline) | — | **−0.90** |
| testsrc | 2000k | (baseline) | — | **−1.11** |

S2 vs HEAD on foreman = **−43.5% BD-rate**. testsrc is **strictly dominated**
(bytes up, PSNR down). cross_decoder **4/4 byte-exact**, decode_failed 0
throughout — this is a quality-optimality issue in the picker's mode ranking,
not a decode-correctness defect.

### 1.2 Root cause (toggle-isolated, in the journal)

- `RVP9_S2_ACDC_RT` (AcDc-always-run) is a **pure no-op for output** — only
  picker compute. Not a factor. (It is defaulted off; do not resurrect.)
- `RVP9_S2_GATE` (the skip gate) is the **entire** S2 effect — the foreman win
  AND the testsrc loss. They are inseparable from the gate itself.
- **Mechanism = RD-domain mismatch × content statistics.** The honest skip gate
  forces the picker to weigh the coef-domain `coef_rate` against the pixel-SSE
  `dist` on the ~97% of leaves that code residual:
  - **foreman @250k:** ZEROMV 86%→46%, nonzero-MV 14%→54% → motion compensation
    activates → **+2.76 dB**.
  - **testsrc @1000k:** ZEROMV 76%→**80% (the WRONG way)**, nonzero-MV 24%→20% →
    `coef_rate` over-stated on synthetic high-frequency residual → picker flees
    to ZEROMV and over-spends mode/MV overhead → bytes up, PSNR down → **−0.88
    dB**.
- **No cheap universal fix exists** (already falsified, journal RCA):
  - A **q-threshold** gate is FALSIFIED — foreman *wins* at testsrc's exact
    bitrates, so the split is **content-dependent, not q-dependent**.
  - The gate cannot be split from its own benefit (it is one decision).
  - Only the **domain alignment** targets the mechanism — and the naive version
    (S3) regresses (§1.4). That is precisely what this HLD must calibrate.

### 1.3 The RD-domain mismatch, made precise (with unit analysis)

The picker's mode-ranking RD is `rd_cost(lambda, rate, dist)` =
`ROUND_POWER_OF_TWO(rate·lambda, 9) + (dist << 7)` (`mode_decision/mod.rs:74-89`,
`RDDIV_BITS=7`, `VP9_PROB_COST_SHIFT=9` — both verified faithful). The three
inputs and their **domains**:

| Term | Code | Domain | Scale construction |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rate` (residual part = `coef_rate`) | `inter.rs:2678`, built in `reconstruct_via_round_trip:3336` | **coefficient** (`vp9_prob_cost`, 1/512-bit) | `(satd_of_Hadamard_qcoeff) << (2 + 9)` + `eob_cost << 9`. The `<< 2` is libvpx's SATD-to-bits scale ("K1=2"). |
| `dist` (today) | `inter.rs:2053/2551`, UV-augmented `:2786` | **pixel** | raw `sum((src - recon)^2)`, i.e. pixel-SSE-of-recon. |
| `coef_dist` (the alt) | `block_yrd.rs:161`, `inter.rs:3283` | **coefficient** | `sum((had_coeff - had_dqcoeff)^2) >> 2`, on **Hadamard** coefficients. |
| `lambda` | `cost/rd_mult.rs`, `compute_rd_mult` | (tuned for the dist domain it was measured against — see below) | `q² · (4.15 + 0.001·q)` for inter. Byte-faithful to libvpx. |

**libvpx pairs `rate`(coef) with `dist`(coef):** its `block_yrd`
(`vp9_pickmode.c:835`) sets `this_rdc.dist = block_error_fp(coeff, dqcoeff) >> 2`
— coefficient-domain — and ranks `RDCOST(rate_coef, dist_coef)`. rvp9 instead
pairs `rate`(coef) with `dist`(**pixel**). So:

```
  rvp9 today:   RDCOST = lambda·rate_coef   +   (SSE_pixel        << 7)
  libvpx:       RDCOST = lambda·rate_coef   +   (block_error>>2   << 7)
```

The two `dist` terms are **not the same magnitude**, and the difference is
**content-dependent**, which is the crux:

1. **Pixel SSE vs coef-domain block-error are related by the transform's
   energy gain, NOT equal.** Parseval relates pixel-domain energy to
   *DCT*-domain energy up to the DCT's orthonormal normalization. But rvp9's
   `coef_dist` is computed on the **Hadamard** rate-proxy coefficients
   (`had_coeff`/`had_dqcoeff`, `inter.rs:3283`), not the DCT used for recon. The
   un-normalized Walsh-Hadamard transform (`hadamard.rs`) has a large energy
   gain (each of the two 1-D passes is a sum/difference butterfly with no
   `1/√N` scaling), so `block_error_fp(had_coeff, had_dqcoeff)` lives on a
   **much larger scale than pixel SSE**, even before the `>> 2`. Critically,
   the *ratio* between Hadamard-coef-error and pixel-recon-SSE is **not a
   constant** — it depends on how the residual's energy distributes across
   Hadamard basis functions (flat low-frequency residual vs. testsrc's
   high-frequency synthetic detail transform very differently). This is exactly
   why a single global rescale (the skip-shift sweep) could not recover S3.
2. **`coef_dist` is also a *different distortion* from what S2's skip branch
   measures.** The skip branch reports `sse_after_pred` (pixel SSE of the
   *prediction*, no residual). The encode branch's pixel `dist` is SSE of the
   *reconstruction*. Both are pixel-domain and directly comparable today. The
   moment the encode side moves to `coef_dist` (Hadamard-domain), the skip side
   must be rescaled into the same domain to keep the skip/encode comparison
   meaningful — and there is **no single shift** that does this faithfully
   across content (per point 1).

**This is the calibration problem:** to use a coef-domain `dist`, lambda (and/or
the rate scale, and/or the dist scale) must be re-matched so that
`lambda · rate_coef` and `dist_coef << 7` sit in the proportion libvpx's RD was
tuned for. rvp9's lambda is faithful to libvpx's formula — but libvpx's lambda
is applied against libvpx's `dist_coef`, whereas rvp9's lambda has only ever
been *exercised* against rvp9's pixel `dist`. We have no measurement that the
faithful lambda is correct against rvp9's *Hadamard* `coef_dist` — and S3's
regression is direct evidence that it is **not**.

### 1.4 Why the existing domain-switch (S3) REGRESSES — the result this HLD inherits

S3 already exists behind `RVP9_S3_DOMAIN` (`inter.rs:181-212, 2855-2894`):
encode-side `dist → coef_dist`, skip-side `sse << s3_skip_shift` (default 4,
`RVP9_S3_SKIPSHIFT` to sweep). The campaign **measured it ON TOP OF S2** and
found:

- **S2+S3 = +21.6% BD-rate REGRESSION** vs S2 alone. foreman @250k 33.64 →
  32.94 (**−0.71**). A skip-shift sweep `{0,2,4,6,8}` recovered nothing.
- The in-code doc (`inter.rs:181-192`) records the honest post-mortem:
  *"feeding the Hadamard-domain `coef_dist` against rvp9's pixel-SSE-tuned
  lambda (kept UNCHANGED) shifts the RD balance the wrong way."*

So the prior HLD's central prediction was **refuted**: it predicted S2-alone
regresses and S3 is the magnitude lever. The opposite held — **S2 alone is the
win; S3-as-built is the regression.** The reason (per §1.3): S3 changed the dist
*domain* but left lambda matched to the *old* domain. Swapping the dist domain
without re-matching lambda is exactly the move that breaks the RD balance.
**Lever 3b is the missing half of S3: the lambda/scale re-match that S3 needs to
stop regressing — established by measurement.**

## 2. Prior art and warnings (read before touching anything)

### 2.1 What libvpx does

libvpx's RT picker (`vp9_pickmode.c`) computes both `rate` and `dist` in the
**coefficient domain** inside `block_yrd` (rate = SATD `<< (2 + 9)`, dist =
`block_error_fp >> 2`), and ranks `RDCOST(rdmult, rate, dist)` with `rdmult` from
`vp9_compute_rd_mult_based_on_qindex`. The skip tie-break compares
`RDCOST(rate, dist_coef)` against `RDCOST(0, sse << 4)`, where `sse` is the
pixel-domain `model_rd_for_sb_y` SSE rescaled `<< 4` into the coef-dist domain.
**libvpx's `<< 4` is the empirical bridge between its pixel SSE and its
*DCT*-domain `block_error`** — it is not a first-principles constant, and there
is no guarantee it transfers to rvp9's *Hadamard* `coef_dist` (it didn't — §1.4).

### 2.2 ⚠️ STALE / MISLEADING — F4 and the K1=4 note

`wrk_docs/Track_F/F4_HLD_convergence_and_calibration.md` and
`notes/F4-calibration-sweep.md` recommend **K1=4** as the "winning" SATD-to-bits
scale. **This is stale and must NOT be acted on:**

- K1=4 was landed (`5fe3d76`) then **deliberately reverted to K1=2** (`88b97c9`)
  once the Hadamard port made K1=2 (the libvpx default) correct. The live code
  is K1=2 (`block_yrd.rs:176`, `inter.rs:3336`: `<< (2 + VP9_PROB_COST_SHIFT)`),
  and the in-code note at `inter.rs:2664-2672` already retires the K1=4 claim.
- The F4 sweep itself admits K1 carries only **~0.07 dB** — a **dead end**.
- The F4 sweep's Phase B (the only relevant part — K2 = `coef_dist`) was
  **blocked** by the `u32` saturation of `coef_dist` and deferred. That
  prerequisite (`u64` `rd_cost` widening) is **now done** (landed with S2,
  `fbfe97e`), so Phase B is finally runnable — but as a **lambda-matched**
  calibration (this HLD), not a K1 sweep.

**Action (docs-only, part of this lever's landing):** do not re-run a K1 sweep;
do not re-apply K1=4. If anything in F4 is cited, cite it only as the
*negative* result it is.

### 2.3 ⚠️ Lambda is verified byte-faithful — changing it is a deliberate departure

`notes/stage4a-lambda-units-investigation.md` and the `rd_mult.rs` unit tests
(pinned q0/q128/q255 to hand-derived libvpx values) establish that
`compute_rd_mult` is a **line-for-line port** of libvpx
`vp9_compute_rd_mult_based_on_qindex`. The "256× over-weight" hypothesis was
**REFUTED**. Therefore:

- Any lambda change is **not** a bug-fix; it is a **deliberate departure from
  libvpx fidelity**, and must be justified *only* by the domain pairing it
  enables. If we move `dist` to the coef domain, lambda was never validated
  against that domain (§1.3), so a lambda re-match is a *legitimate* departure
  **iff** it is the lambda libvpx would have used for *that* dist — i.e. the
  departure restores fidelity at the RD-balance level even as it changes the
  literal constant. This must be argued explicitly, not hand-waved.
- The lowest-risk outcome keeps lambda **untouched** and instead rescales the
  *dist* (or *rate*) into the domain lambda already expects. That option is
  evaluated head-to-head with the lambda-change option in §3.

## 3. Design — the calibration, by measurement

The fix must make `lambda · rate_coef` and `dist << 7` sit in the proportion
that ranks modes correctly on **both** corpora. There are three families of
where to put the correction. **All are measured against the same pre-registered
gates (§4); the design picks none a priori** — that is the whole point, given S3
already refuted the "obvious" choice.

### 3.1 Option A — dist → coef_dist, with a re-matched lambda (the libvpx-faithful pairing)

Switch encode-side `dist → coef_dist` (S3's move) **and** introduce a single
calibration scalar that re-matches the RD balance. Two equivalent knobs (pick
one to sweep; they are algebraically interchangeable at fixed inputs):

- **A1 — lambda scale `s_λ`:** rank with `rd_cost(round(lambda · s_λ), rate,
  coef_dist)`. Departs from faithful lambda (§2.3) — must be justified.
- **A2 — dist scale `2^k` (no lambda change):** rank with `rd_cost(lambda, rate,
  coef_dist >> k)` (or `<< k`). Keeps lambda byte-faithful; folds the entire
  domain bridge into a dist rescale. **Lower risk** (§2.3) and the **preferred
  starting point**.

Because `RDCOST = lambda·rate + dist<<7` is linear in `dist` and in `lambda`,
A1 and A2 produce *identical rankings* for a global constant (`s_λ` on the rate
term ≡ `1/s_λ` on the dist term up to rounding). The choice is therefore about
**fidelity narrative and rounding**, not expressive power — A2 is preferred
because it leaves the verified lambda alone.

The skip side moves to the same domain: `rd_skip = rd_cost(lambda, 0,
(sse_after_pred >> k_skip) ...)` matched to the encode side's scale. The
existing `RVP9_S3_SKIPSHIFT` knob already parameterizes this.

**Honest risk for A:** §1.3 point 1 — the Hadamard-coef-error / pixel-SSE ratio
is **content-dependent**, so a *single* global `k`/`s_λ` may improve foreman and
worsen testsrc (or vice-versa) — i.e. it may *not be able to make S2 universal*,
which is exactly what the S3 skip-shift sweep already suggested. Option A is the
faithful pairing, but the measurement may show no global constant works. That is
a real possible NULL result (§3.5).

### 3.2 Option B — keep pixel-SSE dist, rescale coef_rate into the pixel domain (no dist domain change)

Leave `dist` as pixel-SSE-of-recon (untouched, comparable, faithful to the
current RD that S2's foreman win was measured under) and instead **attenuate
`coef_rate`** so the coef-domain rate sits correctly against the pixel-domain
dist:

- **B1 — rate scale `2^-m`:** `effective_coef_rate = (coef_rate >> m)` before it
  folds into `rate`. This directly addresses the journal's RCA — "*`coef_rate`
  over-stated on synthetic high-freq residual*." The over-statement is the
  disease; B shrinks the dose.

Option B is attractive because:
- It keeps the dist domain that S2's **measured** foreman +3 dB was achieved in
  (we are *attenuating an over-statement*, not re-deriving a new RD).
- It keeps lambda byte-faithful (§2.3 satisfied).
- It targets the *named* mechanism (coef_rate over-statement) rather than
  swapping the whole dist domain.

**Honest risk for B:** `m` is a global constant against a content-dependent
over-statement (same content-dependence as A). And there is a fidelity story
to tell: attenuating `coef_rate` is *also* a departure from libvpx's `<< 2`
scale (it is K1 by another name). The difference from §2.2's dead-end K1 sweep
is that K1 was swept **against the pixel dist with the skip tie-break masking
coef_rate** (so it barely mattered, ~0.07 dB); here `coef_rate` is **un-masked**
by S2 and reaches mode ranking on 97% of leaves, so `m` has real leverage. This
distinction must be stated, because a reviewer will (correctly) ask "isn't this
just the K1 sweep you said was dead?" — it is the same knob, but in a
*different regime* (S2-on), and the journal RCA explicitly names coef_rate
over-statement as the testsrc mechanism.

### 3.3 Option C — content-adaptive (only if A and B both fail to be universal)

If no global constant in A or B makes S2 universal (the q-threshold was already
falsified; the split is content-dependent), the only remaining honest lever is a
**content signal** that the over-statement correlates with. Candidate signals
(measure correlation, do not assume): residual high-frequency energy (the
testsrc tell), Hadamard-coef-error / pixel-SSE ratio per block, or
prediction-SSE magnitude. **C is explicitly out of v1 scope** — it is a
fallback to *propose to Arthur* if A and B's measurements both show
irreconcilable content-split, NOT something to build speculatively (premature
abstraction; §quality-philosophy). C is named here only so the NULL path (§3.5)
has somewhere to escalate to.

### 3.4 Recommended search (pre-registered)

Run **B1 first** (lowest-risk: dist domain unchanged, lambda untouched, targets
the named mechanism), then **A2** (faithful pairing, lambda untouched), then
**A1** (lambda departure) only if A2's rounding leaves signal on the table.
Reason for B-before-A: B preserves the exact RD regime S2's foreman +3 dB was
measured in, so a B setpoint that fixes testsrc is the *smallest* departure from
a known-good state.

| Phase | Knob | Range (pre-registered) | Lambda | Dist domain | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | B1: `coef_rate >> m` | m ∈ {0 (=S2 today), 1, 2, 3, 4} | faithful | pixel (unchanged) | LOW |
| **P2** | A2: `coef_dist >> k` + skip `>> k_skip` | k ∈ {0..6}, k_skip from `RVP9_S3_SKIPSHIFT` sweep | faithful | coef (S3 path) | MED |
| **P3** | A1: `lambda · s_λ` | s_λ ∈ {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0} | **departs** | coef | HIGH |

Each setpoint is one foreman + one testsrc matched-bitrate run + cross_decoder +
the qp=70 cross-tab. **Stopping rule:** stop at the first setpoint that passes
**both** mandatory gates (§4.3); if a phase exhausts its range with no
dual-pass setpoint, advance to the next phase. If all three phases exhaust →
NULL result (§3.5).

These knobs need **one** new env-gated scalar each (`RVP9_3B_RATE_SHIFT`,
reuse `RVP9_S3_DOMAIN`+`RVP9_S3_SKIPSHIFT` for A2, `RVP9_3B_LAMBDA_NUM/DEN` for
A1) — sweep scaffolding only, ~15 LOC, no default behavior change until a
setpoint is chosen and burned in.

### 3.5 Pre-registered success and NULL definitions

**SUCCESS (both mandatory, the headline):**
1. **foreman:** retains **most** of S2's +3 dB — concretely **≥ +2.0 dB mean
   over {100k, 250k}** (S2 alone is +3.0; we accept up to ~1 dB of give-back to
   buy testsrc, but not more — a setpoint that craters foreman to buy testsrc is
   not a win).
2. **testsrc:** **≥ baseline (HEAD, gate-off) at every point** {1000k, 2000k} —
   i.e. the −0.9/−1.1 dB regression is **gone** (Δ ≥ 0, within ±0.1 dB noise).
   This is the **regression guard** and is non-negotiable: matching vpxenc means
   no testsrc regression.

If a setpoint passes both, **the lever lands WITH S2 flipped default-ON** as one
unit (§6).

**NULL (pre-registered — what if no pairing makes S2 universal):**
If P1–P3 all exhaust with no setpoint passing both gates, the honest conclusion
is **the foreman win and the testsrc non-regression are not simultaneously
reachable by a global RD-domain/lambda constant** (the content-split is
irreducible at this granularity). In that case:
- **Do NOT ship S2 default-ON** (it would regress testsrc).
- **Do NOT ship a lambda departure** that only helps one corpus (it abandons
  fidelity for a corpus-specific gain — strictly worse than the faithful
  default).
- **Escalate to Arthur** with the full sweep table and the explicit choice:
  (i) keep S2 gated default-OFF as preserved infrastructure (status quo, zero
  regression), or (ii) authorize Option C (content-adaptive correction) as a
  *new* scoped lever. The NULL result is itself a deliverable: it proves the
  split is content-structural, narrowing the remaining search.

## 4. Test / validation plan

### 4.1 The regression guard is the load-bearing oracle

The whole reason this lever exists is the testsrc regression. **Every** sweep
setpoint is measured on **both** corpora; a foreman-only improvement is treated
as a failure, not a partial win.

| Test | Tool | Corpus / points | Pass criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Foreman matched-bitrate Y-PSNR** | `bdrate` harness (`crates/rvp9-bench/src/bin/bdrate.rs`) + per-frame PSNR | foreman_320x256_60f @ **100k / 250k / 1000k / 2000k** | mean Δ over {100k,250k} **≥ +2.0 dB** vs HEAD; no point below HEAD |
| **Testsrc matched-bitrate Y-PSNR (REGRESSION GUARD)** | same harness | testsrc 1280×704 @ **100k / 250k / 1000k / 2000k** | Δ **≥ 0 at every point** (≥ −0.1 dB noise band); the −0.9/−1.1 must be gone |
| **qp=70 picker/emit cross-tab** | `RVP9_S2XTAB=1` (`lib.rs:5301`, `S2XTAB` lines) + offline parse | foreman + testsrc | picker_skip/emit_code divergence stays collapsed (~1%); mode-mix moves the *right* way on testsrc (ZEROMV% does **not** rise) |
| **Cross-decoder gate** | `crates/rvp9-encoder/tests/cross_decoder_gate.rs` | all scenarios | **4/4 byte-exact** (rvp9-decoder == vpxdec, ffmpeg fallback) |
| **decode_failed** | harness counter | all streams | **0** |
| **Mode-mix / ZEROMV%** | `RVP9_DIAG_PICKER` per-frame summary | foreman + testsrc | foreman ZEROMV% stays down (motion comp active); testsrc ZEROMV% returns toward baseline (the over-statement is corrected) |
| **rd_cost unit tests** | `cargo test -p rvp9-core --lib` | `mode_decision::mod::tests` | green (incl. the saturation-clamp test for the `u64` dist path) |
| **S2 self-consistency** | `inter.rs::s2_skip_gate_never_skips_nonzero_dct_eob` | — | green (the Y-slice invariant survives) |
| **720p30 realtime** | `realtime_gate` | 720p30 | median ≤ 33 ms (S2's gate already measured *faster* than HEAD — 162 vs 184 ms — so headroom exists; confirm no regression) |
| **Byte-exact emitter tests** | `crates/rvp9-encoder/tests/` synthetic fixtures | KF/skip/single-block | unchanged — 3b touches the **picker**, not the emitter |

### 4.2 Cross-tab as the mechanism check (not just PSNR)

PSNR says *whether* it worked; the cross-tab + mode-mix say *why*. The testsrc
RCA tell is "ZEROMV 76%→80% (wrong way)." A passing 3b setpoint must show
testsrc ZEROMV% **not** rising (ideally falling toward baseline) — confirming we
corrected the over-statement rather than masking it. If PSNR passes but the
mode-mix moved the wrong way, treat it as suspect (possible bitrate-matching
artifact) and re-measure.

### 4.3 Exact pass criteria (the gate, restated)

A setpoint **passes** iff ALL hold:
- foreman mean Δ{100k,250k} ≥ **+2.0 dB** vs HEAD, AND no foreman point < HEAD;
- testsrc Δ ≥ **0** (±0.1 dB) at **every** of {100k,250k,1000k,2000k};
- cross_decoder **4/4**, decode_failed **0**;
- 720p30 median ≤ 33 ms;
- all unit/self-consistency/byte-exact-emitter tests green.

Anything less is not a ship. A foreman win with *any* testsrc regression is the
NULL path (§3.5), not a partial ship.

## 7. Non-goals

- **No chroma transform / tx-size port into the picker** — S1 already measured

  passer AND it survives a third-corpus check (R2) — and even then it is framed

## 8. Executive summary (for the lead)

1. **S2 is a content-split, not a win yet:** +~3 dB foreman / −0.9..−1.1 dB
   testsrc (strictly dominated), so it shipped gated default-OFF (`fbfe97e`).
   Lever 3b's mandate is to make it **universal** (foreman gain kept AND testsrc
   regression gone) so S2 ships default-ON — the two land as **one unit**.

2. **The cause is a precise, content-dependent RD-domain mismatch:** the picker
   prices coef-domain `coef_rate` (Hadamard SATD `<< 11`) against pixel-SSE
   `dist`; libvpx pairs coef-rate with coef-dist. The mismatch over-states
   `coef_rate` on testsrc's synthetic high-freq residual → picker flees to
   ZEROMV (76%→80%, the wrong way) and over-spends. The Hadamard-coef-error /
   pixel-SSE ratio is **not constant across content**, which is why a single
   global rescale is hard.

3. **The "obvious" fix already exists and already REGRESSES.** `RVP9_S3_DOMAIN`
   (dist→coef_dist, skip `<<4`) was measured at **+21.6% BD-rate regression** on
   top of S2; a skip-shift sweep couldn't recover it — because it swapped the
   dist domain without re-matching the lambda that was tuned for the old domain.
   Lever 3b is the **missing lambda/scale re-match**, set **by measurement**.

4. **Three measured options, pre-registered, lowest-risk first:** (B1) attenuate
   the over-stated `coef_rate` in the S2-on regime, lambda + dist-domain
   untouched; (A2) dist→coef_dist with a dist rescale, lambda untouched; (A1)
   lambda departure, last resort. Stop at the first setpoint that passes **both**
   foreman (≥+2.0 dB) and the **testsrc regression guard (Δ≥0 everywhere)**.

5. **Honest about lambda and the NULL:** lambda is verified byte-faithful — any
   lambda change is a deliberate departure that must be justified by the domain
   pairing and survive a third corpus. The pre-registered NULL: if no global
   constant makes S2 universal, **do not ship** a corpus-specific retune —
   escalate to Arthur to choose between keeping S2 gated-off or authorizing a
   content-adaptive Option C.

**Single biggest open question for the lead:**
**Re-derive lambda for the coef domain (A1) vs. re-scale `coef_rate`/`coef_dist`
to keep lambda faithful (B1/A2) — which is the more faithful, lower-risk fix?**
My read: **B1/A2 (lambda untouched) is both lower-risk and more honest** —
lambda is verified faithful (§2.3), and a global rate/dist rescale is
algebraically equivalent to a lambda scale for ranking, so we get the same
expressive power without abandoning fidelity. A lambda change should be the
*last* lever, not the first, and only if it uniquely passes and survives a third
corpus. But this is a fidelity-vs-pragmatism call I want your ruling on before
any code, because it determines whether 3b is a "faithful calibration" or a
"deliberate tuning departure" — and the S3 regression proves our intuition about
this domain is unreliable, so I am not confident enough to decide it unilaterally.
