# Stage 4b cyclic-refresh AQ HLD

Status: DRAFT V1

## Quality brief

- Simplicity and cleanliness matter. Do not overengineer; solve the problem at hand. If you can't hold the design in your head, it's wrong.
- Development sandbox: no migration or rollback support, no backwards compatibility with old code, no ops choreography.
- Minimize environment variables; avoid feature flags.
- Design for testability: the HLD must specify how correctness will be verified (acceptance criteria / test oracles).

## Executive summary

Stage 4b should complete the existing cyclic-refresh AQ mode 3 implementation, not invent a broader AQ framework.

The live code already has the public switch (`AqMode::Off | CyclicRefresh`), optional CR state, spatial segmentation, segment-1 ALT_Q, per-leaf effective q, keyframe reset, and post-encode age/q feedback. The remaining gaps are the parts that make mode 3 faithful and measurable: dynamic BOOST1/BOOST2 deltas, production use of the per-block cyclic-refresh update, narrow cyclic-refresh-aware RC estimates, and hard AQ-on gates.

The design spine is risk-first: the final segment ID must be settled before final quantization, coefficients, and reconstruction are committed. If AQ changes a leaf's segment after seeing the winning rate/distortion/MV/skip result, that leaf is re-simulated once at the final effective q. A cheap post-hoc segment flip is not acceptable.

Stage 4b can be designed and partially implemented in parallel with Stage 4a, but product acceptance is blocked until the Stage 4a AQ-off quality, cross-decoder, and realtime gates are green.

## Scope

In scope:

- VP9 cyclic-refresh AQ mode 3 only.
- `AqMode::Off` remains default and preserves Stage 4a behavior.
- `AqMode::CyclicRefresh` for the product operating point: realtime VP9, speed / `cpu-used=7`, `lag=0`, single layer, 8-bit Profile 0, no altref, no denoiser, TPL off, and VBR per the current quality gate.
- Dynamic segment ALT_Q deltas for BOOST1 and BOOST2.
- Production per-block cyclic-refresh segment update before final quant/recon commit.
- Narrow RC integration required by the LLD: cyclic-refresh weighted `bits_per_mb` inside q regulation and cyclic-refresh weighted `estimate_bits_at_q` inside rate-correction-factor update.
- Hard acceptance gates for AQ quality, AQ-on external decode, and AQ-on realtime.

Out of scope:

- AQ modes 1, 2, or 4.
- SVC, resize, ROI/active-map, screen-content special cases, denoiser interactions, altref interaction, TPL interaction, two-pass AQ tuning, Profile 1, or high bit depth.
- New environment variables, feature flags, or product knobs.
- A new RC mode, broad RC rewrite, GOP rewrite, or golden-frame cadence rewrite.
- Byte-identical libvpx output as a release gate. BD-rate gain parity and decoder-valid streams are the gates.

## Current state

The current implementation is partially live:

- `frame_loop.rs` exposes `AqMode::Off | CyclicRefresh`; default config and CLI default keep AQ off.
- `StreamEncoder` allocates `CyclicRefresh` only for `AqMode::CyclicRefresh`.
- Inter frames can build a segmentation map and emit segment ALT_Q data.
- `lib.rs` already computes effective per-leaf q from segment deltas.
- Keyframes reset CR state and do not emit AQ segmentation.
- Post-encode feedback updates CR age and `last_coded_q_map` from actual coded segment and skip data.

The important missing pieces are:

- BOOST2 is not live; current production deltas only use segment 1.
- The dynamic q-delta ramp exists but is not wired as the production policy.
- The production path does not use the full per-block rate/distortion/MV/skip cyclic-refresh update before committing leaf coefficients and recon.
- RC remains cyclic-refresh blind. The rate-control LLD explicitly assigns Stage 4b to consume `vp9_cyclic_refresh_rc_bits_per_mb` in `regulate_q` and `vp9_cyclic_refresh_estimate_bits_at_q` in `update_rate_correction_factors`.
- The existing Stage 4b quality harness prints the 0.3% AQ-gain result but does not assert it.
- AQ-on cross-decoder and AQ-on realtime gates are missing.

## Design

### Ownership boundary

Rate control owns base q, RC mode, target bitrate, buffer model, and correction-factor state. AQ owns only cyclic-refresh state, segment ALT_Q deltas, segment selection, and the weighted estimates that RC asks for when AQ mode 3 is active.

`AqFramePlan` is a frame-local snapshot. It must not become a second owner of RC decisions or a future generic AQ framework.

### Frame ordering

Use a two-step cyclic-refresh setup so q-independent and q-dependent work stay in the right places:

1. Before base-q selection, update q-independent CR frame parameters from previous-frame state: active/gated status, refresh percentage, rate ratio, segment weighting, and any gating reason needed for tests.
2. RC picks base q. If AQ is active, RC uses the CR weighted `bits_per_mb` estimate in its existing q-regulation path. AQ does not change RC mode, target bitrate, or buffer accounting.
3. After base q is known, finalize the AQ frame plan: compute BOOST1/BOOST2 q deltas, thresholds, and the initial cyclic-refresh map.
4. Inter mode/partition selection sees the initial segment ID for each leaf.
5. After the winning leaf stats are known, but before final residual quantization and recon commit, run the cyclic-refresh segment update using the final rate, distortion, MV/ref, skip, and block size.
6. If the segment changes, re-simulate that leaf once at the final effective q. No stale coefficients, recon pixels, skip state, or RD stats may survive a segment change.
7. Emit spatial segmentation with ALT_Q deltas for segment 1 and segment 2 when AQ is active. Keyframes and gated-off inter frames emit segmentation disabled.
8. Post-encode feedback updates age, `last_coded_q_map`, and actual BOOST1/BOOST2 counts from the final coded segment IDs and skip flags.
9. Rate-correction-factor update uses the CR weighted `estimate_bits_at_q` when AQ was active. Actual emitted bits remain the feedback truth.

### Segment model

Segment 0 is base q. Segment 1 is BOOST1. Segment 2 is BOOST2.

The dynamic q-delta ramp must satisfy:

- segment 0 delta is zero / no ALT_Q;
- BOOST1 and BOOST2 deltas are non-positive;
- under product defaults, `BOOST2 <= BOOST1 <= 0`;
- deltas clamp to the legal qindex range.

### TPL and other excluded features

The Stage 4b product profile has TPL off. The HLD does not design TPL plus cyclic-refresh AQ. Tests and product gate constructors must assert `enable_tpl=false` for Stage 4b. If public config currently allows `enable_tpl && aq_mode == CyclicRefresh`, implementation may reject that combination, but must not add a feature flag or alternate TPL/AQ path.

`limit_q`, screen-content flat-static behavior, SVC layer context swaps, altref, denoiser, ROI, and resize are excluded from Stage 4b product acceptance. If the signed-off product gates fail after the scoped work is implemented, treat those as explicit follow-up design decisions rather than hidden knobs.

## Acceptance criteria

These gates are the build run's definition of done.

### Preconditions

Stage 4a AQ-off product gates must be green and non-skipped:

```powershell
$null | mdtimeout 1800 cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --release --test quality_gate quality_gate_stage4a_realcontent -- --ignored --nocapture
$null | mdtimeout 600 cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --release --test cross_decoder_gate cross_decoder_gate_vpxdec_ffmpeg -- --ignored --nocapture
$null | mdtimeout 600 cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --release --test realtime_gate realtime_gate_720p30 -- --ignored --nocapture
```

Missing corpus/reference binaries are allowed to skip ordinary developer runs, but they block Stage 4b sign-off evidence.

### Focused AQ correctness

```powershell
$null | mdtimeout 180 cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --lib aq_cyclic_refresh -- --nocapture
```

Required oracles:

- AQ-off keeps segmentation disabled and effective q equal to base q.
- Keyframes keep segmentation disabled.
- Dynamic q deltas are monotone under product defaults: `BOOST2 <= BOOST1 <= 0`.
- AQ-on positive controls observe non-zero refreshed cells and at least one BOOST1/BOOST2 coded segment.
- High-motion/high-distortion blocks demote to base.
- A low-rate, zero-MV, eligible block promotes to BOOST2.
- If segment changes after the winning leaf stats are known, committed coefficients and recon use the final effective q.
- Post-encode feedback updates age, `last_coded_q_map`, and actual BOOST1/BOOST2 counts from final coded data.

### AQ quality gate

```powershell
$null | mdtimeout 1800 cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --release --test quality_gate bdrate_gate_stage4b_aq_realcontent -- --ignored --nocapture
```

Required oracles:

- Foreman real-content input is present.
- libvpx `vpxenc` and `vpxdec` are present.
- All four curves are finite: rvp9 AQ off, rvp9 AQ on, libvpx AQ off, libvpx AQ on.
- Curves have valid PSNR overlap and no gate-breaking monotonicity failure.
- `abs(gain_rvp9 - gain_libvpx) <= 0.3%`.

The existing 2.0% line may remain informational. It is not an acceptance threshold.

### AQ-on interoperability and realtime

```powershell
$null | mdtimeout 600 cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --release --test cross_decoder_gate cross_decoder_gate_aq_cyclic_refresh_vpxdec_ffmpeg -- --ignored --nocapture
$null | mdtimeout 600 cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --release --test realtime_gate realtime_gate_720p30_aq_cyclic_refresh -- --ignored --nocapture
```

Required oracles:

- rvp9 AQ-on output decodes through rvp9-decoder and libvpx `vpxdec` under the product profile.
- ffmpeg follows the existing cross-decoder gate contract.
- No available external decoder may fail.
- Missing external decoders block sign-off evidence.
- AQ-on 720p30 median frame time is `<= 33 ms` in release mode. Report tail latency, but do not invent a new hard threshold unless the existing gate already has one.

### Standard local gates

```powershell
$null | mdtimeout 120 cargo fmt --all --check
$null | mdtimeout 300 cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets
$null | mdtimeout 600 cargo test --workspace
```

The fresh worktree may lack ignored corpus data. If workspace tests fail only because ignored test data is absent, rerun from a data-populated checkout or fetch the data before treating it as a code failure.

## Parallelization guidance

Stage 4b design can proceed while another team finishes Stage 4a.

Stage 4b implementation can also begin in parallel if the teams hold these boundaries:

- Stage 4a owns the AQ-off baseline, core quality gap, and baseline gates.
- Stage 4b owns AQ mode 3 behavior and AQ-on gates.
- RC owns base q. AQ may add only the narrow CR weighted estimate hooks.
- Any Stage 4a change to picker RD, partitioning, skip decisions, motion search, or RC can move AQ feedback. Stage 4b must rebase and rerun the product gates after Stage 4a lands.

Do not accept Stage 4b from a branch whose AQ-off Stage 4a gates are not green.

## Residual risks

- The 0.3% AQ-gain delta is tight and can be masked by unrelated Stage 4a quality movement.
- One-time leaf re-simulation may be enough, but a severe q change could still expose a local optimum mismatch. The quality gate decides whether this is acceptable.
- The AQ-on realtime budget has limited margin. If it fails, optimize the new AQ work first; do not hide it behind a flag.
- Screen-content `limit_q`, SVC, denoiser, altref, resize, and TPL/AQ interactions remain excluded. A failure clearly attributable to one of them needs a new scoped design.
- Cross-decoder evidence depends on local reference binaries and corpus availability.

## Decision log

- Draft selection: three independent HLD drafts were produced. All three judges selected Draft B, the risk-first design, as the spine.
- Runner-up grafts: the final design keeps Draft A's narrow implementation scope and Draft C's oracle-first acceptance structure.
- Tie-break: narrow AQ-aware RC integration is included because PLAN Stage 4b says "Integrate with rate control" and `notes/lld/rate-control.md` assigns the two CR estimate hooks to Stage 4b. Broader RC behavior remains out of scope.
- Correctness invariant: final segment ID must be fixed before final quantization, coefficients, and recon commit.
- Stage 4a dependency: Stage 4b work may proceed in parallel, but acceptance is blocked until Stage 4a gates are green.
- TPL: product profile keeps TPL off. This HLD does not design TPL plus CR AQ.
- Evidence: reporter-only output and skipped external prerequisites are not signed-off Stage 4b evidence.
