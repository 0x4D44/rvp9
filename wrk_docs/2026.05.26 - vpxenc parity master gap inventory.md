## 1. Scope and method

### 1.1 Scope statement

Aggregates findings across the 22 audit zones covering every encoder subsystem libvpx ships in `third_party/libvpx/vp9/encoder/`. Per Arthur 2026-05-25 (`wrk_docs/2026.05.25 - GOAL - vpxenc parity port.md`), full vpxenc parity is in scope:

- **Profiles:** 0 (8-bit 4:2:0), 1 (8-bit 4:2:2/4:4:0/4:4:4), 2 (10/12-bit 4:2:0), 3 (10/12-bit 4:2:2/4:4:0/4:4:4).
- **Rate-control modes:** CBR (RT), 1-pass VBR, 2-pass VBR + lag-in-frames, Constrained-Quality, Constant-Quantizer.
- **Speed presets:** `cpu-used 0..=8` — RDO at low speeds, RT picker at high speeds.
- **Reference-frame features:** ALTREF (filtered), compound prediction, multi-ref masking, multi-layer ARF.
- **Encoder subsystems:** pre-analysis (noise, skin, scene, source-SAD), temporal denoiser, all AQ modes, full segmentation, SVC, sub-8x8 picker, RDO intra/inter, ML partition, TPL.
- **SIMD:** all x86_64 paths (SSE2/SSSE3/SSE4.1/AVX/AVX2). **ARM NEON/SVE explicitly out of scope** (Arthur 2026-05-25). **GPU out of scope** (libvpx is CPU-only).

### 1.3 Method

Each audit walked its libvpx source(s) line by line, classified every helper / data structure / call site as PORTED / PARTIAL / NOT PORTED / OUT OF SCOPE, and cited a `file:line` for every row. The team-lead journal (`wrk_journals/2026.05.26 - JRN - vpxenc parity audit drive.md`) documents the prompt-hardening progression. This master inventory:

1. Aggregates the 22 audits' row counts into a single cross-zone table.
2. Retags scope-bias rows (see §3) per Arthur's full-parity scope.
3. Verifies every OOS row has a libvpx-absence or Arthur-signoff citation.
4. Builds a dependency DAG and identifies critical-path zones.
5. Proposes 7 implementation waves sized to prior multi-day port drives.
6. Lists the 50 highest-impact items globally as a day-to-day backlog.
7. States the exit criterion gate (Phase 3 of the goal doc).

### 4.2 Topological order

Computed by Kahn's algorithm with manual inspection. Foundation tier has no incoming dependencies; intermediate consumes foundation; leaf has no outgoing dependencies.

**Foundation (level 0 — no incoming deps):**
- **A12** (CLI plumb-through): user-facing surface only; no algorithmic dependency. Unblocks A7, A3 mode dispatch, A8 sharpness/delta_q_uv, A16/A17 arnr/noise flags, A18 svc flags, A20 bit-depth flag.
- **A20 (HBD u16 storage layer)**: pairs with every kernel zone but is itself a foundation (none of A8/A13/A16/A17 HBD twins exist without it).

**Foundation-intermediate (level 1):**
- **A7** (speed-features dispatcher): consumes A12 cpu-used/deadline flags. Gates A1, A3, A4, A8, A9, A11, A13, A14, A15, A16, A18, A21.
- **A10** (buffer pool + ref_frame_map): no algorithmic upstream; structural foundation for ALTREF, compound, SVC, dynamic resize.
- **A19** (lookahead pipeline): no algorithmic upstream; structural foundation for 2-pass, ARF, denoiser, SVC, TPL.

**Intermediate (level 2):**
- **A3** (rate control rc_mode + GF/ARF state): consumes A19 (lookahead for 2-pass and lag-VBR); produces inputs for A11 (CR↔RC), A16 (ARF GF planning), A23 (TPL RC integration).
- **A5** (pre-analysis): consumes encoder source frames; produces inputs that A1 picker, A4 partition, A11 AQ, A17 denoiser already consume as PARTIAL stubs.
- **A2** (frame-loop driver): consumes A10 (ref rotation), A19 (lookahead pop); produces frame-context bank switching and recode loop infrastructure for A1, A3, A4.

**Mid-level (level 3):**
- **A16** (ALTREF / temporal filter): consumes A3 (define_gf_group ARF gating), A10 (ALTREF slot), A19 (peek across GOP); produces ALTREF sign-bias for A15 compound.
- **A17** (temporal denoiser): consumes A5 (skin, noise estimator), A10 (force_refresh_longterm_ref).
- **A11** (segmentation/AQ): consumes A3 (RC primitives + CR feedback), A5 (skin/noise/source-SAD), A6 (pack_segmentation + segmap method).
- **A4** (partition descent including sub-8): consumes A5 (avg_source_sad), A7 (speed gates), A11 (energy AQ), A17 (denoiser scale_part_thresh).

**Upper (level 4 — major leaf clusters):**

- **A18** (SVC): consumes A2, A3, A6, A10, A11, A16, A17, A19; produces no downstream dependencies.

**Leaf (level 5 — no outgoing dependencies):**

### 4.3 Strongly connected components

No SCCs in the canonical DAG. Two near-cycles to be aware of:

- **A13 ↔ A4**: A4 RDO partition descent calls into A13 RDO mode picker; A13 is partly motivated by A4's needs (rect partition cost model). Resolution: land the picker (A13 P1-P10) first as a single-leaf consumer; then activate from A4's `rd_pick_partition` (Wave 4). No cycle in practice because A13 can be driven from a synthetic test caller at land-time.
- **A11 ↔ A3**: AQ-CR's `vp9_cyclic_refresh_rc_bits_per_mb` is called by A3's `regulate_q`, but `update_parameters` reads A3's `avg_frame_low_motion`. Resolution: land A3 rc_mode dispatch and rolling-bits state first (Wave 1); then the AQ-CR refinements (Wave 5) consume them.
