## 3. MISSED gaps (in-scope, NOT tracked in tails/deferrals)

### 3.1 — SEVERITY-1: Inter-frame DRIFT — the encoder is correct but NOT competitive (uncommitted journal, not in any roadmap/tail list)

`wrk_journals/2026.05.27 - JRN - encoder quality+speed measurement (where we stand).md` (dated today, **uncommitted**, surfaced by this audit) documents the first real BD-rate/PSNR measurement:

- **BD-rate vs libvpx is UNMEASURABLE** — rvp9's entire RD curve sits **11–28 dB below** libvpx; no common PSNR range to integrate.
- **Per-frame PSNR decays monotonically across the GOP** (KF ~31.8 dB → frame-7 low-20s) while vpxenc stays flat at ~35.7 dB. Definitively rvp9-specific inter-frame drift, not a clip artifact.
- **The RDO path (cpu4) is ~10 dB WORSE than the RT picker (cpu7)** — the inverse of intent. The "RDO is a genuine upgrade" claim rested on unit-RD-ordering + byte-exactness, never PSNR.
- **The byte-exact cross-decoder gate is blind to this** — encoder and decoder agree on the same poor-but-self-consistent reconstruction. Byte-exactness proves self-consistency, never closeness-to-source.

This is the most important finding of pass 4. The full-integration roadmap (TPL/SVC/ML/HBD, ~7000 LOC) is implicitly being layered onto an encoder that loses ~10 dB to drift over a GOP. The README's stated goal is BD-rate within 0.5% of libvpx; the encoder is presently nowhere near that, and **no zone in the 22-zone inventory tracks "reconstruction fidelity / drift"** — the inventory is a *feature/wire-parity* checklist, so a systematic residual/recon bug is invisible to it. Prime suspect per the journal: the `me_ref` vs `last_recon` split (a26f6d4) — whether inter frames code residual against, and commit, the TRUE decoded recon.

### 3.2 — Gate speed-coverage gap (see §0)

The Phase-II integrations (RDO descent, ML, TPL) never run under the sacred 4/4 gate (speed-7-only). Their byte-exactness rests entirely on individual `#[ignore]` PROOF tests. This is defensible (libvpx also gates them by speed) but means the *headline* gate is not a regression net for the headline work. A speed≤4 leg in the cross-decoder gate would close this.

### 3.3 — Encode speed 7.7–11.4× slower than vpxenc single-thread (§ measurement journal)

Also untracked by the 22 zones (which are correctness/feature-parity, not perf). 320×256 single-thread clears 30 fps at cpu7 but naive 1080p scaling misses the README real-time target. Out of the *parity* exit gate's scope but a real product gap.
