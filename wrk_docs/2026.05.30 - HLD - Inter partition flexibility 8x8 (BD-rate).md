> **One-line thesis.** The measured "0 % 8×8" is **not** caused by a stubbed 8×8 *leaf* mode-search — the production per-leaf picker (`pick_inter_mode`) already runs a full real search on Block8x8 and the only `picker_supported` exclusion is the three genuine sub-8 sizes. The 8×8 share is killed *upstream*, structurally, by the **A4 RD descent**, which (a) floors `min_partition_size` at 16×16 via `variance_hint_bounds` and (b) hard-excludes Block8x8 from PARTITION_NONE in two independent places in `descend()`. The descent then **overwrites** the variance hint's 8×8 leaves with a 16×16 NONE leaf. So the lever is **tractable** (extend the descent to RD-rank an 8×8 NONE/SPLIT) — but it lands squarely in the cost-model regime, and the prior C2 probe's flat result is a genuine warning we must pre-register a NULL against.

## 0. TL;DR for the lead (read this, then §1 and §7)

- **C2 root cause — DECIDED by code reading (not inference):** the per-leaf picker is **NOT a stub** for 8×8. The stub is in the **descent**, which never evaluates an 8×8 NONE and replaces hint-8×8 leaves with 16×16. C2 (relaxing only `variance_hint_bounds`) could not have produced *descent-searched* 8×8 leaves at all — the descent hard-codes 8×8 out independently of the bounds. So the lever is "make the descent able to choose 8×8", which is tractable.
- **BUT the cost-model risk is real and unresolved.** C2's flat/worse quality is consistent with two non-exclusive causes: (i) the few 8×8 leaves it *did* surface (via the infeasible-fallback path) flowed through a picker whose inter-RD is pixel-SSE self-consistent but NOT domain-aligned to libvpx (the open Lever-3b problem), and (ii) E14 measured that the NEWMV-vs-NEAREST RD margin is **size-invariant**, so finer granularity alone doesn't flip RD. Enabling a *real* 8×8 descent search is the clean experiment that disambiguates — and we **pre-register the NULL**: if real 8×8 still gives no matched-bitrate PSNR gain, the binding constraint IS the cost model → escalate, do not force the histogram.
- **Phase A (enable 8×8)** is the whole 35 %-share prize and is the recommended scope. **Phase B (64×64 + rect) is assess-then-defer** — 64×64 is nearly free to add (raise `max`, no new leaf code) but rect needs the H/V leaf-emit path that the variance picker also lacks (A4 G1); both are smaller shares (6.5 % / ~15-25 %).
- **Honest LOC:** Phase A is **NOT** a "real 8×8 leaf picker" rewrite (that already exists). It is ~**60-120 LOC** of descent surgery + recon/edge guards + an oracle gate, plus substantial *measurement*. The risk is the measurement outcome, not the code volume.

## 1. Problem

### 1.1 The measured divergence (given, established-oracle)

At cpu7, area-weighted inter partition distribution (`e1_block_stats`, foreman 250k):

| size | rvp9 | libvpx |
|---|---|---|
| 8×8 | **0 %** | 35 % |
| 16×16 | ~95 % | 47 % |
| 32×32 | ~3 % | 11 % |
| 64×64 | **0 %** | 6.5 % |

rvp9 is locked at 16×16. It can use neither small blocks (fine motion / detail) nor big blocks (low overhead on flat regions). The ~1.8-2.2 dB matched-Q inter-coding gap (`wrk_journals/2026.05.29` §"where we stand", Q80: 33,262 vs 30,595 b/f at −1.83 dB) is **largely** attributable to this. Because TX size is bounded by block size (`max_txsize(bsize)`), partition also gates the just-landed tx-size lever (lever #2, the 2026-05-30 CLEAN-WIN).

### 1.2 What "given" facts I verified vs corrected

The task brief carries two competing hypotheses from the journal. After reading the code, the **measured-fact / inference split** is:

- **FACT (code, `lib.rs:3729-3732`):** the per-leaf `picker_supported` guard excludes ONLY `Block4x4 | Block4x8 | Block8x4`. **Block8x8 is `picker_supported = true`** and runs the full real `pick_inter_mode` (NEWMV diamond + sub-pel + per-plane round-trip). The "8×8 leaf picker is a STUB" claim in `wrk_journals/2026.05.29` (lines 22, 357) is **imprecise**: there is no live 8×8 *leaf-mode* stub.
- **FACT (code, `lib.rs:2272-2276`, `:2339`):** the docstrings DO say "Block8x8 → ZEROMV / skip stub" and the diag string at `:3782` is literally `"SKIP-fallback (Block8x8)"`. These are **STALE** — they describe the retired F10.1c behaviour; the `picker_supported` predicate they sit above no longer matches them. (Recommend fixing the comments as part of this work; they are the likely source of the journal's "stub" framing.)
- **FACT (code, `nonrd_partition.rs:771-777`):** the **descent** is the real 8×8 stub. See §2.

## 2. Why C2 failed — diagnosis FIRST (the decision-critical section)

**Verdict: the C2 flat result is NOT explained by a stubbed 8×8 leaf picker. It is explained by (a) the descent structurally eliminating 8×8 regardless of the bounds C2 touched, plus (b) the cost-model regime that any surfaced 8×8 leaf then enters. This makes the lever tractable but NOT free of the cost-model trap.**

### 2.1 The production path (code-verified)

The default cpu7 inter path is `pick_and_simulate_inter_encode_partitions` (`crates/rvp9-encoder/src/lib.rs:2374`). Per 64×64 SB:

1. **Variance hint** — `choose_partitions_for_frame` → `choose_partitioning` (`partition.rs`) builds a `PartitionDecision` quad-tree. On **inter** frames `allow_split_to_8x8 = !is_keyframe = true` (`partition.rs:489`), so the hint **does** emit Block8x8 leaves on textured/high-motion content. (E11/E14 measured 50k-55k 8×8 hint leaves on foreman before any cap.)
2. **A4 RD descent** — default ON (`RVP9_A4_RD_PARTITION_DISABLE` opt-out, `lib.rs:2660`). `nonrd_pick_partition_with_pruners` (`nonrd_partition.rs`) is run with `(min,max)` from `variance_hint_bounds(&hint)`, and its `descent_result.leaves` **REPLACES** `sb_partitions[sb_idx]` wholesale (`lib.rs:3702`).
3. **Per-leaf picker loop** (`lib.rs:3705+`) runs `pick_inter_mode` on every surviving leaf where `picker_supported` (all sizes except sub-8).

The W4 modules (`rdo/pick_partition.rs`, `rdo/pick_inter_sb.rs`) are **standalone / infrastructure-only** (their own docstrings say "standalone-scope", "NONE+SPLIT only", "no min/max clamps") and are NOT the production hot path. Do not be misled by them.

### 2.2 The descent hard-codes 8×8 out — TWICE, independent of the bounds

`variance_hint_bounds` (`nonrd_partition.rs:512-556`) floors **both** `min_bs` and `max_bs` at `Block16x16` (the "R3 floor"). C2 relaxed exactly this floor. But even if `min_partition_size` were lowered to `Block8x8`, the recursive worker `descend()` still kills 8×8 in two more places (`nonrd_partition.rs:771-777`):

```rust
let bsize_at_floor =
    (bsize as u8) <= (params.min_partition_size as u8) || matches!(bsize, BlockSize::Block8x8);

let none_allowed = (bsize as u8) <= (params.max_partition_size as u8)
    && (bsize as u8) >= (params.min_partition_size as u8)
    && !matches!(bsize, BlockSize::Block8x8);   // <-- 8×8 can NEVER be a NONE candidate
```

Trace it: at `bsize == Block16x16`, SPLIT recurses to `descend(Block8x8)`. There, `none_allowed == false` (the `!matches!` clause), so the picker is never called; `bsize_at_floor == true`, so SPLIT is never attempted; the function falls to `inherit_hint(...)` and returns `None` (infeasible). The parent's SPLIT sum sees an infeasible child → SPLIT becomes infeasible → **16×16 NONE wins by default and `commit_leaf` stamps a 16×16 leaf over the region.** This is the precise structural cause of "0 % 8×8".

**Therefore C2 (bounds-only relax) literally could not produce descent-RD-chosen 8×8 leaves** — the two `matches!(bsize, Block8x8)` clauses are independent of `min/max`. The only way C2 shifted the histogram toward 8×8 is via `inherit_hint` fallback leaves (hint-8×8 that survived because NONE happened to be infeasible at *every* enclosing level — frame-edge / degenerate-MV-envelope SBs). Those few inherited 8×8 leaves then flowed to the per-leaf loop, where Block8x8 IS picker_supported and got a *real* search — so they were not "worthless stubs", but there were too few of them, in the wrong (edge) places, to move quality.

### 2.3 So which hypothesis is true?

The task's binary ("stub picker → tractable" vs "real picker + C2 failed → cost-model trap") is a false dichotomy once the code is read. The honest answer is **a third thing**:

- The **descent** is the 8×8 stub (cannot RD-rank an 8×8 NONE). FIXABLE — that's Phase A.
- The **per-leaf picker** is real for 8×8. So once Phase A surfaces genuine 8×8 leaves, they get a real mode search.
- **AND** that real mode search uses the inter-RD that is pixel-SSE self-consistent but not yet libvpx-domain-aligned (Lever-3b, designed-not-landed). E14 separately showed the NEWMV/NEAREST RD margin is **size-invariant** — granularity alone doesn't flip RD on foreman.

**Conclusion:** the lever is **tractable to *enable*** (the code change is bounded and well-understood), but whether it **pays in BD-rate** is exactly the open cost-model question. The C2 flat result was *under-powered* (it never enabled a real 8×8 search), so it neither confirms nor refutes the cost-model trap. **Phase A is the properly-powered experiment.** We pre-register the NULL (§6.4): if a real 8×8 descent search ALSO gives no matched-bitrate PSNR gain, the binding constraint IS the cost model (Lever-3b) → escalate.

## 3. libvpx's partition mechanism (faithful summary)

Sourced from `wrk_docs/2026.05.26 - AUDIT A4` (which walked every `static` fn in `vp9_encodeframe.c:277-5710`) and the existing rvp9 docstrings that quote line numbers. No local libvpx `.c` tree is present (`D:\language\libvpx-stock` is the built distribution only), so line numbers are quoted via the A4 audit.

### 3.1 RT path — `nonrd_pick_partition` (`vp9_encodeframe.c:4922-5181`)

This is the cpu5-8 motion-aware RD descent rvp9's `nonrd_partition.rs` ports the skeleton of. At each `bsize`:

1. Compute `partition_none_allowed` / `partition_horz_allowed` / `partition_vert_allowed` from `auto_min_max_partition_size` + `use_square_partition_only`. Crucially, **`partition_none_allowed` is gated on `bsize >= BLOCK_8X8`, NOT `> BLOCK_8X8`** — libvpx DOES evaluate a NONE candidate at 8×8 (it just doesn't SPLIT below it; `do_split = bsize >= BLOCK_8X8` makes 8×8 a leaf). This is exactly the clause rvp9 inverted with `!matches!(bsize, Block8x8)`.
2. Optional ML predictor (`ml_predict_var_partitioning`) — not relevant here.
3. **PARTITION_NONE** via `nonrd_pick_sb_modes` (the per-leaf mode picker) at `bsize`, add `partition_cost[ctx][NONE]`, compare to best.
4. **NONE breakout** (`:5017-5028`): if rate AND dist are both far below the q-conditional `partition_search_breakout_thr`, skip SPLIT/HORZ/VERT.
5. **PARTITION_SPLIT** (`:5037+`): recurse into 4 children at `subsize`, early-out at `:5042` when `sum_rdc >= best`.
6. **PARTITION_HORZ / VERT** (`:5072-5156`): build a 2-leaf rectangular partition via `nonrd_pick_sb_modes` on the rect subsize (NOT recursed), add `partition_cost[ctx][HORZ|VERT]`, compare. (rvp9's A4-rect already ports this for 16/32/64; see `nonrd_partition.rs:899-1007`.)

### 3.2 8×8 sub-block mode handling (`vp9_pick_inter_mode_sub8x8`, A14 zone)

For leaves *below* 8×8 (BLOCK_4X4 / 4X8 / 8X4), libvpx routes to `vp9_rd_pick_inter_mode_sub8x8` / the nonrd `vp9_pick_inter_mode_sub8x8` (`vp9_encodeframe.c:2077`, `:4695`, `:4761`). That picker searches per-`bmi[4]` sub-block MVs (the 4-entry `b_mode_info` array). **This is NOT needed for Phase A** — Block8x8 is a *whole-block* size handled by the normal `vp9_pick_inter_mode` / rvp9 `pick_inter_mode`. The bmi[4] machinery only matters if we ever enable true sub-8 partitions (out of scope; A14).

### 3.3 RDO path — `rd_pick_partition` (`vp9_encodeframe.c:3985-4489`, cpu0-4)

Entirely NOT PORTED in rvp9 (A4 G8). The 505-LOC RD descent with NONE/SPLIT/HORZ/VERT, `must_split` energy forcing, breakout thresholds, `prune_ref_frame_for_rect_partitions`, and the ML hooks. Out of scope here — rvp9 has no cpu0-4 path. Phase A targets the RT (cpu5-8) `nonrd` descent rvp9 actually runs.

### 3.4 The one structural difference that matters for this HLD

libvpx descends to 8×8 and **RD-ranks an 8×8 NONE** against the 16×16 parent's NONE and SPLIT. rvp9's port deliberately excised the 8×8 NONE (the `!matches!(bsize, Block8x8)` clause + R3 floor) for a cpu7-RT-budget reason recorded at `lib.rs:2537-2545` (A1': "the picker's NEWMV diamond ... is too expensive to run on every Block8x8 leaf at 720p"). **That A1' speed gate comment is now purely descriptive — there is no active area-gate code after it (`lib.rs:2547` is just an `assert!`).** So the 8×8 suppression today is *entirely* in the descent, not in any live area gate. Phase A re-introduces the 8×8 NONE that libvpx has.

## 4. Phased design

### Phase A — enable 8×8 (the 35 %-share prize) — RECOMMENDED SCOPE

**Objective:** let the A4 descent evaluate a real `pick_inter_mode` 8×8 NONE candidate and RD-rank 16×16-SPLIT-to-four-8×8 against 16×16-NONE, exactly as libvpx does, so genuine fine-motion / detail regions get 8×8 leaves.

**A.1 — Descent: admit 8×8 as a NONE candidate and a SPLIT target.**
Three coupled edits in `crates/rvp9-core/src/mode_decision/nonrd_partition.rs`, behind an env/config gate (see A.5):

- `variance_hint_bounds` (`:512-556`): lower the `min_bs` floor to `Block8x8` (keep the `max` floor at 16×16 to preserve the "always one larger size" invariant). The `min`-floor R3 rationale ("hint can collapse to all-8×8") no longer applies once 8×8 is a legal descent size.
- `descend` `none_allowed` (`:775-777`): drop the `&& !matches!(bsize, BlockSize::Block8x8)` clause so an 8×8 NONE is evaluated (matching libvpx `partition_none_allowed` = `bsize >= BLOCK_8X8`).
- `descend` `bsize_at_floor` (`:771-772`): keep the `matches!(bsize, Block8x8)` term (8×8 is still the floor — no SPLIT below it, matching libvpx `do_split = bsize >= BLOCK_8X8`). So 8×8 evaluates NONE but never recurses. The `subsize_for_split(Block16x16) == Some(Block8x8)` path (`:578`) is already correct.

Net effect: at a 16×16 parent, the descent now compares `NONE@16×16` vs `SPLIT → 4× (NONE@8×8)`, RD-picking the winner via the **real** `pick_inter_mode` callback that already exists (`lib.rs:3085+`). The callback's sub-8 guard (`lib.rs:3105-3112`) already returns `None` only for true sub-8 — Block8x8 already passes through it and builds full `MvCandidates` + envelope. **No new leaf-picker code.**

**A.2 — Per-leaf loop + recon: already 8×8-ready.**
`picker_supported` (`lib.rs:3729`) already admits Block8x8. The recon TX walk, UV-plane helpers, and writer (`infer_inter_partition`) are documented rect/8×8-aware (`lib.rs:3719-3728`). The MV-envelope tightening + degenerate-envelope ZEROMV fallback (`lib.rs:3790-3838`) was *introduced for Block8x8 edges* per its own A1 comment — it already handles 8×8 frame-edge safety. **Verify (not assume) via the cross_decoder gate** that 8×8 leaves round-trip byte-exact; the recon path claims support but it has been exercised at near-zero frequency, so this is a real test target, not a given.

**A.3 — Stale-comment cleanup (correctness hygiene, not behaviour).**
Fix the misleading docstrings (`lib.rs:2272-2276`, `:2339-2342`) and the `"SKIP-fallback (Block8x8)"` diag string (`:3782`) to say "sub-8 (4×4/4×8/8×4)". These caused the "8×8 stub" misdiagnosis. Zero behaviour change.

**A.4 — Cost-model faithfulness (the load-bearing risk).**
The descent ranks NONE-vs-SPLIT via `rd_cost(rd_mult, rate, dist)` (`nonrd_partition.rs:802`), where the picker's `rate` includes mode/mv/ref + (when active) coef-rate, and `dist` is pixel-SSE. The just-landed tx-size signalling-cost fix (lever #2, 2026-05-30) made the *emit-side* tx RD slightly more faithful but did NOT change the picker's partition-RD. The honest position:
- The 8×8 NONE candidate's `rate` carries 4× the per-block mode/ref/partition overhead of one 16×16 NONE. For the descent to *prefer* 8×8, the 8×8 SPLIT's distortion reduction must beat that overhead at the current `rd_mult`. E14 measured this margin is size-invariant for NEWMV/NEAREST → **the cost model may simply rank 16×16-NONE below 8×8-SPLIT on most foreman blocks even with a correct search.** That is the C2 trap, now properly powered.
- **We do NOT pre-tune `rd_mult` or coef-rate K1 to force 8×8.** That would be Goodhart-gaming the histogram. We enable the honest search, measure, and let the NULL (§6.4) decide whether the cost model is the real blocker.

**A.5 — Gating + speed.**
Gate Phase A behind a new env (`RVP9_PHASE_A_8X8_ENABLE`, default OFF for landing safety; flipped ON only after the oracle passes), composed with the existing A4 gate. **Speed:** enabling 8×8 NONE adds one `pick_inter_mode` call per 16×16 that splits, plus four 8×8 NONE calls per 8×8-SPLIT decision — i.e. up to ~5× picker calls on blocks that go 8×8. This is the exact cost the A1' comment warned about for 720p RT. Since this HLD targets **BD-rate, not RT**, the mitigation is: **apply Phase A only at cpu ≤ 4** (or behind the env, off in the RT preset). The descent already reuses NEWMV via the Phase-2 cache (`phase2_reuse_enabled`, `lib.rs:2663`), which partly amortises the extra calls. Bound the speed cost in the measurement (realtime_gate ms before/after) and report; do NOT ship 8×8 into the cpu7 RT preset if it blows the 33 ms budget.

### Phase B — assess 64×64 and rect (likely DEFER)

**B.1 — 64×64 (6.5 % share) — cheap to add, assess.**
`variance_hint_bounds` already floors `max` at 16×16 and lifts it to the hint's max; the descent already evaluates `NONE@64×64` when `max_partition_size == Block64x64` (the all-64 hint test passes, `nonrd_partition.rs:1298`). The reason rvp9 shows 0 % 64×64 is that the **variance hint never emits an all-64 SB on real content** (everything looks high-variance vs a static ZEROMV reference — A4 G3: no `int_pro` MV ref pick), so `max` rarely reaches 64. So 64×64 is gated by the *variance front-end*, not the descent. Adding `int_pro` to the partition reference (A4 P0, ~30 LOC) is the real 64×64 lever; raising the descent `max` alone does nothing. **Assess as a follow-up; do not bundle into Phase A.**

**B.2 — rect (HORZ/VERT, ~15-25 % of libvpx decisions) — DEFER.**
The descent's A4-rect machinery already evaluates HORZ/VERT for 16/32/64 parents (`nonrd_partition.rs:899-1007`) and the recon/writer claim rect support. Extending rect down to a 16×16-parent → 16×8/8×16 is *mechanically* in range (the descent caps at 16×16; `subsize_for_horz(Block16x16) == Block16x8`). BUT: (i) the **variance hint** still emits square-only (A4 G1 / `set_vt_partitioning` HORZ/VERT NOT PORTED), so rect leaves can only come from the descent, not the hint; (ii) rect doubles the picker calls again. **Defer** — it is a second-order share behind 8×8 and entangled with the same cost-model question. Re-assess only if Phase A pays.

### VP9 spec constraints (addressed)

- **Sub-8 modes / bmi[4]:** NOT touched. Phase A is whole-block 8×8 only (`vp9_pick_inter_mode`, not `sub8x8`). The three sub-8 sizes stay on the ZEROMV/skip fallback (`picker_supported == false`). No `b_mode_info[4]` sub-block-MV handling needed.
- **Quad-tree legality:** the descent's `commit_leaf` / `inherit_hint` already produce legal aligned quad-trees (`PartitionDecision::is_valid_partition`). 8×8 leaves are single-cell — trivially aligned.
- **TX size:** an 8×8 leaf bounds `max_txsize` to `Tx8x8`, which is correct VP9 and is exactly the finer-TX lever #2 wants on detail regions.
- **Picker/emit consistency:** the descent and the per-leaf loop call the *same* `pick_inter_mode`, so the partition shape the descent chose and the modes the emit loop re-picks are consistent at 8×8 (same drift-fix caveat as today, `lib.rs:4504`).

## 6. Regression guard + oracle (PRE-REGISTERED before any code)

**Baseline:** current `main` HEAD with default gates (A4 ON, S2 ON, S3 OFF, lever-#2 tx-size ON). Re-measure HEAD fresh; do not reuse stale numbers.

**Primary clip:** foreman_cif 256×256, 64 frames (the established `e1`/`e9` clip). **2nd clip:** a 2nd natural clip (e.g. `akiyo`/`city` from the corpus) AND testsrc (the synthetic-content trap-detector).

### 6.1 Distribution oracle — `e1_block_stats`
Success requires the **8×8 area share to RISE materially toward libvpx** (target: from 0 % to double-digit %; not necessarily 35 %). Report the full `[8,16,32,64]` area histogram rvp9 vs libvpx, before/after.

### 6.2 Matched-Q oracle — `e9_quality_gap`
Run `cargo test --release --test e9_quality_gap -- --ignored --nocapture` before/after. Report per-Q (Q80/Q120/Q160): pred-PSNR, recon-PSNR, bits/frame by category, inter-Y tx histogram. We want matched-Q **coef-bits down or PSNR up** at equal q.

### 6.3 Matched-bitrate PSNR (the BD-rate-facing metric)
At the 4 stage-4a target bitrates, mean Y-PSNR rvp9 vs libvpx. **Success = mean-PSNR UP / BD-rate improved on foreman AND no regression on the 2nd clip AND no regression on testsrc.**

### 6.4 PRE-REGISTERED NULL (the honesty gate — decides tractable vs blocked)
> **If enabling a real 8×8 descent search moves the partition distribution toward libvpx (8×8 share rises) BUT matched-bitrate mean-PSNR does NOT improve (within noise) — then the binding constraint is the COST MODEL, not partition granularity. The C2 trap is confirmed at full power. STOP. Do NOT tune `rd_mult`/coef-K1 to force a histogram match. Escalate to Arthur: the 8×8 lever is blocked behind Lever-3b (inter-RD domain alignment).**

This is the exact failure mode E14 + C2 hinted at; pre-registering it stops us from Goodhart-gaming the `e1` histogram into looking like libvpx while quality stays flat.

### 6.5 Hard correctness gates (non-negotiable)
- **cross_decoder 4/4 byte-exact**, **decode_failed 0** across all gate streams. (R2.)
- `cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --release` green; clippy clean on touched files.
- Speed report: realtime_gate ms before/after (informational for R3; not a pass/fail on the BD-rate axis, but must be reported so the lead can decide RT-preset inclusion).
