**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Driver:** Claude (codec engineer) — for Arthur (team lead) sign-off
**Status:** PROPOSED — DESIGN ONLY, NO production code. Awaiting lead sign-off.
**Related:**

- `wrk_docs/2026.05.26 - AUDIT A4 - partition gap inventory.md` (the partition map)
- `wrk_docs/2026.05.29 - HLD - Inter-RD reconciliation (skip + domain alignment).md` (S2 / Lever-3b — the SAME picker this phase calls)
- `wrk_journals/2026.05.27 - JRN - A4 RDO partition descent landed.md` (the descent this phase optimizes)

> **One-line thesis.** rvp9 runs the inter mode picker **twice** per leaf: once
> inside the recursive RD partition descent (`nonrd_pick_partition_with_pruners`,
> profiled at 52.7 %) to choose the SHAPE, and again per FINAL leaf (37.4 %) to
> re-pick the MODE — discarding everything the descent computed. libvpx at
> cpu-used 7 runs the picker **once** per leaf (variance partition + a single mode
> pass, no recursive RD descent). This phase removes the redundancy.
> **MEASURED CORRECTION TO THE PROFILE STORY (this HLD, §1.3): disabling the
> descent entirely buys only ~1.17–1.21× speed but costs ~1.0–1.3 dB Y-PSNR AND
> raises bitrate — so the descent is earning its quality, and the win is NOT a
> free 2×.** The right move is **(A) cache + reuse the descent's leaf picks**, not
> (B) delete the descent. Decision-changing ⇒ quality-gated, not byte-identity.

## 1. Problem

### 1.1 The picker runs twice per leaf (code-verified)

Profiling (established, not re-litigated) attributes **96 % of inter-frame time to
`pick_inter_mode`, invoked twice**: ~52.7 % in the partition-shape RD descent and
~37.4 % per FINAL leaf. The structure, confirmed in
`crates/rvp9-encoder/src/lib.rs::pick_and_simulate_inter_encode_partitions`:

**Pass 1 — the descent (shape search).** For each 64×64 SB, when the descent is
enabled (default ON, §1.2), `lib.rs:2977-3586` builds a `PartitionDecision` hint
from the variance leaf list, derives `(min,max)_partition_size`
(`variance_hint_bounds`), and calls
`nonrd_pick_partition_with_pruners(&hint, &params, &mut descent_picker, …)`
(`lib.rs:3579-3585`). The `descent_picker` closure (`lib.rs:3052-3391`) is the
**real** picker: it builds a full `PickInterModeParams` per candidate sub-block
and calls **`pick_inter_mode(&pick_params)`** (`lib.rs:3375`). The descent
(`crates/rvp9-core/src/mode_decision/nonrd_partition.rs`, `descend`,
`nonrd_partition.rs:753-1084`) invokes this callback for every candidate shape it
ranks:
- PARTITION_NONE at the parent size (`nonrd_partition.rs:796-797`),
- 4× recursive children for PARTITION_SPLIT (`nonrd_partition.rs:855-866`),
- 2× halves each for PARTITION_HORZ and PARTITION_VERT
  (`nonrd_partition.rs:967-991`, A4-rect).

So at a 64×64 SB the descent can call `pick_inter_mode` at the 64 NONE, the four
32 NONEs, their sixteen 16 NONEs, plus the rect halves at each level — **many
picker invocations per SB**, all to rank shapes.

**Pass 2 — the final per-leaf loop (mode re-pick).** The descent's chosen tree
**replaces** the SB leaf list: `sb_partitions[sb_idx] = new_leaves`
(`lib.rs:3654`). Crucially, `new_leaves` carries only the leaf **geometry**
(`mi_row_in_sb`, `mi_col_in_sb`, `leaf_bsize`) and seeds the mode fields to
throwaway defaults — `picked_mode: ZeroMv`, `picked_mv: ZERO`, `skip: true`
(`lib.rs:3639-3641`), explicitly commented *"The picker loop below overwrites
these"* (`lib.rs:3635`). Then the per-leaf loop (`lib.rs:3657`+) rebuilds **all**
the same per-leaf state — `mv_limits` (`lib.rs:3774-3832`), `find_mv_refs` /
`mvc` / `mvc_golden` (`lib.rs:3899-3949`), `inter_mode_ctx`, `skip_ctx`,
`switchable_interp_ctx`, intra edges, neighbour MVs, `leaf_qindex` — and calls
**`pick_inter_mode(&pick_params)` again** (`lib.rs:4455`, via the `rdo_unified`
match; the RT path is the `None` arm).

**The descent's `pick_inter_mode` result is computed, then thrown away.** The
callback maps `result` → `PartitionRdCandidate { rate, dist, mv, mode, ref_frame }`
(`lib.rs:3384-3390`), and the descent stores it on the winning `NonRdLeaf.pick`
(`nonrd_partition.rs:446`) — but `new_leaves` (`lib.rs:3604-3653`) **drops
`leaf.pick` entirely**, keeping only the geometry. The final pass then recomputes
the identical thing for the leaves the descent chose as PARTITION_NONE.

### 1.2 What exactly is recomputed (quantified)

For a leaf the descent committed as **PARTITION_NONE at size S**, the descent
already called `pick_inter_mode` for that exact `(mi_row, mi_col, S)` and got
`(best_mode, best_mv, best_ref_frame, best_rate, best_distortion,
picked_interp_filter, …)`. The final pass re-runs `pick_inter_mode` for the **same
`(mi_row, mi_col, S)`** — the most expensive single operation in the encoder —
to recover fields the descent had in hand and discarded. This is the **pure
redundant work**: the ~37.4 % final-leaf cost is, for descent-NONE leaves, a
recomputation of a Pass-1 result.

For leaves the descent committed via **SPLIT / HORZ / VERT**, the descent's
deepest picker calls were at the **child / half** sizes, which ARE the final leaf
sizes — so those final picks are also re-runs of the descent's leaf-level calls.
The descent's **interior** NONE/HORZ/VERT calls at *parent* sizes that lost
(e.g. the 64 NONE when SPLIT won) are genuine extra shape-ranking work with no
final-pass twin; those are not "double" picks, they are the cost of the RD search
itself.

**Net: the redundancy is "for every committed leaf, the descent already picked
its mode and the final pass picks it again."** The interior shape-ranking picks
are not redundant (they have no final twin) but are the descent's own cost.

### 1.3 MEASURED opportunity ceiling (this HLD — the load-bearing data)

The brief asked us to run `RVP9_A4_RD_PARTITION_DISABLE=1` to bound the opportunity
cheaply. **We did.** foreman 320×256 (60 f), cpu-used 7, CBR, single pass, release
build, best-of-3 wall-clock:

| descent | bitrate | best time | fps | size (KiB) | mean Y-PSNR | min | KF |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ON** (default) | 400k | 1.496 s | 40.1 | 129.8 | **33.392** | 31.986 | 53.923 |
| **OFF** (`=1`)   | 400k | 1.278 s | 47.0 | 130.3 | **32.087** | 30.293 | 53.923 |
| **ON** (default) | 1200k | 1.960 s | 30.6 | 286.2 | **38.657** | 34.526 | 53.923 |
| **OFF** (`=1`)   | 1200k | 1.619 s | 37.1 | 292.4 | **37.658** | 34.039 | 53.923 |

**Three measured facts that reshape the phase:**

1. **Deleting the descent (option B's ceiling) is a BAD trade at speed 7.** It
   costs **−1.31 dB** (400k) / **−1.00 dB** (1200k) mean Y-PSNR **and** *raises*
   the bitrate (129.8→130.3, 286.2→292.4 KiB) — worse on both rate AND distortion
   — for only **~1.17–1.21× speed**. The RD descent is genuinely improving the
   partition shape; killing it is not "free speed".

2. **The descent's speed cost (~1.17–1.21×) is far below the 52.7 % profile
   share.** The 52.7 % figure is the *picker time spent inside the descent*, but
   removing the descent does NOT remove 52.7 % of frame time — because the final
   per-leaf pass still picks every leaf, and the descent's interior shape-ranking
   picks at parent sizes are a minority of total picker calls. **Inference (not
   measured):** the redundant *double-pick* portion — the part that (A) can remove
   without changing decisions — is a *subset* of the 52.7 %, namely the
   descent's leaf-level NONE picks that the final pass re-runs. The profile share
   and the wall-clock delta measure different things; do not conflate them.

3. **Therefore the win from (A) cache-reuse is bounded by the double-pick subset,
   not by 52.7 %.** Honest expectation for (A): a meaningful but sub-2× speedup at
   **zero or near-zero quality change** (it reuses the descent's own picks). The
   big "halve the picker" framing in the Phase-1 HLD §7 was optimistic; this
   measurement corrects it.

> **Honest call-out to the lead:** the Phase-1 HLD framed Phase 2 as "halving the
> 52.7 %". The measurement says the descent-vs-no-descent wall-clock delta is only
> ~1.2×, and removing the descent wholesale *loses quality*. So Phase 2's real,
> safe lever is the **cache-reuse of the redundant leaf picks (A)** — recover the
> double-pick subset at no quality cost — NOT pruning the descent (B). The speed
> ceiling is lower than advertised; the quality risk of the aggressive option is
> now quantified as ≥1 dB.

### 1.4 The default-ON gate

`RVP9_A4_RD_PARTITION_DISABLE` (`lib.rs:2660-2662`): the descent runs by **default
ON** at all speeds; setting the env var to anything opts OUT to the pure
variance-hint partition (pre-A4 behaviour). The legacy
`RVP9_F3_NONRD_PARTITION_ENABLE` forces ON for back-compat. This is the flag we
used in §1.3 and the flag this HLD recommends keeping as the A/B measurement knob.

## 2. Prior art — how libvpx avoids the double-pick

Verified in `third_party/libvpx/vp9/encoder/`. **libvpx has TWO RT partition
strategies, and NEITHER double-picks. They differ in how they avoid it.**

### 2.1 At cpu-used 7 the default is a ONE-SHOT partition + ONE mode pass

`vp9_speed_features.c`: at `speed >= 5` `partition_search_type =
REFERENCE_PARTITION` (`:596`), but at `speed >= 6` it is **reset to
`VAR_BASED_PARTITION`** (`:667`). So **cpu-used 7 uses `VAR_BASED_PARTITION`** —
the cheap variance-driven `choose_partitioning` (`vp9_encodeframe.c:1261`,
non-recursive, no RD), followed by `nonrd_use_partition`
(`vp9_encodeframe.c:5317-5424`).

`nonrd_use_partition` reads the pre-decided shape from `mi[0]->sb_type`
(`:5333`), then per leaf calls `nonrd_pick_sb_modes(...)` (= the mode picker,
`:5344/5355/5377/…`) **once** and **immediately** `encode_b_rt(...)` right after
(`:5350/5361/5383/…`). The pick is consumed by the emit in the same call — **the
mode is picked exactly once per leaf, and there is no recursive RD shape search at
all.** This is structurally option (B) done right: a one-shot variance partition,
one mode pass, emit-from-the-pick. **rvp9's default (recursive RD descent +
re-pick) is strictly more work than libvpx does at this speed.**

### 2.2 When libvpx DOES run a recursive partition, it CACHES the leaf pick (option A)

The RD-descent path (`nonrd_pick_partition`, `vp9_encodeframe.c:4922-5210`, used
under `REFERENCE_PARTITION`/`ML_BASED_PARTITION`, e.g. cpu-used 5) does run the
picker per candidate shape via `nonrd_pick_sb_modes` (`:5001/5077/5121/…`). But
`nonrd_pick_sb_modes` (`:4711-4778`) **stores the result into the
`PICK_MODE_CONTEXT *ctx`**: `ctx->rate`, `ctx->dist` (`:4776-4777`), and the
caller copies `ctx->mic = *xd->mi[0]` (the full chosen ModeInfo), `ctx->mbmi_ext`,
`ctx->skip_txfm[0]`, `ctx->skip`.

Then `fill_mode_info_sb` (`:4780-4835`) walks the winning PC_TREE and **splats the
cached `pc_tree->none.mic` / `pc_tree->{vertical,horizontal}[i].mic` into the mi
grid WITHOUT re-picking** (`:4794`, `:4800`, `:4813`, recursing on SPLIT at
`:4824-4830`). The emit (`encode_b_rt`) then reconstructs from the cached
ModeInfo + `ctx->skip_txfm`. **libvpx never re-runs the picker for the emit — the
descent's chosen leaf result is the emit's source of truth.**

### 2.3 Conclusion

- **cpu-used 7 (rvp9's target speed):** libvpx = one-shot variance partition +
  ONE pick per leaf (no descent). rvp9 = recursive RD descent + a SECOND pick per
  leaf. **rvp9 does two things libvpx does zero of.**
- **When a recursive partition IS used (cpu-used ≤5):** libvpx caches the pick in
  the PC_TREE and the emit reuses it (option A). It never picks twice.

rvp9 has neither mechanism: it keeps the recursive descent (a cpu-used-≤5 feature
it ported to all speeds) AND re-picks for the emit (because the leaf list throws
away `leaf.pick`). This phase makes rvp9 do what libvpx does — **reuse the
descent's pick (A)** — and optionally lets speed 7 skip the descent like libvpx
does (B).

## 3. Design options

All three are **decision-changing or decision-neutral with a correctness
obligation** — none is byte-identity-by-construction the way Phase 1 was. They are
quality-gated (§4). (A) and (C) compose.

### 3.1 (A) — Cache + reuse the descent's leaf picks (RECOMMENDED FIRST)

**Change.** Carry the descent's `pick_inter_mode` result through to the final pass
and skip the re-pick for the leaf the descent committed.

- Widen `NonRdLeaf.pick` (it already holds a `PartitionRdCandidate` with `(rate,
  dist, mv, mode, ref_frame)`, `nonrd_partition.rs:446`) — OR add a parallel
  store — so the **full** `PickInterModeResult` the callback received
  (`lib.rs:3375`) survives. Today the callback narrows it to 5 fields
  (`lib.rs:3384-3390`); to reuse it for the emit we need the rest
  (`picked_interp_filter`, `picked_intra_mode`, `coef_rate`, `coef_dist`, `var_y`,
  `sse_y`, `dct_eob_total`, `best_rd_cost`). Keep the full result in a per-SB
  side-table keyed by `(mi_row_in_sb, mi_col_in_sb)`.
- In `new_leaves` (`lib.rs:3604-3653`) seed `picked_mode`/`picked_mv`/`skip`/etc.
  from the cached result instead of the ZeroMv placeholders.
- In the final loop, when a cached pick exists for the leaf, **use it** instead of
  calling `pick_inter_mode` (`lib.rs:4455`). The downstream machinery the final
  pass STILL must run (the redundancy is only the *pick*, not the emit):
  - **TX-size RD** (`choose_inter_luma_tx_size`) and the forward-quant coef-source
    bridge — these consume the picked `(mode, mv, ref, filter)` and are not part
    of `pick_inter_mode`; they run regardless.
  - The **mi-grid splat** + recon IT-add.

**How close to byte-identical?** This is the central honest question, and the
answer is **NOT byte-identical in general, because the descent's picker context
differs from the final pass's context** — three measured divergences:

1. **Causal mi-grid mismatch (the big one — already documented in-tree).** The
   descent runs **before** the per-leaf loop, so its `mi_grid` carries only
   prior-SB neighbours (`lib.rs:3050` reads `mi_grid_ro`, `:3113-3117` notes
   "within-SB neighbours return `None`"). The final pass runs **after** earlier
   leaves are splatted, so it sees a **causal** grid with within-SB neighbours.
   This changes `find_mv_refs`/`mvc`, `inter_mode_ctx`, `skip_ctx`,
   `switchable_interp_ctx`, and the P13 neighbour MVs — which change the pick.
   **The DRIFT-FIX at `lib.rs:4504-4533` already exists precisely for this:** it
   re-pins NEAREST/NEAR `best_mv` from the causal `mvc*` because *"the cpu≤4 RDO
   descent picks during the partition search with a grid that lacks the within-SB
   neighbours present at decode time — so its NEAREST/NEAR `best_mv` can disagree
   with the decoder"*. **This is a cross-decoder correctness hazard, not just a
   quality one.** Any (A) that reuses the descent's NEAREST/NEAR MV verbatim would
   reintroduce the exact drift this fix guards against. (A) MUST keep the
   DRIFT-FIX re-pin (re-derive NEAREST/NEAR MVs from the causal grid even when
   reusing the descent's mode choice).
2. **`rd_thresh_table` divergence.** The descent passes `rd_thresh_table: None`
   (`lib.rs:3337`, deliberately, to avoid coupling descent decisions to post-pick
   state). The final pass uses the **live** table and **mutates it post-pick**
   (P20, `lib.rs:4485-4492`). So even at the identical position with an identical
   grid, the picker can rank modes differently (the adaptive RD threshold shifts
   candidate early-exits). Reusing the descent's pick means the chosen mode was
   selected under `rd_thresh = None`; the final pass would have used the live
   table. **This is a genuine (small) decision movement.**
3. **Intra-edge / recon source.** The descent reads `recon_y_ro`
   (`lib.rs:3051`, last-recon-initialised) for intra edges; the final pass reads
   the in-progress `recon_yuv_out` (`lib.rs:4033`) updated by earlier leaves in
   this frame. For an intra-winning leaf the edges differ → the pick differs.

**So (A) is decision-changing in a bounded way.** It is **byte-identical only on
the subset of leaves where the causal-grid, rd_thresh, and intra-edge contexts
happen to coincide** (e.g. the first leaf of an SB with no within-SB neighbours
and an inter winner). For the rest, (A) substitutes the descent's pick (made under
a slightly different context) for the final pick. The quality gate (§4) measures
whether that substitution costs anything. **Pre-registered expectation: ≈0 dB —
the descent already uses the real picker, and its context differs only by
within-SB neighbours + adaptive thresholds, which are second-order.** But it is
NOT a "by construction" claim; it must be measured.

> **A correctness-safe variant of (A):** reuse the descent's pick for *seeding*
> only (mode/ref/filter), then keep the final pass's cheap context-dependent
> corrections (the DRIFT-FIX MV re-pin from the causal grid; the skip-context
> derivation). This is "(A) for the expensive search, final-pass for the cheap
> causal fixups". It is the recommended concrete shape because it preserves the
> cross-decoder invariant (R1) while still skipping the diamond/subpel search and
> candidate RD — which is where the picker's cost lives.

**Speed.** Removes the redundant `pick_inter_mode` (the diamond + subpel + per-
candidate convolve + model-RD) for every committed leaf — the double-pick subset
of §1.3. Estimated **~1.2–1.5× overall** (bounded above by the §1.3 ceiling minus
the descent's irreducible interior shape-ranking cost; not the 2× the 52.7 %
suggests).

**Complexity.** MEDIUM. The plumbing is the work: a per-SB pick cache, widening
the carried result, and the careful preservation of the DRIFT-FIX + skip-context
fixups. The descent and picker themselves are untouched.

### 3.2 (B) — Prune the descent at speed ≥ 7 (libvpx-RT parity)

**Change.** At cpu-used ≥ 7, skip `nonrd_pick_partition_with_pruners` entirely;
use the variance hint's partition directly (`choose_partitioning` output), one
mode pass per leaf — i.e. what libvpx does (§2.1). This is the
`RVP9_A4_RD_PARTITION_DISABLE=1` path promoted to the speed-7 default.

**MEASURED trade (§1.3): this is a BAD deal at speed 7.** −1.0 to −1.31 dB mean
Y-PSNR, *higher* bitrate, for only ~1.2× speed. The variance hint over-splits at
high q (the A4 audit §G3: variance vs a static ZEROMV reference systematically
over-splits high-motion content) and the RD descent is correcting that. **Do NOT
pursue (B) as the speed-7 default on this evidence.**

(B) only becomes attractive if the variance hint is first improved to libvpx
parity (A4 audit P0–P5: int_pro ref pick, force_split propagation, content-state
thresholds) so the one-shot partition is good enough to not need RD correction.
That is a large, separate workstream (the A4 audit's Tier-0/1). **Out of scope
here.** (B) is the long-term destination; it is not reachable cheaply today
without the quality loss above.

### 3.3 (C) — Hybrid / partial

Two coherent hybrids:

- **(C1) (A) + descent depth cap.** Cache-reuse (A) AND cap the descent recursion
  one level shallower at speed 7 (e.g. never search below 16×16 as NONE — the
  descent already floors at Block16x16, `nonrd_partition.rs:771-772`; a tighter
  cap would skip the 32→16 NONE ranking). Smaller incremental speed, smaller
  quality risk than (B). Measurable with `min_partition_size` tweaks.
- **(C2) (A) + breakout.** Cache-reuse (A) AND turn on the **already-ported** ML
  partition-search breakout (`sf.rd_ml_partition.search_breakout`, the
  `BreakoutPruner` hook wired at `lib.rs:3438-3504`, gated `base_qindex >= 100`).
  The breakout suppresses SPLIT recursion when NONE is decisively best — cutting
  the descent's interior picks (the non-redundant part (A) can't touch). This is
  the cleanest way to also shrink the descent's own cost without deleting it.

(C) is not a standalone first step; it is "(A) plus a knob". Recommend landing (A)
first, measuring, then layering (C2) breakout if more speed is needed.

### 3.4 Recommendation

**Land (A) — cache + reuse — in the correctness-safe variant (§3.1), measure,
then optionally layer (C2) breakout.** Rationale:
- (A) recovers the redundant leaf picks at ≈0 quality cost (the descent already
  uses the real picker; reuse is faithful modulo the bounded context divergences).
- (B) is measured to lose ≥1 dB for ~1.2× — a bad trade; shelve until the variance
  hint reaches libvpx parity.
- (C2) breakout is the natural follow-up that attacks the descent's *own* cost
  (the part (A) leaves on the table) using infrastructure already in-tree.

**Use `RVP9_A4_RD_PARTITION_DISABLE` as the standing A/B knob** to re-confirm the
ceiling whenever the variance hint or picker changes.

## 4. Quality oracle + test plan (pre-registered)

Because (A) is decision-changing (§3.1), the gate is **matched-bitrate Y-PSNR**
plus the conformance gates — NOT byte-identity. Pre-registered before any code.

### 4.1 Primary oracle — matched-bitrate Y-PSNR, two corpora

Reuse the existing harness `scripts/agent_psnr_sweep.py` (rvp9enc → vpxdec →
per-frame Y-PSNR) and the in-tree `quality_gate` test:
- **foreman** `tests/corpus/foreman_320x256_60f.y4m` (320×256, multiple-of-64 —
  the §1.3 corpus). Also `foreman_cif` 256×256 centre-crop
  (`quality_gate_stage4a_realcontent`, **the headline Track-F gate**).
- **testsrc** — **NOTE: the raw `testsrc_720p30.y4m` is 1280×720; 720 is NOT a
  multiple of 64, so the Stage-4a encoder rejects it** (verified: *"height must be
  a positive multiple of 64"*). Use the **256×256 centre-crop** the existing
  `quality_gate_stage4a` test already uses (`read_cropped`), matching the brief's
  "testsrc" intent.

Bitrates: 400k, 1200k (foreman); the gate's 250–2000k range (testsrc crop).
cpu-used 7 (the target), and 4 (the RDO path, since (A) touches `rdo_unified`
too).

**Baseline (measured this HLD, descent ON = current default):** foreman cpu7 mean
Y-PSNR 33.392 dB @ 400k (129.8 KiB), 38.657 dB @ 1200k (286.2 KiB).

### 4.2 Pass criteria (pre-registered)

- **Quality:** matched-bitrate mean Y-PSNR regression **≤ 0.1 dB** on foreman AND
  the testsrc-crop, at every measured bitrate, vs the descent-ON baseline (§4.1).
  (A) is expected to land at **≈0.00 dB** — it reuses the descent's own picks; the
  0.1 dB is the tolerance for the bounded context divergences (§3.1). If a
  position is byte-identical (first-leaf inter winners, §3.1) the PSNR is
  unchanged there by construction.
  - **Stretch / nice-to-have:** if (A) is implemented so the reused leaves are
    *exactly* the descent's pick AND the descent context were made causal (a
    larger change), some leaves go byte-identical — measurable via the F.0.2
    decision oracle's partition-match% (`crate::f02_oracle`). Not required for
    sign-off.
- **Speed:** foreman cpu7 fps improves **≥ 1.2×** (the floor; §1.3 bounds the
  ceiling). Report fps + ratio-vs-vpxenc. (A) alone is not expected to be ≥1.5×;
  if the lead wants ≥1.5× it requires (C2) breakout on top.
- **Conformance (MUST hold):**
  - `cross_decoder_gate` (`crates/rvp9-encoder/tests/cross_decoder_gate.rs`,
    `--ignored`) **stays 4/4** (rvp9-decoder vs vpxdec/ffmpeg byte-exact across
    its scenarios). **This is the load-bearing guard for the DRIFT-FIX hazard
    (R1).**
  - **`decode_failed == 0`:** every post-change fixture decodes cleanly through
    both `vpxdec` AND `rvp9-decoder` (exit 0, pixel counts match).
- **Regression guards green:** `quality_gate` (both stage4a tests),
  `realtime_gate` (median frame-time, ≤ budget), `w_rd_partition` /
  `w_rd_partition_rect` (the descent PROOF tests — (A) must not break the descent
  itself), `cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --lib`.

### 4.3 Positive control — the double-pick actually shrank

Add one cheap instrumented counter: `pick_inter_mode` invocation count per frame
(an atomic bump at the two call sites, `lib.rs:3375` and `:4455`), reported under
an env diag. **Target:** total picker invocations/frame drops by the descent's
committed-leaf count (the reused picks). A change that claimed (A) but left the
count unchanged would be a no-op masquerade — this counter catches it. (Mirrors
the anti-G3 discipline already used by `RD_PARTITION_RESHAPED_SBS`,
`lib.rs:3595`.)

### 4.4 Attribution

Run the §1.3 `RVP9_A4_RD_PARTITION_DISABLE` A/B **again after (A) lands** to
confirm the ceiling moved as expected: with (A), descent-ON time should drop
toward descent-OFF time while keeping descent-ON quality. The gap between
"(A)-on" and "descent-off" is the descent's irreducible interior shape-ranking
cost — the budget (C2) breakout would then attack.

## 5. Risks + rollback

## 6. Interactions

- **Phase-1 SIMD (independent, composes).** Phase 1 made each `pick_inter_mode`
  cheaper (byte-identical); Phase 2 (A) makes the encoder call it *fewer times*.
  Orthogonal: SIMD speeds the kernel, (A) removes a call. They multiply. (A) does
  NOT touch any SIMD kernel or dispatch slot.
- **S2 / Lever-3b (the SAME picker — note the coupling).** S2/Lever-3b
  (`wrk_docs/2026.05.29 - HLD - Inter-RD reconciliation …`) modifies
  `evaluate_candidate_for_filter` inside `pick_inter_mode` (the skip tie-break +
  `coef_rate` domain). **Phase 2 (A) caches the OUTPUT of `pick_inter_mode`.** If
  S2 changes what the picker returns (it changes `best_rate`/skip), then:
  - The descent's cached pick and the final pass's pick BOTH change identically
    (same picker) — so (A) stays faithful; no new divergence from S2.
  - BUT the §4.1 quality baseline (33.392 dB etc.) is the **pre-S2** number. If S2
    lands first, re-baseline before measuring (A). **Sequencing recommendation:
    land S2/Lever-3b first (it's a quality fix with a measured ~6 dB headline),
    re-baseline, THEN land Phase-2 (A) (a speed fix) on top.** (A) is
    quality-neutral, so stacking it after S2's quality gain is clean; the reverse
    order would force re-measuring (A) against a moving picker.
  - **Conflict surface:** both touch the `PickInterModeResult` plumbing region. If
    S2 adds fields (it adds `dct_eob_total`/`picker_skip`, already present
    `inter.rs:1041-1045`), (A)'s cache must carry them. Low risk, but coordinate
    the struct's field set if both are in flight.

## 8. Executive summary (for the lead)

- **The double-pick is real and code-confirmed:** the descent
  (`nonrd_pick_partition_with_pruners`, `lib.rs:3579`) runs the **real**
  `pick_inter_mode` per candidate shape (`lib.rs:3375`), then the leaf list throws
  the result away (`new_leaves` keeps only geometry, `lib.rs:3604-3653`), and the
  final per-leaf loop re-runs `pick_inter_mode` for the same leaf
  (`lib.rs:4455`). libvpx at cpu-used 7 picks **once** per leaf (variance
  partition + `nonrd_use_partition`, no recursive RD descent); when it does run a
  descent (cpu≤5) it **caches** the pick in the PC_TREE and the emit reuses it
  (`fill_mode_info_sb`, `vp9_encodeframe.c:4780`). rvp9 does neither.
- **MEASURED opportunity ceiling (this HLD, `RVP9_A4_RD_PARTITION_DISABLE` A/B,
  foreman cpu7):** deleting the descent buys only **~1.17–1.21×** speed but costs
  **−1.0 to −1.31 dB** mean Y-PSNR **and raises bitrate**. The descent is earning
  its quality; the 52.7 % profile share is NOT a 2× speed bucket.
- **Recommendation: (A) cache + reuse the descent's leaf picks**, in the
  correctness-safe variant that **keeps the existing DRIFT-FIX** (the descent's
  NEAREST/NEAR MV is cross-decoder-unsafe to reuse verbatim, `lib.rs:4504-4533`).
  Expected ~1.2–1.5× at **≈0 dB**, gated at ≤0.1 dB. ~210 LOC, fully revertible,
  behind the existing default-ON gate.
- **(B) prune the descent at speed 7 is measured to be a bad trade (−1 dB for
  ~1.2×) — recommend AGAINST** until the variance hint reaches libvpx parity (a
  separate, large A4-audit workstream). The aggressive option's quality risk is
  now quantified, not speculative.
- **Interactions:** composes with Phase-1 SIMD (independent — SIMD speeds the
  kernel, (A) removes calls). **Couples with S2/Lever-3b via the shared
  `pick_inter_mode`** — land S2 first, re-baseline, then (A) (quality-neutral) on
  top; coordinate the `PickInterModeResult` field set if both are in flight.

**Single biggest open question for the lead:** **(A) cache-reuse (low-risk, ~1.2–
1.5×, ≈0 dB) vs (B) descent-prune (bigger framing, but MEASURED −1 dB for ~1.2× at
speed 7) — which first?** The measurement answers it: **(A) first** — (B) loses
quality for less speed than its profile share implied. The sub-question is
**whether (A) should reuse the pick *fully* (skip the final `pick_inter_mode`
entirely, accepting the bounded context divergence + keeping the DRIFT-FIX) or
*seed-only* (reuse the search center, let the final pass re-rank under the live
causal/rd_thresh context)** — full reuse is faster, seed-only is strictly safer on
the 0.1 dB gate. Recommend full reuse + the DRIFT-FIX, with seed-only as the
measured fallback.
