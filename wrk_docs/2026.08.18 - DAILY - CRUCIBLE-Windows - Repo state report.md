## 4. The spatial-VBR gap — what was established, and where the reasoning breaks

### 4.1 What the oracle genuinely established (not disputed)

Across Units 1208–1211 and 1215, on exact-input paired candidates:

- Prediction/model inputs, transform size, DC/AC skip thresholds, normalized
  variance and SSE, DC/AC dequantizers, `round_fp` and `quant_fp` all match
  **22/22**, every aggregate ratio exactly `1.000000`.
- Residual, Hadamard, and quantized-coefficient fingerprints are **bit-exact wherever
  both encoders run them** — 8/8 jointly traced 16×16 on In To Tree, 9/9 on Ducks,
  7/7 on Old Town.
- The divergence is a **policy gate**: rvp9 runs coefficient RD on 0 of 12 8×8
  candidates (In To Tree), 0 of 22 (Ducks), 0 of 7 (Old Town), where libvpx runs all
  of them.
- Unit 1214 separately closed the partition-topology hypothesis with 18 decoded
  stream pairs (2,160 frames, zero failures): live VBR rvp9 is already *coarser* than
  libvpx, the opposite of the earlier fixed-Q proxy.

That is real, well-evidenced elimination work and it deserves credit.

### 4.2 The gate is a deliberate carve-out, not an oversight

`block8_coef_rd_enabled` at `crates\rvp9-encoder\src\lib.rs:5641-5658` enables the
8×8 path only for CPU6 spatial components that are either **VBR two-layer ≤720p** or
**three-layer CBR**. The failing configuration — CPU6, VBR, **three-layer** — is
precisely the case it excludes. Commit `2003e3b3` (2026-08-15, "close CPU6 two-layer
spatial VBR") added the VBR disjunct to close CPU6/S2, which is exactly why the S2
curves are green and S3 red. `notes/divergence-ledger.md` records the governing
policy: *"widen only after the low-resolution single-layer and CPU7/8 corpus gates
show a Pareto improvement."*

So the conservatism is deliberate and documented. The problem is not that it exists —
it is where the bar was set.

### 4.3 The screening rule is stronger than the release gate — and it is what blocked progress

The release gate is **per-layer BD-rate ≤ +0.5%**. The pre-registered screen
(journal line 35057) is verbatim:

> Reject the change on any Ducks regression or failure to improve both red base-layer
> curves.

That is a **one-clip, unbounded veto** that fires on movement the release gate does
not penalise. Scoring each candidate by total points that layers exceed +0.5% — the
quantity the gate actually measures — using the journal's own recorded curves:

| Candidate | Aggregate excess over +0.5% | Δ vs baseline | Red layers | Colour flips |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| accepted baseline | 6.109623 | — | 5 | — |
| **Unit 1212** | **4.512030** | **−1.597593** | 5 | none |
| Unit 1213 | 6.234447 | +0.124824 | **4** | Old Town top **red → green** |
| **Unit 1216** | **4.787400** | **−1.322223** | 5 | none |
| Unit 1217 | 5.650465 | −0.459158 | 5 | none |

*(Arithmetic re-derived independently this pass from the journal's recorded curves.)*

- **Unit 1212 cut aggregate over-gate excess by 26.1% and pushed zero layers from
  green to red.** Its disqualifying "Ducks base regression" is
  `+0.196194 → +0.400090` — a movement that **never leaves the green zone** of a
  +0.5% gate.
- **Unit 1213 is the only candidate that cleared a red layer** (Old Town top,
  `+0.997766 → +0.462041`). It was rejected purely as "not a Pareto improvement",
  with no acknowledgement that a gate failure was eliminated.
- **Unit 1216's** three named regressions are +0.045 and +0.198 on layers already red,
  and +0.134 on a layer sitting 4 points inside green.

**Fair statement of what this does and does not mean.** None of these candidates
ships — all still leave 5 (or 4) layers failing the per-layer gate, exactly as the
baseline does. Aggregate excess is a *search-direction signal*, not the shipped gate.
But when you are five layers from green, a binary Pareto veto discards the gradient
you need to follow. That is what happened four times yesterday.

### 4.4 The hypothesis class is not exhausted

Unit 1217 asserts the four experiments "cover the coherent Block8, AcDc, and raw skip
combinations". Three orthogonal knobs are actually in play — Block8 RD on/off, AcDc
included/short-circuited, and raw-vs-forced skippability, the last being separately
meaningful at 8×8 and 16×16. The four runs sampled four cells of an ≥8-cell space,
and **the 8×8 raw-skip factor was never varied in isolation**. The untested
composition of Units 1212 and 1217 — Block8 RD on, AcDc shortcut preserved, raw skip
consumed — was never run. "Coherent" is doing the work of excluding those cells; it
is a post-hoc label, not a proof.

### 4.5 The gate was toggled, never conditioned

`block8_coef_rd_enabled` keys only on speed, RC mode, spatial component, three-layer
flag, and frame area — all frame/sequence-level. It carries **no Q, rate, frame-position,
reference-frame, or per-candidate term.** All three enabling experiments did the same
thing: relax the topology restriction so the predicate fires for *every* CPU6/S3 VBR
base-layer 8×8 candidate at every rate in every frame.

The codebase already has the missing idiom. `s3_q_gate_active` at
`crates\rvp9-core\src\mode_decision\inter.rs:447-449`, consumed by
`s3_domain_enabled_for_q` at `:595-599`, is a Q-conditioned gate on the sibling skip
policy. `disable_16x16_split_nonkey_enabled` (`lib.rs:5619+`) conditions on
`avg_inter_qindex > 208` and `current_video_frame > 8`. Neither pattern was applied to
`block8_coef_rd`.

And the journal's own data points at exactly that. Unit 1213 records the In To Tree
base-layer PSNR delta across 420/630/840/1260/2100/3360 kbps as
`-0.009124/-0.097781/-0.079656/-0.072514/-0.004597/+0.003730 dB` and reads it as
*"damage concentrated through the middle of the operating curve"*. Harm that is
near-zero at both endpoints and concentrated mid-curve is the signature of a policy
that **should be rate/Q-conditioned**. It was read as evidence the policy is wrong; it
is equally evidence the gate condition is too coarse. That reading was never tested.

### 4.6 Methodological gap worth naming

**No BD-rate variance, noise, or repeatability estimate is recorded anywhere in the
drive journal.** Every "noise floor" reference in the file concerns timing, not
BD-rate; no curve has been re-run to establish a discrimination threshold.
Differences of +0.045 points are therefore being treated as decisive without evidence
they exceed measurement resolution, while 1.3–1.6 points of aggregate improvement are
discarded. Separately, Unit 1216's curves are recorded to 4 decimal places where the
others use 6, so cross-unit comparison is not on uniform precision.

## 9. Conclusions

1. **The strategic conclusion in the journal is not supported by its own data.**
   Coefficient policy is recorded as "measured closed"; it is not. Four cells of an
   ≥8-cell space were sampled, one factor was never isolated, and the gate was toggled
   rather than conditioned.
2. **The screening rule, not the hypothesis space, is the binding constraint.** Three
   of four rejected candidates improved gate-relevant standing; one cleared a red
   layer. A one-clip unbounded veto discards the search gradient.
3. **The diagnostic work itself is excellent and should not be second-guessed.** The
   arithmetic exoneration and the partition-topology closure are solid, expensive
   results. The problem is downstream of the measurement.
4. **Maintenance closure has now stalled for six reports** while feature and
   diagnostic work continued. This is the persistent trend across every report since
   08-13, and it is widening rather than holding.
5. **The evidence base has a platform hole.** One approved toolchain, on the platform
   none of the Windows fleet boxes runs.
