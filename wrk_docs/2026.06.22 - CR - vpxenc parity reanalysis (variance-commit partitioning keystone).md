# 2026-06-22 - CR - vpxenc parity reanalysis: the variance-commit partitioning keystone

Commissioned by Arthur after the lever work-loop stalled at +6.43% BD-rate /
+1.16 dB: *"step back and commission a comprehensive reanalysis of how vpxenc
achieves its magic and how we get to that point. It must be possible for us to
get closer; we just haven't worked it out yet."*

Method: a 5-agent parallel deep-read of the **real libvpx C** (`third_party/libvpx/vp9/`)
vs the rvp9 implementation, plus a quantitative gap-attribution pass over the
existing block-level data, synthesized into this review. All code claims are

**Verdict up front: Arthur is right.** The remaining gap is *not* a fundamental
floor — it is a **coupled-architecture deficit with a concrete exit**. The
dominant, *currently-unattacked* root is the **partition architecture**: rvp9
computes libvpx's variance partition and then **throws it away**.

---

## 1. How vpxenc achieves its magic (ranked by contribution)

vpxenc at `--cpu-used=7` does not win by better RD *math* — rvp9 mirrors most of
the picker faithfully. It wins by a different **architecture**:

### 1a. Variance-commit partitioning — the keystone (~50–60% of the gap)
At speed ≥ 6 libvpx sets `VAR_BASED_PARTITION` (`vp9_speed_features.c:667`).
`choose_partitioning` (`vp9_encodeframe.c:1261`) builds a 64→32→16→8 **source-vs-
reference variance tree**; `set_vt_partitioning` takes NONE iff variance < a
threshold, else SPLIT. It is **q-aware but lambda-DECOUPLED** — the extra
partition/header bits of a finer split can *never veto it*. A 16×16 force-splits
to four 8×8 when raw variance exceeds `thresholds[2]` **or** the 8×8 minmax
variance is high (`vp9_encodeframe.c:1587-1613`). The thresholds scale with
`y_dequant[q][1]` (`set_vbp_thresholds:579-580`) and are q-gated (see §3).
The **consume** path (`nonrd_select_partition`, `vp9_encodeframe.c:5183-5232`)
**commits the variance leaf directly** and runs a *bounded local* RD refine only
where variance already voted SPLIT. This is what produces libvpx's 0→37% **8×8
growth with bitrate** (8×8 lands precisely on detail; 32×32-NONE stays on flat
regions and skips).

### 1b. Quality-stratified reference chain (~25–35%, grows with bitrate)
A sharp keyframe (`kf_boost = DEFAULT_KF_BOOST = 2000` → low-q floor ≈ q57) plus a
**periodic golden refresh + boost** every `baseline_gf_interval` with
`af_ratio = 10` (`vp9_ratectrl.c:2069-2116`) gives the GF a ~5–6× fat target and a
low q (~130) via `get_gf_active_quality`. Coasting inters then predict from a
reference ~70–100 qindex points *sharper than themselves* → small residual, high
skip. Net is a **sawtooth: invest, then coast cheap.**

### 1c. Co-calibrated residual / skip / tx (~10–20%)
One variance model keys `calculate_tx_size` (`vp9_pickmode.c:353-384`), reused at
emit; the `model_rd` AC/DC block-skip is honored as a *no-transform* skip
(`vp9_encodemb.c:590-596`); `vp9_quantize_fp` (flat, trellis off) runs on
survivors. tx_size and skip are decided **once** by the variance model the
quantizer is calibrated to → ~86 concentrated coded blocks, not 278 thin ones.

**The ranking matters:** 1b and 1c only pay off *because* 1a gives finer leaves
that can spend a fresh reference and concentrate residual.

## 2. Where rvp9's +1.16 dB / +6.43% gap lives (attribution)

| source | share | dB | bitrate signature |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Partitioning (the 16×16 lock)** | **50–60%** | ~0.5–0.65 | near-zero at low rate, **maximal at high rate** — owns the +1.16→+1.43 widening |
| Inter mode/MV (ZEROMV↑/NEWMV↓) | 30–40% booked, but **almost entirely downstream of the lock** | ~0.3–0.5 | the picker *math* alone is <0.1 dB (4 refuted lever families) |
| Residual/skip/tx | 10–15%, partly confounded w/ partitioning | ~0.3–0.45 | ~190 extra coded blocks/frame, ~1200–1900 wasted bits on a ~2088-bit budget |
| Reference/GOP | net-positive *potential* only | ~0.1–0.15 | **proven net-negative in isolation** (locked inters can't capitalize) |

**Dominant source: partitioning.** It is also the *root* that mis-calibrates the
other three: the 16×16 lock forces the ZEROMV/NEWMV inversion (at 16×16, NEWMV's
MV bits can't be justified; libvpx codes 8×8 where motion is coherent → NEWMV
pays, and 32×32 where flat → ZEROMV legitimately wins), forces the thin-residual
spread, and — via the q-gate (§3) — keeps q elevated.

## 3. Why ~28 isolated levers regressed — the coupling, now code-verified

Every refuted lever perturbed ONE term of an RD balance **co-calibrated against a
16×16-locked, uniform-q, degrading-reference substrate.** Three coupling
mechanisms, two newly verified in code:

1. **The partition floor is the root (verified).** rvp9 computes a faithful
   variance hint (`partition.rs:371 choose_partitioning`), but
   `variance_hint_bounds` **floors both min and max at `Block16x16`**
   (`nonrd_partition.rs:547-557`, with an explicit comment that all-8×8 at high q
   would defeat F.3), AND `p8_default_enabled` returns **false for VBR**
   (`lib.rs:2402-2404`). The RD descent then **replaces the hint entirely**
   (`lib.rs:3877 sb_partitions = new_leaves`). So **8×8 is architecturally
   unreachable on VBR inter frames** — 0% 8×8 is a *floor*, not an RD outcome.
   Every picker-term lever was tuned on top of this floor.

2. **The q-gate self-reinforces (the new finding, verified).** libvpx's
   `threshold[2]` is q-gated: q>220 → `base<<5`; q>200 → `base<<4`
   (`vp9_encodeframe.c:622-625`), and rvp9 ports this **exactly**
   (`partition.rs:320-329`). rvp9 runs foreman inters at **q≈236 (>220 →
   base<<5)**; libvpx runs them at **q≈205 (>200 → base<<4 = HALF the
   threshold)**. So rvp9's elevated q — caused by its degrading reference —
   **inflates its own split threshold**, keeping it at 16×16 → weaker prediction →
   q stays high. Partition size and reference-q are mutually reinforcing.

3. **The RD-descent re-litigates.** Unlike libvpx's variance-commit, rvp9's
   descent re-decides NONE-vs-SPLIT at every level under the high-q `rdmult`,
   where the partition-bit cost dominates the SPLIT delta → collapse to the 16×16
   hint (95.7%). Prior `P8=ON` experiments regressed precisely because they kept
   this RD gating — they got 8×8 *overhead* without variance *concentration*.

## 4. Roadmap — coherent bundles, in order B → C → A

Isolated levers fail; these are **bundles whose pieces must move together.**

### Bundle B — Variance-commit partitioning (the keystone) · LARGE · do first
- **Moves together:** stop the RD descent replacing the variance hint on VBR
  inter frames (`lib.rs:3877`); commit rvp9's already-computed
  `choose_partitioning` shape **directly**; lift the `Block16x16` floor
  (`nonrd_partition.rs:547-557`) **and** the VBR p8-off default (`lib.rs:2402-2404`)
  **together**; run a bounded local 8×8 RD refine **only where variance voted
  SPLIT** (mirror `nonrd_select_partition` else-branch + `:5211-5221`); co-adopt
  libvpx's lighter per-8×8-leaf mode set so the finer leaves stay inside the
  33 ms RT budget; **leave the picker's per-leaf RD math unchanged.**
- **Why it co-improves where isolated levers regressed:** it changes the *input
  distribution* the whole picker sees. At an 8×8 leaf NEWMV becomes affordable; at
  32×32 ZEROMV legitimately wins — both correct **without touching** the picker
  constants the isolated levers regressed on. It also breaks the q-gate loop
  (finer leaves → better prediction → lower q → smaller threshold).
- **Predicted:** matched-Δ +1.16 → **~+0.6–0.8 dB**; targets the high-rate
  (BPM=2.0, +1.43) case most; the 250 kbps point (33.19 vs 36.75) moves most;
  raises achieved bitrate (closes the undershoot).
- **Risk:** the floor was added deliberately (would defeat F.3); per-8×8 picker
  cost may blow the RT budget unless the lighter mode set lands with it.
- **Validation (curve-shift-safe):** 4-point VBR foreman gate reading **both
  matched-Δ AND achieved bitrate** — finer partitioning must *increase* rvp9's
  achieved bitrate (close the undershoot), not just shift the curve.

### Bundle C — Residual/skip/tx co-port · MEDIUM
- **Moves together:** reuse the picker's `calculate_tx_size` as `mi.tx_size`,
  delete `choose_inter_luma_tx_size` from emit (`lib.rs:5516-5541, 6901-7099`);
  plumb the `model_rd_for_sb_y` AC/DC skip into a **real** per-block plane-0 skip
  (mirror `vp9_encodemb.c:590-596`) replacing the diagnostic gate (`lib.rs:5504`);
  switch the inter survivor quantizer `vp9_quantize_b → vp9_quantize_fp`
  (`lib.rs:6438-6464`). All three move together because `ac_thr` is derived for
  the fp quantizer (`ac_thr = zbin² >> 6`); block-skip without `quantize_fp`
  double-counts the deadzone — *exactly the regression family seen restoring
  libvpx skip terms in isolation.*
- **Predicted:** 278 thin blocks → ~86 concentrated; skip 69% → ~86%; ~0.3–0.45
  dB. **Landing after B+C: ~+3 to +4% BD-rate** (from +6.43%).
- **Risk:** `quantize_fp` changes recon → `cross_decoder_gate` must stay green
  (the main hazard). The AcDc model is ~23% wrong on Y (`inter.rs:2807`), so skip
  won't move monotonically.

### Bundle A — Reference cadence as a system · MULTI-SESSION · do last
- **Moves together:** restore the pruned VBR GF rate branch (`vbr.rs:33-41`): a
  `FrameKind::Golden` that stops collapsing to `Inter`
  (`frame_loop.rs:1649-1652`), a boosted GF target + lower active-best via
  `get_gf_active_quality` (`gf_boost ≈ 2000`); make the picker actively **prefer
  the fresher golden** for coasting frames (`bias_golden`/`frames_since_golden`,
  `inter.rs:1057-1066`); and **pay for it by taking bits from coasting frames**
  (rebalance, GOP bit-sum constant). Fix the `KF_ACTIVE_QUALITY_BOOST=32 < KF_LOW=400`
  bug (`cbr.rs:387/376`) too.
- **Why it now works (when isolation didn't):** prior experiments *added*
  reference bits on top of an unchanged inter budget, starving the already-
  undershooting coast frames, AND the 16×16-locked inters couldn't exploit a
  fresher reference. **After B+C the inters CAN capitalize** — invest creates a
  sharper reference, the picker routes coasting prediction to it, and the
  rebalance makes coast frames *cheaper*, not starved.
- **Predicted:** ~+0.1–0.15 dB **once inters exploit it** — the piece that takes
  +3–4% down toward **+2%**. Grows with bitrate.
- **Validation:** 4-point gate tracking the GOP **bit-SUM stays constant** (so you
  measure the sawtooth, not added bits); confirm coasting-frame avg q falls toward
  205.

## 5. Is ≤ +2% reachable? (honest)

**Yes — plausibly, with MEDIUM confidence, ONLY via B → C → A** (a 2–3 bundle,
multi-session program). It is almost certainly NOT reachable by any isolated
lever (28 refuted) nor by reference allocation first (proven net-negative).

- Realistic landing after **B+C: ~+3 to +4%** (from +6.43%); **+2% needs A's
  sawtooth** contributing once inters can exploit a fresh reference.
- What keeps it MEDIUM not HIGH: (1) the gap is coupled — even a perfect partition
  fix may leave residual gap until A lands, and A's payoff post-B+C is unverified;
  (2) per-8×8 picker cost is real (`lib.rs:2618-2634` notes the 8×8 inter picker
  blows the 33 ms RT budget) so B must co-adopt the lighter mode set or be
  correct-but-slow; (3) the AcDc skip model is ~23% wrong (`inter.rs:2807`).

## 6. Recommended first step — the variance-shape-DIRECT A/B (never run)

On VBR inter frames, **bypass the RD NONE-vs-SPLIT shape re-decision entirely** and
commit rvp9's already-computed `choose_partitioning` hint as the partition shape,
with the `Block16x16` floor (`nonrd_partition.rs:547-557`) and the VBR p8-off
default (`lib.rs:2402-2404`) **lifted together**, calling the existing faithful
picker once per variance-chosen leaf (mirror `nonrd_select_partition`'s
else-branch commit + bounded 8×8 refine only where variance voted SPLIT).

Why highest-leverage: it is the ROOT the q-gate traces back to and the unique
owner of the bitrate-growth fingerprint (the dominant ~50–60% share); all prior
8×8 experiments kept the RD gating and so **tested the wrong thing**; it is
*minimal new code* (the variance partition is already computed — the change is to
**stop overriding it**) for *maximal structural change*; and it is
decoder-byte-parity-neutral.

**Cheapest pre-flight (decisive about the keystone):** dump `hint.cells` *before*
`variance_hint_bounds` at foreman q236 and histogram. If the hint is already a
healthy 8×8 mix → the floor is the whole story and Bundle B will move the metric.
If the hint is itself 16×16-dominant (because the `base<<5` q-boost already
suppressed it) → the q-gate coupling must be addressed too (fold the reference-q
feedback into `for_inter` at `partition.rs:320-329`), confirming B and A must
eventually co-move. **Either outcome is decisive.**
