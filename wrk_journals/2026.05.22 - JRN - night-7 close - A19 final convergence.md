## Why Q saturates on foreman

The rate controller is pushing Q to 255 on inter frames because
foreman has continuous motion that the picker can't model well
(no ALTREF, no proper RDO, no multi-pass bit budget). The picker emits
many residual-heavy modes, the rate controller drives Q up to meet the
target bitrate, and at high Q the residual quantizes to mostly-zeros —
which the picker sees as cheap and re-selects. Feedback loop.

The architectural fix is to put bits where they matter (ALTREF temporal
filtering, multi-pass lookahead) or to improve the picker so it doesn't
demand bits the budget can't supply.

Both are out of v1 scope per `PLAN.md`.
