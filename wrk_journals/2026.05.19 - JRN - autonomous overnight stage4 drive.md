## Decision log

### Decision A: BmiInfo `as_mode` field reuse (no struct change)

`BmiInfo.as_mode: u8` is currently doc'd for intra sub-blocks. For
inter sub-8, we'll reuse the same byte to carry `InterMode::idx()`.
Parent's `BlockMode` discriminant tells the consumer which
interpretation to use. No struct change; writer's contract is just
"caller supplies `bmi[j].as_mode = mode.idx()` for sub-8 inter
leaves."

Rationale: extending BmiInfo would touch every BmiInfo construction
in the codebase, mostly for no benefit since the discriminant is
already implicit in the parent's BlockMode.

Decoder-side symmetry (also populating `as_mode` for inter sub-8
during `resolve_sub8_bmi`) is desirable but out of scope for the
overnight drive — encoder side moves first; decoder-side polish can
follow.

### Decision B: Drive ordering for the overnight session

Picked the routine dispatches first; encoder-integration architectural
calls saved for sync with Arthur:

1. **S4.16e** — sub-8 inter ModeInfo writer (this session's first
   dispatch). Extends `write_inter_block_mode_info` to handle
   Block4x4/4x8/8x4. Mirror of `resolve_sub8_bmi`. ~400-500 ll.
2. **S4.16f** — relax BLOCK_8x8 partition pin in the inter walker
   so sub-8 leaves become reachable from `write_tile_inter`.
   Small follow-up to S4.16e. ~50-100 ll.
3. **S4.16g** — coef integration into inter leaves (relax
   `skip=true` invariant). Caller supplies per-TX-block raw coefs
   + eob; writer dispatches `encode_coefs`. ~300 ll.
4. **S4.17** — end-to-end test for an inter frame with non-skip
   blocks + non-zero residuals, through the real decoder.
5. After S4.17: pause for journal wrap-up. If Arthur hasn't woken
   up yet, start picking off encoder-integration tasks with
   libvpx-faithful defaults (mode-decision skeleton, fixed-Q
   quantizer from config).

Pace target: 5-10 commits before next pause.

### Next: S4.16g — coef integration into inter leaves

The `skip = true` invariant in `write_inter_block_leaf` and the
replay walker assert (`assert!(skip)`) need relaxing so the encoder
can emit non-zero residuals. Wire-mechanical:

1. Make `mi.skip` callable as `false`. When `!mi.skip`:
   - `tx_size` becomes select-eligible (`allow_select = !skip || !is_inter`)
   - After `write_inter_block_mode_info`, walk the leaf's TX blocks
     and call `encode_coefs` for each (luma + 2 chroma planes).
2. Need a coef provider — easiest is to extend `ModeInfo` with a
   coef-grid borrow OR pass a parallel coef-grid to `write_tile_inter`.

Architectural call needed: how to plumb per-TX-block coef arrays
into the writer without bloating ModeInfo or fragmenting the API.

**Decision C** (taking on Arthur's behalf for the autonomous drive):
add a parallel coef-grid parameter to `write_tile_inter`. The grid
is `Option<&InterCoefProvider>` where `InterCoefProvider` is a trait
the encoder driver implements (returns `(raw_coefs, eob)` per
`(mi_row, mi_col, plane, tx_idx)`). Keeps `ModeInfo` clean,
testable, and decouples wire-format from residual storage.

Rationale: ModeInfo is currently a single-frame data type; coef
data is much bigger and lives in its own pipeline. A trait
parameter is more flexible than a borrow (lets the encoder hold
coefs in any storage layout) and simpler than a struct field.

Smaller-bite alternative if the trait design proves too thorny:
just take `Option<&[(usize, usize, usize, usize, Vec<i32>, u16)]>`
(a flat slice of `(mi_row, mi_col, plane, tx_idx, coefs, eob)`).
Less ergonomic but mechanical to wire up.

Will reassess after reading the decoder's
`decode_and_reconstruct_inter_block` to understand the coef walk.
