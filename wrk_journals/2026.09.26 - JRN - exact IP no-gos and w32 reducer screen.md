# Exact-IP follow-up and 32-wide reducer screen

The post-zero-EOB Crucible profile was checked against its hash-matched PE/PDB before choosing another kernel. Town round-trip reconstruction's 122/1,160 self samples include 91 at one `if reconstruct` branch (`inter.rs:5533`); Tree has zero samples at that IP despite more round-trip tiles. Earlier exact scratch and Hadamard specializations were neutral/slower, so the branch spike is not a credible removable-time estimate. Ducks quantizer samples are in AVX2/EVEX code, not the scalar fallback; the remaining EOB tail is small after the integrated zero-EOB shortcuts. Tree quantize metrics' narrow-difference check has 38/1,195 Tree and 10/1,160 Town samples, but no exact invariant allows removal. These are no-go selections, not performance measurements.

The new bounded trial split the 32-wide generic variance reduction into two independent `(sum, sse)` chains. Its scalar differential failed deliberately with a wrong second-half source offset, then passed when restored. AArch64 focused variance tests, x86 cross-compilation, and 10 Crucible AVX2 focused tests passed. The paired native-release screen in `wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-26-w32-variance-two-chain-rejected/` kept all 36 exact-output encodes and measured candidate/control process CPU at Ducks 1.083032×, Tree 1.014132×, Town 1.015603×, equal-clip 1.037098×. Reject the code; the patch and raw evidence are archived. The latest integrated libvpx parity remains 1.134647×, needing about 7.5% less RVP9 CPU for the ≤1.05× target.

The subsequent exact-PE Ducks audit also found no new bounded picker or diamond change. Of 165 picker self samples, 128 map to the broad `inter.rs:2428` closure rather than TLS; the largest isolated IP has 26 samples at a denoiser-minimum stack reload, covered by the neutral denoiser-off prototype. The next isolated picker IPs have seven and six samples. The diamond symbol has 105 samples, but the 31 on `full_pixel.rs:665` spread over four required SAD-result/MV-cost paths. Earlier exact MV-cost, SAD4D, and denoiser probes were neutral or slower. Do not project a gain from these source-line counts. The next selection needs stronger operation-level evidence, perhaps a longer exact-image capture, not a repeat of those rejected kernels.

The rejected local and Crucible prototype worktrees were cleaned after the patch and raw data landed on `origin/main`; the main clone is clean. Profile self counts are ceilings, not gain estimates.

An Astra read-only second opinion found one distinct small hypothesis in `inter/mv_ref.rs:938`: if **all** realtime reference lists already contain two candidates after the direct and previous-frame pass, skip the entire cached-neighbour scaled-candidate pass at entry. Unit 1368 instead tested a per-neighbour early-exit predicate and regressed 2.3%; Unit 1392 later cached the lookups and gained 0.46%. The proposed entry guard is not proven profitable. Its 14 Ducks iterator samples are only 0.68% of the EXE sample set before accounting for entry-state frequency or branch cost. First collect that frequency; do not treat 14 samples as removable CPU. A differential must cover all-full, one-full, and neither-full lists, with a deliberately wrong `any-full` guard going red; preserve context, temporal references, `finish`, and clamping.

The higher-leverage next measurement is repeated **same 60-frame, same exact PE/PDB** sampled-stack captures to reach roughly 20–30k target-process samples per clip, plus a separate diagnostic work census by caller, block shape, and horizontal/vertical sub-pixel phase on RVP9 and pinned libvpx. Record output hashes and loss counts per run; do not use instrumented CPU as speed evidence. That split can tell whether the parity gap is extra evaluations, slower same-shape kernels, or surrounding search overhead. It is a measurement proposal, not a proven cause or a new parity number.
