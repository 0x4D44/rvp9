## Refuted diagnostics: fixed-Q header contamination and frame-parallel mode

Hypothesis: the fixed-Q residual and/or real-content curve might be driven by
simple header-mode deviations from vpxenc: loop-filter level, high-precision MV,
frame-context deltas, or frame-parallel decoding mode.

Validation:

- Generated 16-frame Foreman fixed-Q streams for rvp9 and libvpx at q80/q160,
  then inspected packet headers with the harness bitstream parser.
- Reused prior `RVP9_HIGHMV=1` Stage 4a run in
  `target/tmp/quality_realcontent_highmv.txt`.
- Temporarily env-gated a source probe `RVP9_FRAME_PARALLEL=1` that emitted
  `frame_parallel_decoding_mode = true` on visible inter frames and skipped the
  encoder's post-frame backward-adaptation mirror. The source probe compiled
  with `mdtimeout 180s cargo check -p rvp9-encoder --lib`, was measured with
  `mdtimeout 1200s cargo test -p rvp9-encoder --release --test quality_gate quality_gate_stage4a_realcontent -- --ignored --nocapture`,
  and was then backed out.

Result:

- The loop-filter Q formula is not the fixed-Q root cause. On same-Q inter
  frames rvp9 and libvpx pick the same LPF-Q levels: q80 inter frames use LF 11,
  and q160 inter frames use LF 28. The earlier Stage-SSE post-LF loss is a real
  local symptom, but not because the default LPF-Q formula is obviously wrong.
- The E9 "fixed-Q" comparison is not a pure equal-Q/reference decomposition.
  At rvp9 q80, rvp9 writes the keyframe at `base_qindex=80`/LF7 and inter frames
  at q80/LF11. Libvpx `--end-usage=q --cq-level=20` writes the keyframe at
  q15/LF2, then inter packets at q80/q67/q54/q37 with matching LF changes. At
  rvp9 q160, libvpx similarly uses a q70 keyframe plus inter q160/q151/q140/q121
  rather than a flat q160 chain.
- Header differences are real but not individually promotable: libvpx also sets
  high-precision MV, LF deltas, and frame-parallel mode where rvp9 currently
  does not. Prior `RVP9_HIGHMV=1` was effectively neutral on the primary metric
  (`+1.16 dB` mean, BD-rate `+6.53%`) and slower.
- The simple frame-parallel source probe was a hard regression. Its rvp9 curve was
  `62.5/26.64`, `63.7/26.90`, `70.6/27.39`, `173.8/32.64`; BD-rate worsened to
  `+159.00%`, and mean matched-bitrate delta worsened to `+2.30 dB`. The probe
  was backed out.
- Conclusion: do not pursue LPF-Q, HIGHMV, or frame-parallel as one-switch
  parity fixes. The fixed-Q residual is materially contaminated by libvpx's
  q-mode keyframe/reference Q choices; the remaining real objective is still
  coding-efficiency / curve-shape work that improves PSNR per bit instead of
  masking the gap through RC shape.
