# Unit 1408 — variance 16x16 inline rejection

The fresh Crucible profile on integrated trunk `9e882a46` put
`var_sum_sse_16x16_inner` at 4.213347% of weighted x86-64 encoder samples.
The bounded candidate was a legal plain `#[inline]` annotation; Rust 1.95
rejects `#[inline(always)]` on a `#[target_feature]` function, so the stronger
form was not a valid experiment.

The candidate passed the focused Crucible variance tests. A deliberate
one-row mutation compiled and failed `differential_random_all_sizes` at 16x16,
confirming the red oracle. The paired 1080p Crucible screen then produced a
corpus ratio of 1.002275x (candidate/control), with matching IVF hashes. That
is a small regression, not a gain, so the source annotation was removed and
the candidate was not integrated.

The profile points the next search toward the picker, quantizer, SAD4D, and
motion-search paths. The full profile and screen evidence live under
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-22-unit1408-*`.
