**What `EncoderConfig::aq_mode = AqMode::CyclicRefresh` does (verified end-to-end):**
1. Segmentation enabled in uncompressed header with `update_map=true`, `temporal_update=false`, `update_data=true`, `abs_delta=false`, `feature_data[1][SEG_LVL_ALT_Q] = Some(segment1_q_delta)`.
2. Pre-encode of each inter frame: `CyclicRefresh::pre_encode_pick_segments` produces seg map; ~10% of SBs marked segment 1.
3. Per-leaf `segment_id` projected from seg map (top-left mi cell).
4. Forward quant uses `effective_qindex_for_segment(base_q, segment_id, seg_q_deltas)` per leaf.
5. Inter writer emits `SegmentationWriteParams` driving per-leaf segment_id bits.
6. Decoder reads + applies per-segment Q-delta on dequant; encoder↔decoder reconstruction stays BIT-EXACT.
7. Post-encode: `CyclicRefresh::post_encode_update` ages baseline cells, resets refreshed cells to age=0 (skip-coded cells excluded).
8. Keyframes reset the cyclic refresh state (libvpx-faithful).
