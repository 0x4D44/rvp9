## F8.0-fix oracle result

8×8 vertical stripes at q=40 under block_yrd:
- DC: rd=42754 dist=282 rate=462
- **V : rd=28723 dist=0 rate=1993** ← winner
- H : rd=60859 dist=297 rate=1585

Old picker (post-recon SSE) gave DC = winner because reconstruction collapsed all modes to dist=0. New picker (block_yrd) correctly distinguishes modes in coefficient domain.

Shift formula from libvpx vp9_rdopt.c:574: `shift = (tx_size == TX_32X32) ? 0 : 2`.
