- Falsified paths: raw variance DIRECT, DIRECT+Bundle-C, standalone Bundle-C, blanket NEWMV diff-bias disable, small-leaf NEWMV pruning, S3/q-gated S3 alone, VBP force-split, live partition cost, A4 rdmult-only, GF-RC/keyframe spend/q-only spend, and global BPM-only tuning.
- Remaining diagnosis concentrates on mode/partition/TX efficiency, not rate control alone. At the promoted baseline, rvp9 still has far less 8x8 and far more 16x16 than libvpx, plus different ZEROMV/skip mix.
- The cleanest next design surface is A4 split admission in `crates/rvp9-core/src/mode_decision/nonrd_partition.rs`: the split failure run showed `Block16x16 -> 4x Block8x8` attempts failing by `partial_rd_early_out`, not child infeasibility; feasible splits beat NONE but many are later displaced by RECT. Rect-disable is harmful, so the design must complete/reprice split comparisons without suppressing rect.
- Coupling constraints: avoid new permanent env flags; keep promoted `stage4a_vbr()` as the baseline; do not let descent dry-run decisions become final emit decisions without respecting causal neighbor reconstruction; keep skip picker logic and emit-side zero-EOB override distinct unless intentionally replacing both; use block-stat and F.0.2-style oracles to prevent "better scalar metric, worse mechanism" outcomes.
