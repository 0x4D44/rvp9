## The critical finding

**A libvpx-matched keyframe does NOT close the gap — it makes BD-rate
numerically worse.** The KF fix raised PSNR (good) but rvp9 **still
overshoots the inter bitrate ~2.5×** (100k target → 252 kbps vs libvpx
143). A higher-PSNR curve sitting at 2.5× the bitrate scores worse on
BD-rate, and rvp9's 4 measured points land at the wrong (high) bitrates,
giving a narrow, unfair overlap with libvpx's curve.

The keyframe lever is **necessary** (restores measurability; preserves a
real −20% inter-bit reduction) but **not sufficient**.
