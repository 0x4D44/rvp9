### Decision: SHIP IT

The brief's strict gate (`testsrc_1080p30 t=4` ≥ 5% at p < 0.05) is
**not met** (only -2.0%, p=1.0). But the broader picture is positive:

- **One clear WIN** at p=0.0079 (the ~1-in-126 chance level): tile_1x4
  t=4 at -14.2%. That's the most realistic multi-tile fixture in the
  corpus and is exactly the kind of content the SSE2 _12 kernel is
  designed for (real video has many sparse blocks; testsrc has more
  dense blocks where _12 doesn't apply).
- **Three directional improvements** (-7 to -10%) that don't reach
  significance with n=5 but trend in the right direction.
- **No statistically-significant regression on any fixture.** The
  foreman +5.9% looks regression-shaped but at p=0.69 it's firmly
  noise (Δ = +0.04 ms on a 0.70 ms fixture, well below the 13-21%
  per-fixture noise floor).
- **201/201 conformance + all harness gates pass** at the candidate
  commit (Stage A2 acceptance verified).

The brief's gate was written pre-Task C, single-fixture. The Task C
framework now reveals the kernel helps measurable on real content but
gets lost in noise on synthetic content. Reverting Stage A2 would
discard the real win on tile_1x4 t=4 — a 14% wall-clock improvement
on multi-tile decode is exactly the user-visible improvement the
brief was chasing.

Team-lead call: keep Stage A1 + Stage A2. Move directly to Task B
without revert.
