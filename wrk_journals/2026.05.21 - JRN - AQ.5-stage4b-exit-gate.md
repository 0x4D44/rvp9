## What landed

### Deliverable 1 — Four-curve BD-rate harness

`crates/rvp9-encoder/tests/bdrate_gate.rs::bdrate_gate_stage4b_aq` — a
new `#[ignore]`-gated integration test that:

1. Reads `tests/corpus/testsrc_720p30.y4m` (1280×720@30fps natural
   content), parses the y4m header, and centre-crops to **256×256**
   (a 64-multiple inside the encoder's accepted range). Falls back to
   an in-process "textured-foreground" synthetic source if y4m is
   missing.
2. Builds four RD curves at target bitrates `[200, 400, 800, 1600]`
   kbps:
   - Curve 1 — rvp9 `AqMode::Off`
   - Curve 2 — rvp9 `AqMode::CyclicRefresh`
   - Curve 3 — libvpx `--aq-mode=0`
   - Curve 4 — libvpx `--aq-mode=3`
3. Computes the AQ-on gain on each side via `bdrate(off, on)`. Negative
   = AQ saves bits at matched PSNR; positive = AQ costs bits.
4. Computes `delta = gain_rvp9 - gain_libvpx`.
5. Reports `PASS` only if both gains are finite AND `|delta| ≤ 0.3 %`;
   otherwise prints the honest `FAIL` or `NOT MEASURABLE` verdict with
   the failure reason.

## Honest assessment — what this measures and what it doesn't

### Infrastructure: GREEN

- All four curves produced.
- libvpx leg has a sensible PSNR-vs-bitrate shape (54 → 59 dB) on
  matched config.
- BD-rate computation runs on all four curves and returns finite
  numbers — no overflow, no NaN-from-no-overlap.
- y4m reader successfully parses our 1280×720 natural-content
  corpus, centre-crops to 256×256, and feeds both encoders bit-
  identical input data.
- vpxenc / vpxdec round-trip works at `--aq-mode=0` AND
  `--aq-mode=3`, confirming the libvpx CLI flag plumbing is
  correct.

### Literal 0.3 % gate: NOT MEASURABLE in this regime

The measured `delta = +15.0 %` is **not a real BD-rate gain delta**;
it's the artefact of an underlying rate-control issue that prevents
either side from producing a clean RD sweep:

1. **rvp9's rate controller saturates the bitrate well below target.**
   At target 1600 kbps the rvp9 encoder produces only ~39 kbps. The
   actual bitrate plateaus around 32–43 kbps regardless of target.
   This is the same pattern F12.0 documented at 128×128 (plateau ~20
   kbps regardless of 250–2000 kbps target). At 256×256 with richer
   content the plateau is slightly higher but the same shape.
2. **rvp9's PSNR is non-monotonic across target bitrate** (max drop
   2.71 dB). With actual-bitrate clamped near a single value, PSNR
   variation is noise: the rate controller picks a slightly
   different q-index per frame, and the differential AQ vs no-AQ
   reaches across that noise.
3. **libvpx saturates around 130 kbps even with 1600 kbps target.**
   This is libvpx's `--rt --cpu-used=7` real-time mode quality
   ceiling for 8 frames of this content — not enough material to
   warm up the encoder. libvpx's PSNR rises sensibly but the upper
   half of the sweep is degenerate.
4. **The "AQ gains" are noise-domain.** rvp9 +16.2 % means cyclic
   refresh slightly inflates the bits-vs-bits comparison at this
   content/sequence-length, but doesn't reflect a real BD-rate
   delta. Real natural-content libvpx AQ-3 gains are typically
   -3 to -8 % on screen content over 30+ frames at proper
   operating bitrate. We're three orders of magnitude away from
   that operating point.

The 8-frame sequence and rvp9's still-developing rate controller
make a literal 0.3 % BD-rate verdict **impossible**, not just
inconclusive. Two compounding gaps drive this:

| Gap | Owned by | Stage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| F9.0 rate controller can't push q-index high enough to consume targeted bitrate | encoder team | Stage 4b rate-control hardening (current) |
| 8-frame sequence too short for libvpx to reach its operating point | harness | Stage 5 corpus + long-sequence runs |

### Stage 4b literal exit-gate verdict

**NOT MEASURABLE** at AQ.5. Deferred to Stage 5 alongside:

- A real-content corpus loop ≥ 240 frames (8 s at 30 fps).
- A rate-control responsiveness fix so rvp9's actual bitrate
  tracks target across the full operating range.
- Cross-decoder verification (vpxdec / ffmpeg) on rvp9 output.
- A frame-time oracle (720p30 RT verification — Stage 4b's
  second exit-gate criterion, also still NOT MEASURED).

The **infrastructure** to compute the measurement once those gaps
close is now GREEN.

## Open punch-list (Stage 5)

In rough priority order:

1. **Rate controller q-index range fix.** Investigate why rvp9
   actual bitrate plateaus 5-40× below target. Suspect: the
   `pic_norm`-correction-factor + fixed-shift hand-off underestimates
   how many bits a frame at our coded-surface widths consumes, so
   the controller never lowers q far enough to spend the target.
2. **Long-sequence corpus harness.** 240-frame sweeps from at least
   two distinct y4m sources at 256×256 or 512×512. Per-frame stats
   accumulated. Re-run the four-curve gate.
3. **720p30 real-time oracle.** Median frame-time over a multi-
   frame run, asserted ≤ 33 ms. PLAN.md Stage 4b exit gate #1.
4. **Cross-decoder verification.** Pipe rvp9-encoder IVF output to
   `vpxdec` and `ffmpeg -c:v vp9`; assert exit 0 and PSNR parity.
5. **BD-rate cubic-polynomial fit.** Once the regime is non-
   degenerate, the trapezoidal ±0.5 pp uncertainty likely matters
   at the 0.3 % gate. Port the cubic fit (no new crate dependency).
