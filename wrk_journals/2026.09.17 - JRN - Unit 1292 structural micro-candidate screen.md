# Unit 1292 — reject two structural micro-candidates

This unit screened two output-exact cleanups against the integrated trunk
`ffb19b504b08a8ae3307931ff0f71640cdd526bb`. Neither clears the established
one-percent whole-encode retention bar, so neither source commit is retained.

## Candidates and focused evidence

### Lazy inverse-IDCT dispatch

Candidate commit: `159f25eba21d28b3df6c6c6f6ba896ff360db7f1`.

`reconstruct_via_round_trip` now resolves `RoundTripIdctDispatch` only when
`RoundTripOptions.reconstruct` is true. Hadamard-only realtime candidates still
collect coefficient metrics but do not reconstruct, so this removes three dead
function-pointer resolutions without changing any transform kernel.

The lead reran the three hadamard/dispatch tests and the exact-zero
reconstruction test. All four requested focused tests, the restored-path test,
formatting, and diff hygiene passed in the candidate worktree. The worker's
deliberate inverted-condition mutation failed the reconstruction dispatch
invariant test before restoration.

### Reuse absolute diamond-search MVs

Candidate commit: `d59dd7e46ca93033155be5ec80b0b6be23942a78`.

The all-in diamond path already materializes absolute candidate MVs for its
batched SAD lanes. The candidate reuses those coordinates for MV-cost scoring
and the selected-position update, preserving lane order, strict `<` ties, SAD
values, and the clipped slow path.

The full-pixel diamond tests (7 tests) and the worker's x86/AArch64 SAD4D
differentials passed. Deliberately changing the selected row made the small
shift test fail before restoration.

release targets with Rust/Cargo 1.95.0. The control was the integrated trunk;
each candidate was measured separately. The protocol was one warmup, 15
alternating pairs, two reversed clip-order rotations, three canonical 720p30
clips, CPU-used 6, CBR 1260 kbit/s, one thread, no tiles, 60 frames and a
60-frame keyframe interval. No strict load ceiling was applied, consistent with
the current relaxed Crucible policy.

## Disposition

Do not spend a production-PGO build on either candidate. Their plain-release
whole-encode results are too small for the retention bar, and the diamond result
is visibly order-sensitive. Keep the evidence, leave the integrated trunk
unchanged, and select the next candidate from a larger measured mode/partition
cost rather than another dispatch or coordinate micro-cleanup.
