# Keep GOP cadence past counter saturation — KILN-00072

The queue selected KILN-00072 and the task claimed it before inspection. `StreamEncoder` uses its saturating `u32` frame total for three authoritative GOP queries: frame kind/rate-control class/ALTREF disposition, GOLDEN picker behavior, and emitted refresh flags. `SpatialEncoder` independently uses its saturating access-unit total for aligned keyframe decisions. Saturation therefore freezes the schedule or repeats a boundary indefinitely.

A bounded independent assessment and the lead's source review selected private GOP-relative phase counters. They start at zero, cycle within each validated positive interval, and advance only at the existing successful frame/access-unit completion sites. All three ordinary schedule queries and the spatial key decision must use those phases. `GfGroup` and public counter interfaces remain unchanged. Public totals retain their existing cap at `u32::MAX`; other consumers of those diagnostic totals, such as chessboard parity and oracle labels, are outside this bug's GOP/key/GOLDEN/ALTREF scope.

Regression fixtures first encode a real initial keyframe, then seed the existing total near `u32::MAX` at an ordinal congruent to one modulo the GOP. This keeps the eventual private phase naturally aligned without introducing new test-only state setters. Actual packets/access units across saturation are compared with a `u64` ordinal oracle for GOP 10 (a non-divisor) and 15 (a divisor of `u32::MAX`). These are isolated cadence-boundary tests with initialized references, not a multi-year soak or a claim about every long-run counter.

The tests-only pre-fix run in `.scratch/kiln-00072-fails-before-assertion-red-v2.log` selected four tests and all four failed at the intended cadence assertions: GOP 10 emitted GOLDEN refresh mask `0x01` instead of `0x03` at offset 8; GOP 15 emitted KEY at offset 1; the delayed ALTREF fixture also emitted KEY at offset 1; and the combined GOP 10/15 spatial header assertions disagreed with the access-unit cadence. The lead read the complete log. An earlier compile failure and an earlier ALTREF startup-assumption failure are retained separately and are not counted as regression proof.

The lead independently observed all four boundary tests passing after reviewing the final production changes. Existing integration targets also passed: `w_gop_topology` 2, `w_altref_real_coding` 2, and `w_svc_temporal` 3. Four external/corpus-dependent ignored tests were not run or counted as passes. The normal GOP byte fixture retained its pre-change length and hash. Formatting, scoped encoder-library Clippy and `git diff --check` passed with no new warnings.
