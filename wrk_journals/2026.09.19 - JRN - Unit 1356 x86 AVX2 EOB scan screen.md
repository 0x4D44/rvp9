# Unit 1356 — x86 AVX2 quantizer EOB scan screen

Date: 2026-09-19
Branch: `task/20260919-TSK-HUM-x86-quantizer-avx2-eob-scan-screen`
Source commit: `8d079130`

## Question

Can the fixed-point AVX2 quantizer's final reverse scan be reduced without
changing EOB semantics or regressing the production-PGO encoder?

## Change

The quantizer already produces contiguous raster-order qcoeff.  The candidate
passes `ScanOrder::iscan()` through the x86 production wrapper and reduces
non-zero raster lanes to the maximum one-past scan position with AVX2.  A
16-entry scalar reverse probe handles dense blocks before the fixed-cost vector
reduction.  The 4x4 path and the direct scalar-compatible wrapper remain
unchanged.

## Evidence

The focused x86 release suite passed four tests.  The added vector-fallback
test deliberately places non-zero coefficients at scan positions 0 and n/2,
so the dense-tail probe cannot satisfy it.  Mutating the vector mask to zero
made that test fail (`eob=0` versus `33`); restoring the source made all four
tests pass.  The AArch64 NEON focused suite passed 10 tests and the x86 target
cross-check compiled cleanly apart from the existing x87 warning.

The synthetic ruler showed a mixed result: candidate dispatch was 19.1 ns for
8x8 versus 17.9 ns control, but 37.5 ns for 16x16 versus 38.7 ns control.
The full plain screen nevertheless measured 0.982025x paired geomean against
the integrated `ee1ab2dd` control.

The decisive production-PGO paired corpus ratios were 0.957064x, 0.951647x,
and 0.956785x across three repeated screens (geomean 0.955162x).  Each clip
had one stable output hash in every run.  Against pinned libvpx revision
`12f3a2ac`, the candidate measured 1.221054x paired geomean, a material
improvement over the integrated control's earlier ~1.4x comparison.

## Decision

Keep this branch as an integration-quality candidate.  The result is not a
parity finish: at 1.221x pinned libvpx, it needs roughly another 14% reduction
to reach the <=1.05x goal.  It does close a useful portion of the integrated
control's earlier ~1.44x gap, and this unit is a repeatable gain worth landing
after review.

Full matrices, hashes, command policy, and remote scratch locations are in
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-19-unit1356-eob-scan/summary.txt`.
