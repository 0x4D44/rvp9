## Objective

parity with pinned libvpx v1.14.1. Final gates are correctness, aggregate PSNR
BD-rate no worse than +0.5%, no important unexplained clip regression above
+2%, corpus geomean encode time no worse than 1.05x vpxenc, and the absolute
720p30/1080p30/1080p60 latency budgets.

## Rejected hypotheses to preserve

- Global integer-pel interpolation-filter shortcut: speed win, Foreman BD-rate
  regression.
- Track2 block-size diamond start and coefficient sign-bit cost: did not transfer
  to the current tree.
- High-precision MV, forced max TX, RECT disable, loop-filter disable, broad
  NEWMV masks, GF target boost, keyframe/GF extra spend, and raw q-only scaling:
  none produced a reproducible Pareto improvement.
- Do not use numerical BD-rate from non-overlapping or badly shaped curves as
  completion evidence.
