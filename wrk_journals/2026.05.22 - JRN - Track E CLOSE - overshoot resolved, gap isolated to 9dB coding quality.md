## The decisive E8 finding

The overshoot was a **feedback bug**, not the `active_worst_quality`
formula (which was faithful). `postencode_update` recorded
`active_min_qindex` (the active_best *floor*, ≈0.75·ambient) into the
`avg_frame_qindex[INTER]` EWMA instead of the coded Q. Geometric decay
cratered ambient 255→30 over 31 frames; since the buffer-ramp interpolates
`active_worst` UP *from ambient*, the collapsing ambient dragged the Q
ceiling down to ~190–211. `regulate_q` wanted a higher Q to hit target but
the artificially-low ceiling refused → 2.5× overshoot. libvpx records
`cm->base_qindex`. One-line fix: record `rc.last_picked_qindex`.

**Both diagnostic cases, in sequence:**
- **(A) clamp-was-limiting — CONFIRMED & FIXED.** Overshoot closed to 0.6%.
- **(B) structural quality floor — REVEALED.** At matched ~143 kbps, rvp9
  = **24.5 dB** vs libvpx **33.5 dB**: a **~9 dB per-bit coding-quality
  gap** in residual/transform/mode coding. No rate-control fix closes a
  matched-bitrate quality gap.

## Net result of Track E

**Four genuine libvpx divergences fixed**, all byte-exact, all landed:
1. E2 — inter variance-partition thresholds (was using KF formula).
2. E6 — keyframe target cap (`max_frame_bandwidth` 4×avg vs libvpx ~2 Mbit).
3. E7 — keyframe `active_best_quality` floor (was absent → KF Q crashed).
4. E8 — inter-Q EWMA fed the active_best floor, not the coded Q.

**The confounded +368% BD-rate is decomposed to its true root:** a clean,
measurable **~9 dB matched-bitrate per-bit coding-quality deficit.** This
is the "encoder efficiency" Arthur pointed at — now isolated and
quantified, not a confounded composite.

Foreman BD-rate is currently "not measurable" because the fixes moved
rvp9 to the correct bitrates, where its PSNR (24–27 dB) sits entirely
below libvpx's (28–37 dB) → no curve overlap. This is an honest
consequence of fixing the overshoot, and the BD-rate gate needs either a
matched-bitrate-PSNR fallback metric or the gap closed so curves overlap.

## What the ~9 dB gap IS (the next frontier)

Per-bit coding quality at matched bitrate = how much PSNR rvp9 extracts
per coded bit. The 9 dB deficit lives in:
- **Residual/transform coding** — transform-type selection, quantization
  decisions, coefficient scan/context efficiency.
- **Mode decision / RDO** — rvp9 uses a simplified picker vs libvpx's
  nonrd RD; better mode selection extracts more quality per bit.
- **Coefficient entropy coding** — context modelling efficiency.

This is a deeper class than rate-control tuning. It may hide tractable
E2-style faithful-port wins (e.g. `block_yrd` transform-domain RD,
nonrd-pickmode parity) OR be a multi-week RDO effort. A new diagnostic
(decompose the 9 dB across transform vs mode vs entropy) would scope it.
