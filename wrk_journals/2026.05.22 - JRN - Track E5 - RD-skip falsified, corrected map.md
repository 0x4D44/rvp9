## Why E4's "RD-skip is the lever" was wrong

E4 claimed rvp9 skips LESS than libvpx and that ~14k near-zero-residual
blocks are "blocks libvpx would skip outright." E5 falsified this:

- **rvp9 already skips MORE than libvpx (62% vs ~15%).** E4's libvpx skip
  figure came from the decoder mi-grid mode/skip columns — which E1 and
  E3 had ALREADY flagged untrustworthy (the decoder mi-grid mode-field
  population bug). E4 trusted a known-bad column.
- An RD skip test on a clip that already over-skips removes residuals
  that carry small but real PSNR → net-negative BD-rate on a clip where
  rvp9 is already RC-saturated and far below libvpx.

Lesson: the decoder mi-grid bug has now bitten two analyses (E1's mode
columns, E4's skip claim). Any future cross-encoder mode/skip comparison
must NOT use the decoder mi-grid until that bug is fixed.

## Corrected lever map (after E1–E5)

RULED OUT as the foreman closure lever:
- Motion comp / NEWMV (E3) — residuals already small; ZEROMV near-perfect.
- RD skip (E5) — rvp9 already over-skips; removing residuals regresses.
- Partition thresholds **alone** (E2) — landed +27 pp but hit the q=255
  ceiling where thresholds collapse.

LANDED & KEPT:
- E2 inter-VBP-threshold port (+27 pp, byte-exact). Correct, on critical
  path.

THE GAP IS A FEEDBACK LOOP:
```
bitrate overshoot → RC pins q at the active_worst ceiling (~245)
   → at high q the VBP partition thresholds collapse (split everything)
      → 85→77% 8×8 over-split → inflated coefficient bits (small-transform
        inefficiency + per-block context)  → overshoot  ↺
```
Coefficients are 67% of bits (E4, validated) — but that 67% is *inflated
by* the over-split, not independent of it. E1 (over-partition) and E4
(coef-dominant) are the same phenomenon seen from two angles.

## The one untested UPSTREAM lever: keyframe quality/boost (E1 #3)

rvp9 starves the keyframe: 13.7 kbit @ q=177 vs libvpx 74.5 kbit @ q=57
(5× more, much higher quality). A high-quality keyframe seeds a
high-quality LASTFRAME that the whole GOP predicts from. Mechanism that
could break the loop *at the source*: better LAST → even smaller inter
residuals → fewer coef bits → less overshoot → q not pinned → partition
thresholds (E2) actually work → fewer 8×8 → fewer bits.

Crucially this lever ADDS efficiency rather than TRADING quality for bits
— unlike RD-skip (E5) and the Q-clamp (E4 #2), both of which trade
quality and are now suspect after E5 showed quality-trading regresses
BD-rate on this clip.

Caveat (honest): inter residuals are already small (E3), and the KF cost
(+60 kbit) is large on a 64-frame budget — so the KF boost could help via
reference quality or could just eat budget. Untested. It is, however, the
most mechanistically promising remaining lever and the only one that
improves rather than trades efficiency.
