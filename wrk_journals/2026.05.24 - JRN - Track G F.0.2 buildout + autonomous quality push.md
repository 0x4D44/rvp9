### G.3 — diagnosis (CLOSED)

Findings written to `notes/G3-foreman-block-diff-findings.md`.

**Headline:** `find_mv_refs` returns `Mv::ZERO` for NEAREST 38 % of the
time on rvp9 vs 17 % on libvpx (100 kbps). For NEARMV: 27 % vs 0 %.
The NEAR gap is the cleanest signal — libvpx never produces (0,0)
NEARMVs; rvp9 produces them in 13-35 % of NEARMV picks across all four
bitrates.

This poisons motion compensation on ~16 % of inter blocks and feeds a
self-reinforcing cycle: zero NEAREST/NEAR → picker commits ZEROMV →
mi_grid populated with (0,0) → next block's `find_mv_refs` reads
those zeros → returns (0,0) → cycle continues.

Supporting evidence (all at 100 kbps):
- Over-partitioning: rvp9 64 % at 8×8 vs libvpx 41 %.
- NEWMV picked half as often (8 % vs 18 %).
- ZEROMV picked 3× as often (15 % vs 5 %).
- rvp9's NEWMV motion vectors are 6× larger mean magnitude (median
  34 vs 14, max 1610 vs 260) — independent diamond-search overshoot
  bug (F3, not the priority lever).
- rvp9 never picks GOLDEN (libvpx 0.6 %) — secondary cleanup item.
- Skip rate +7 pp on rvp9 (89 % vs 82 %).

### G.4 — picking the lever

Per the findings doc, L1: **when `find_mv_refs` returns
`mvs[0] == Mv::ZERO`, substitute the SB-level `int_pro` MV seed as the
NEAREST candidate** before the picker compares modes. This:

1. Injects a non-zero NEAREST when spatial+temporal salvage fails,
   so the picker has something useful to evaluate at NEAREST's low
   mode-rate cost.
2. Self-bootstraps the mi_grid out of the all-zero degenerate state.
3. Hooks into existing infrastructure — `int_pro` is already computed
   per-SB at `lib.rs:2654` and held in `sb_newmv_seed`.

Scope: ~50 LOC at `lib.rs::pick_and_simulate_inter_encode_partitions`'s
`build_mvc` callback. Risk: testsrc near-zero motion → int_pro
returns Mv::ZERO → substitution is a no-op there; low risk to the
testsrc halt threshold.

Implementation next.
