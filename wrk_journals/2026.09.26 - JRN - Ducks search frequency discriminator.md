# Ducks search frequency discriminator

The paired subpixel census showed Ducks had about 181,501 extra 16×16
fractional-search entries in RVP9 versus pinned libvpx. A read-only callsite
audit suggested more 16×16 trial work or more LAST/GOLDEN attempts, but could
not attribute calls from the existing counters. A coded-partition difference
was the cheapest hypothesis to test before adding instrumentation.

`e1_block_stats` decoded both already-hash-pinned 60-frame Ducks IVFs. RVP9
had 453,190 coded inter-frame 16×16 leaves; libvpx had 454,966. Therefore
more *final coded* 16×16 leaves do not explain RVP9's larger search count.
RVP9 emitted more NEWMV and GOLDEN area, but that is not a callsite count.
The JSON and command identities are in
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-26-ducks-search-frequency/`.

Next measure actual search entries by origin and reference after the picker
gates. Do not skip searches or tune partition policy from final bitstream
statistics alone: trial blocks and unsuccessful mode searches are invisible
there.
