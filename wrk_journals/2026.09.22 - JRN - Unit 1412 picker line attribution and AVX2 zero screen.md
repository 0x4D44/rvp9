# Unit 1412 — picker source lines and AVX2 zero-quantizer screen

The exact `cdbf7a6a` Crucible PDB resolves 94.48% of direct samples in the
picker callback. Its largest mapped callsite is
`crates/rvp9-encoder/src/lib.rs:11471`, `choose_inter_luma_tx_size`, with
1,224 of 4,797 callback samples (25.52%; 3.09% of all raw encoder samples).
Optimized inlining can charge an entire callee to that callsite, so this is a
target for a bounded experiment, not a measured saving. The input, executable,
PDB, ETL hashes and compact line aggregates are in
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-22-unit1412-profile/`.

Separately, the AVX2 fast quantizer's inclusive share suggested a safe
all-zero predicate, matching the existing AArch64 shortcut. The candidate
checks DC with its own parameters and tests every AC vector without writing
the quantizer outputs. Its focused x86 differential matches the full AVX2
quantizer at transform sizes 4/8/16, qindex edges, random and i32 extreme
coefficients. Deliberately treating every AC vector's first lane as DC made
that test fail; restoring the first-vector-only mask made it pass. The native
AArch64 quantizer and encoder scratch checks also pass.

Two complete relaxed-load Crucible screens kept all output IVF hashes equal.
Both suffered severe, asymmetric second-rotation stalls. Their aggregate
ratios of 0.895943× and 0.993877× therefore cannot establish a gain. The
clean first rotation in the repeat has a 1.000867× geometric mean of clip
medians, with two clips slower and one faster. A sub-percent, mixed result
does not meet the goal's reproducible-gain criterion, even under an "every
little helps" policy. The exact candidate patch and raw timing evidence are
archived in `wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-22-unit1412-quant-screen/`; the code
was not retained. The last direct RVP9/libvpx ratio remains Unit 1410's
1.227734×. The selector callsite remains the next bounded target, but this
unit makes no claim that removing its whole 3.09% share is possible.
