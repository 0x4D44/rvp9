# Stage 5 safety qualification

## Unit 1255 — release worker-scope borrows before completion

- The post-quality Miri sweep found a genuine Stacked Borrows violation in
  `WorkerPool::scope`, tracked as `RVP9-BUG-FLU-00028`. Each lifetime-erased
  worker wrapper retained the scope-local panic-list reference, sent its done
  message, and was only then destroyed by the worker loop. The caller could
  receive every done message and deallocate the panic list while the last
  wrapper still strongly protected that shared reference.
- `WorkerJob` now owns the erased closure and completion sender separately.
  `WorkerJob::execute` consumes and drops the closure under `catch_unwind`
  before sending completion. Caller-participating and queued jobs use the same
  ordering, preserving panic propagation and the unconditional join contract.
- Controlled-red evidence: a new 256-scope reuse regression deterministically
  aborted under Miri before the fix. It is green afterward. The complete
  tile-threading Miri suite passes 23/23, and the native tile-threading suite
  passes 23/23. Focused warnings-denied Clippy, rustfmt, and diff hygiene are
  green.
- Exact next work: commit this safety unit, finish the already-green
  motion-search scalar-Miri dispatch unit, then run the remaining
  mode-decision Miri surface before starting bounded fuzz and soak evidence.

## Unit 1262 — security-review decoder hardening

- A fresh Opus 5 security review found one reachable panic and two unsafe
  scratch-buffer patterns worth removing before release. A legal 64-pixel-high
  frame with two tile rows gives the first row an empty `[0, 0)` mi range, but
  `TileDecodeCtx` rejected it with a positive-height assertion. The defect is
  tracked as `RVP9-BUG-FLU-00029`; its focused regression was observed failing
  on that assertion before the fix and now passes alongside a direct tile-bound
  geometry test.
- Legal empty tile rows now construct normally and zero-iterate like libvpx.
  Hadamard, inverse-transform, and motion-compensation scratch storage is
  initialized before safe references exist, removing the remaining
  `MaybeUninit` reference assumptions on those paths. Architecture-specific DSP
  implementation modules are crate-private; the public accelerated boundary
  exposes safe AArch64 wrappers and explicitly unsafe x86 AVX2 entry points
  with documented CPU-feature and slice-shape contracts.
- A compile-fail doctest was observed failing when the implementation module
  was temporarily public, then passing after privacy was restored. Native
  decoder tests, the 94 affected inter-mode tests, focused warnings-denied
  Clippy, rustfmt, and diff hygiene pass. The x86_64 Apple cross-target also
  passes warnings-denied Clippy for `rvp9-core` and `rvp9-bench`; test-only
  legacy IDCT kernels and AArch64-only test helpers are now compiled only where
  they are exercised.
- The new `fuzz_decode_frame` target parses a bounded raw VP9 packet and decodes
  every contained frame. Its five-minute AddressSanitizer campaign used seven
  official-vector seeds and completed 48,528 executions in 301 seconds,
  reaching 7,094 coverage edges and 37,745 feature edges with a 1,551-entry,
  27 MiB corpus. It produced no crash artifact or sanitizer report. Exact tool
  root: `$TMPDIR/rvp9-stage5-tools.IcyKnD`; corpus:
  `$TMPDIR/rvp9-fuzz-decode-corpus.IJHsEV`; artifacts:
  `$TMPDIR/rvp9-fuzz-decode-artifacts.GWJugV`.
- Exact next work: pass the full workspace test and warnings-denied lint gate,
  commit this hardening unit, then refresh official conformance and both
  cross-decoder matrices on the resulting exact commit.
