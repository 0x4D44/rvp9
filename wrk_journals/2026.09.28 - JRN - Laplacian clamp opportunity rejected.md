# Laplacian clamp opportunity rejected

The integrated profile and prior filter-stage census led to one new bounded hypothesis: avoid `model_rd_from_var_lapndz`'s variable divide when its quotient will clamp. The [native x86 count-only census](../wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-28-lapndz-saturation-no-go/README.md) found only 139,337 clamped calls among 2,713,714 nonzero Ducks calls (5.13%); Tree and Town reached 11.95% and 7.79%. Every canonical IVF was exact. A pre-divide multiplication and branch on every call is not justified by those hit rates, especially on the Ducks-weighted parity gap, so the diagnostic stays off main and no production-PGO candidate was built. The 1.057638× integrated ratio remains unchanged.
