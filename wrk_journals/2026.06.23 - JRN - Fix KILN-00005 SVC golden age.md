# 2026-06-23 - JRN - Fix KILN-00005 SVC golden age

## Pick

Selected `RVP9-BUG-KILN-00005` after KILN-00003. It was the remaining Medium-severity Open bug, localized to SVC/GOP state, and could be fixed unattended with a cheap unit regression. Left `RVP9-BUG-KILN-00011` Open for a later pass because it is lower severity and its notes explicitly require deciding whether the invariant or picker behavior is stale.

## Diagnosis

`encode_inter_internal()` increments `frames_since_golden_refresh`, computes the frame schedule from `GfGroup`, and then applies the temporal-SVC refresh override. The emitted `refresh_flags` are the authority for the reference pool and decoder state. However the age counter reset used the pre-override scheduled `GfUpdate` decision.

That explains the bug: at GOP offset 8, the schedule says `GfUpdate` and would refresh LAST+GOLDEN in single-layer mode. In the two-layer SVC pattern, that same display frame is an enhancement frame and emits `refresh_frame_flags = 0x00`, so GOLDEN is not refreshed. Resetting the counter there makes the picker treat stale GOLDEN as fresh.

The minimal fix is to reset the counter from the final emitted mask: only `refresh_flags & 0x02 != 0` means GOLDEN actually rotated.

## Regression Proof

Added `a18_temporal_svc_golden_age_follows_emitted_refresh_flags`. The test configures two-layer SVC, asserts GOP offset 8 is scheduled as a GOLDEN refresh, encodes through that offset, proves the frame is the SVC enhancement layer, and checks the GOLDEN age sequence.

## Fix

The reset predicate now checks the final emitted refresh mask, not the scheduled update type. The scheduled `GfUpdate` variable became dead and was removed.

Code-reviewer pass found one required test-strengthening item: explicitly assert that the test reaches a scheduled GOLDEN refresh. Applied that assertion before final validation.

Version bumped from `0.1.12` to `0.1.13` because this changes shipped encoder behavior.
