# Unit 1409 — SAD4D row striping rejection

The integrated 1080p Crucible profile placed
`vpx_sad16x16x4d_avx2` at 5.201894% of weighted RVP9 samples. Candidate
`7d66bf08` divided its sixteen rows into two independent accumulation chains
per reference. The hypothesis was that less dependency pressure might save a
small amount of whole-encode time.

The candidate passed all five native x86 SAD4D differential tests. Its new
row-parity test failed as expected when an isolated red build duplicated the
even row in place of each odd row. AArch64 DSP tests passed 88/88.

The exact paired Crucible screen measured `1.002600933x` candidate/control
across the three canonical 1080p clips. Every candidate/control IVF hash
matched. The source candidate and temporary test were removed; the final
branch carries only the screen and correctness evidence under
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-22-unit1409-screen-a/`.

The next profile-guided search should move to a different hotspot. The picker
callback, quantizer, and diamond search remain prominent in the fresh profile;
their recent rejected variants should be checked before choosing a new edit.
