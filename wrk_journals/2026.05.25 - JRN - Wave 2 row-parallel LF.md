### Topology choice

The plan §2 ideal is single-scope (LF workers run inside the same `worker_pool.scope` as tile decode workers, overlapping). I chose **two-scope** (tile decode joins, then a fresh `worker_pool.scope` runs LF) because:
1. The decoder's `frame_mi_grid` is `Vec<Option<ModeInfo>>`, but `setup_mask` needs `&[ModeInfo]`. Materialising the placeholder slab from `Option`-cells requires either (a) doing it after decode joins (which precludes overlap), or (b) doing it per-row inside the LF worker (which adds per-row allocation churn).
2. Two-scope still parallelises the LF kernel application — the heavy work — and should deliver the bulk of the predicted 5-10% gain. Decode-vs-LF overlap is a secondary gain (~600us startup-stall worth on a ~3500us LF phase, per Phase 3 HLD §3.8).
3. Two-scope is dramatically simpler to verify and reason about: each LF worker reads a fully-materialised `frame_mi` slab without race-on-write concerns.

If the measurement shows we need the overlap to hit the perf target, a follow-up commit can convert to single-scope by extending `build_lfm_row` to accept `&[Option<ModeInfo>]` directly.
