# Large-model RD skip-loop experiment

The last integrated direct production-PGO ratio is 1.054068× pinned libvpx equal-clip process CPU, just above the ≤1.05× target. An exact-image instruction profile showed `model_rd_for_sb_y_large` as a sizable Tree/Town symbol, but symbol share did not identify an avoidable operation. An opt-in diagnostic counted when both monotone skip tests had failed. It found 29.90% of Tree and 23.31% of Town TX-cell visits could be skipped, versus 3.25% of Ducks; raw shape histograms and exact IVF hashes are in [the census](../wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-27-large-model-rd-skip-loop-census/README.md). The diagnostic code is committed locally as `0de71b3a`, not integrated.

A clean one-file early-break prototype is committed locally as `78846baa`. A test-only full-loop oracle compared every model result field across 32×32/64×64 blocks and Tx8/Tx16/Tx32 grids, including positive first-cell termination; disabling the break deliberately failed the visit assertion. AArch64 and native x86 focused tests passed. Two exact paired production-PGO Crucible screens measured candidate/integrated-control process CPU at 1.028404× (5 pairs/clip) and 1.025152× (15 pairs/clip), slower on all three clips. The [PGO evidence](../wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-27-large-model-rd-early-exit-pgo/README.md) retains all 132 encodes, profiles, identities, and runner. The prototype must stay unpushed.

This rejects the tempting inference from avoidable cell counts to whole-encode speed. No direct libvpx screen was warranted for the slower candidate; integrated parity remains 1.054068×. The next parity experiment should seek an operation with measurable removable process CPU on Ducks or a cross-clip hot loop, and should price that operation before a full PGO build. Do not revisit this branch by reinterpreting the census alone.
