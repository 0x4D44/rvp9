## Honest residual + diminishing returns
The remaining ~2.54 dB at 1200k cpu7 mean now lives in:
- **CBR rate-control reclaiming SSE as bits** (TX-size RD shrinks SSE per block
  but the bits saved get spent on smaller-TX coef headers; the controller absorbs
  some of the gain at fixed-bitrate).
- Possibly **TX-type RD** (DCT-DCT vs DCT-ADST vs ADST-ADST per block) — libvpx
  selects per-block within a size; not verified for rvp9 yet.
- Possibly **inter mode-search depth / sub-pel refinement quality** — not
  audited beyond iter3's finding that lambda is libvpx-faithful.

Track-F has now done 8 iters yielding ~+0.74 dB total. The per-iter gain is
shrinking. Further iters need a fresh lever (TX-type RD is the cleanest
candidate by analogy) but are entering diminishing returns. Worth surfacing
to Arthur for a checkpoint decision.
