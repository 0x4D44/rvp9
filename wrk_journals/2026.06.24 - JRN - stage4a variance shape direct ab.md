## Next Fork

The remaining work is no longer a cheap scalar tweak. The evidence points to
the coupled partition architecture from
`wrk_docs/2026.06.22 - CR - vpxenc parity reanalysis (variance-commit partitioning keystone).md`,
but DIRECT proves the naive "commit raw variance shape" variant is wrong. The
next implementation choice is between:

- a bounded variance-commit bundle: commit variance structure only where it is
  rate-safe, with local refine / lighter mode rules, then validate bitrate
  growth, block stats, cross-decoder, and realtime; or
- RD-domain calibration first, then re-run the partition bundle once the picker
  can price fine partitions correctly.

That ordering is an architectural choice now, not a mechanical continuation.
