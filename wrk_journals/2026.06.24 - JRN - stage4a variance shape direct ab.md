## NEWMV Diff-Bias Gate Probe

The block stats showed rvp9 still overuses `ZEROMV` relative to libvpx, so I
checked whether rvp9's historical always-on port of `vp9_NEWMV_diff_bias` was
suppressing motion in the one-pass VBR product gate. libvpx wraps that bias in
a realtime-CBR caller gate; rvp9 now exposes `RVP9_NEWMV_DIFF_BIAS=0` as a
diagnostic switch while preserving the old default (`on`) because the A/B was
not promotable.

Bias-off quality gate (`target/tmp/stage4a_vbr_newmv_diff_bias_default_quality.txt`):

- rvp9 actual kbps / PSNR: 64.7 / 27.53, 47.4 / 26.88, 69.1 / 28.93,
  172.5 / 33.23.
- libvpx curve: 45.8 / 28.10, 72.1 / 29.87, 137.5 / 33.13, 288.1 / 36.75.
- BD-rate reported -20.26%, but the curve is non-monotonic at the low points
  and not a reliable win signal.
- Matched-bitrate PSNR delta: mean +1.27 dB, range +0.78..+1.93 dB, worse than
  the guarded default mean +1.16 dB.

Bias-off block stats (`target/tmp/stage4a_vbr_newmv_diff_bias_default_block_stats.md`):

- 25k overspent badly (64.7 kbps actual) and raised rvp9 `NEWMV` share to 26.4%
  while libvpx was 8.8%.
- 50k and 100k stayed too `ZEROMV`-heavy, and rvp9 still selected 0.0% 8x8
  partition area at every point.
- 250k nudged motion share in the right direction but was too small to outweigh
  the low-rate regression.

Conclusion: keep `RVP9_NEWMV_DIFF_BIAS=0` as a diagnostic lever only. The next
quality work should target picker RD-domain calibration or a tightly bounded
hybrid, not a blanket VBR disable of the bias.
