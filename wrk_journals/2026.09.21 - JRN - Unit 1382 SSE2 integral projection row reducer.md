# Unit 1382 — SSE2 integral-projection row reducer

Date: 2026-09-21  
Base: integrated `ac99d983`  
Decision: retain and integrate.

## Candidate

The fresh Crucible profile attributed 356,065 weighted samples (about 1.60%
of rvp9 user-symbol weight) to `fill_int_pro_rows`. The scalar helper summed
16 columns one byte at a time for every projection row. This unit adds SSE2
reducers for 16-, 32-, and 64-row projections and dispatches them on x86-64
for both direct windows and fully x-interior, y-clamped windows. AArch64,
scalar, and Miri paths remain unchanged.

The change is in:

- `crates/rvp9-core/src/dsp/x86_64/int_pro_sse2.rs`
- `crates/rvp9-core/src/mv_search/int_pro_me.rs`

## Correctness

- `cargo fmt --all --check` passed.
- Local AArch64 `cargo test -p rvp9-core --lib int_pro_me -- --nocapture` passed
  15/15 tests.
- Crucible x86-64 release `cargo test --release -p rvp9-core int_pro --lib`
  passed 19/19 tests, including direct and clamped SSE2 differential cases.
- The new tests cover heights 16/32/64, stride jitter, unaligned starts,
  random data, zero rows, and 255-valued rows. The existing mixed-edge and
  interior projection oracles also exercise the dispatch.
- Deliberately adding one to the low-lane normalized output made the Crucible
  x86 debug suite fail the direct reducer test and both existing projection
  path tests. Restoring the implementation made all 19 tests pass.

## Crucible timing

Both screens used native release binaries on the Ryzen 9 9950X3D host, three
1280×720 clips, CPU-used 6, CBR 1260 kbps, 30 fps, one thread, zero tile
columns, AQ0, noise0, 60 frames, keyframe interval 60, one warmup, 15 pairs,
two order rotations, and no `max-load1` cap. The ratio is candidate/control;
lower is faster.

| screen | ducks | in-to-tree | old-town | corpus separate | corpus paired |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0.972260× | 0.985769× | 0.982169× | 0.980049× | 0.986990× |
| 2 | 1.008863× | 0.976401× | 0.966252× | 0.983672× | 0.977510× |

The paired corpus gain repeats at 1.30% and 2.25%. Screen 2 reached 25.27
busy logical cores at its peak load sample; this is within the agreed relaxed
Crucible policy for this machine. The full manifests and raw summaries were
retained during validation at the corresponding `unit1390-sse2-row-screen1`
and `unit1390-sse2-row-screen2` scratch directories on Crucible.
