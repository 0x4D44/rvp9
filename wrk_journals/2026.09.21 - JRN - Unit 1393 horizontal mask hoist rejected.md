# Unit 1393 — horizontal AVX2 mask hoist (rejected)

## Decision

Reject and revert. The experiment constructed the four invariant shuffle masks
once per `convolve8_horiz_avx2` call and passed them into the inlined row and
pair helpers. The shipped diff was reverted in commit `8f3a5c36`; the branch's
final shipped tree is identical to integrated commit `09796b9b`.

## Evidence

- `cargo fmt --all -- --check` passed.
- Cross-target `cargo check -p rvp9-core --target x86_64-apple-darwin --release`
  passed (the host's native `apple-m4` flag was ignored for this cross target).
- Crucible x86-64 release AVX2 convolution differential tests passed: 4/4,
  including all filters and tight/odd shapes.
- The short relaxed screen used three alternating pairs and two rotations over
  ducks, in-to-tree, and old-town. Candidate/control streams were byte-identical
  in every paired output, but the candidate was slower on every clip. The
  corpus paired ratio was `1.011992x`: ducks `1.019505x`, in-to-tree `1.005273x`,
  and old-town `1.011250x`.

The full 15-pair screen was not run because this short screen already failed
the 1.00x retention bar. The evidence is retained under
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-21-unit1393-screen-quick/`.
