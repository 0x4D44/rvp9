# Unit 1365 — half-pel bilinear specialization rejected

## Decision

Do not keep the `[64, 64]` fast path experiment. It used AVX2 `avg_epu16` to
replace the exact half-pel multiply-and-shift in
`crates/rvp9-core/src/dsp/x86_64/subpel_variance_avx2.rs`, including the generic
two-pass path and the two identity-axis shortcuts.

## Evidence

A short ducks-only screen was faster (`0.985645x` paired), but the full
three-clip screen against Unit 1364 measured `1.010119x` paired and `1.018988x`
separate. All stream hashes remained identical. The full run had high Crucible
load at preflight, yet every clip's separate ratio was at or above 1.003x and
the corpus regressed, so this is not a useful Tesco to retain.

The experimental source was removed manually and the worktree is clean at
committed Unit 1364 `ecfb17ff`. The full manifest, raw rows, summary CSV, and
decision summary are retained in
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-19-unit1365-half-rejected/`.

## Follow-up

Only revisit half-pel specialization if a fresh profile shows a phase-specific
hotspot or a lower-noise screen can demonstrate a repeatable gain.
