## Hypothesis

The final inter residual-emission walk still used a scalar source-minus-
prediction loop on x86-64. The existing, differential-tested SSE2 square
subtraction kernel already supports 8x8, 16x16, 32x32, and 64x64 blocks and
can report the non-zero flag during the write. Re-export that entry point
through `crates/rvp9-core/src/dsp/accelerated.rs` and use it for supported
final TX sizes, retaining the scalar path for rectangles, 4x4 blocks, and
architectures without the kernel.

## Decision

Retain the candidate commit and evidence on this task branch. It is an
output-exact, reproducible plain-release gain above the 1% retention bar, and
the reuse-profile direction check is strongly positive. Do not integrate or
push without the partner's explicit go. A fresh canonical 28-row production-
PGO rebuild is still required before calling this a production-qualified PGO
gain; no such build was claimed here. The candidate remains far from parity:
the current direct pinned-libvpx comparison is `1.628697x` on plain release,
and this kernel alone cannot make the `<=1.05x` target.
