### T-5 — Tile dispatch profiling

**H1 strongly confirmed: serial work dominates at 57.8% of frame time.**

Per-phase breakdown (10,300 rows, tile_1x4 t=4):

| Phase | Median (μs) | Share | Serial? |
|---|---|---|---|
| loopfilter | 3,448 | 35.5% | YES |
| join (parallel decode) | 3,809 | 39.3% | parallel |
| merge memcpy | 1,246 | 12.8% | YES |
| grid_stitch | 663 | 6.8% | YES |
| setup | 142 | 1.5% | YES |
| border extend | 105 | 1.1% | YES |
| submit | 20 | 0.2% | YES (trivial) |
| mutex lock-in/out | <1 | <0.01% | — |

**H2 (mutex overhead) decisively rejected** — lock-in + lock-out < 0.01%.

**Recommended fix:** pipeline loopfilter asynchronously against next-frame tile decode (libvpx's `vp9_loop_filter_worker.c` pattern). Would reduce serial fraction from 57.8% → ~22.4%, predicting 4-core scaling 1.79× → 2.35×.
