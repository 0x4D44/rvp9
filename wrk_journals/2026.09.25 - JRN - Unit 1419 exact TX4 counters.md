# Unit 1419 — exact-output TX4 candidate counters

The Unit 1418 TX4-off run changed IVF bytes and could only bound cost
directionally. I therefore added temporary, feature-gated counters around the
existing selector without changing its choices. A focused counter test failed
deliberately on a wrong expected eligible-call count, then passed. The
AArch64 feature test target compiled and the focused test passed; Crucible's
x86 release feature build completed. The three 60-frame CPU6 diagnostic IVF
hashes matched the integrated baseline exactly.

The temporary source patch and 177 raw per-frame counter lines are archived
under `wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-25-unit1419-tx4-candidate-counters/`.
TX4 ran on every eligible selector call and averaged 9.75, 12.58, and 13.22
tiles per attempt on Ducks, Tree, and Town. Most attempts pruned; the measured
TX4 candidate occupied 34.7%, 38.4%, and 40.5% of selector timer sums.
Those instrumented shares are a whole-candidate ceiling. They do not say
what a four-tile batch can save, nor establish a wall-clock gain.

The local source was restored to its integrated blob. The remaining design
choice is whether to prototype a new bit-exact four-TX4 batch kernel and
screen it, given its complexity and uncertain gain. No codec code from the
diagnostic should land.
