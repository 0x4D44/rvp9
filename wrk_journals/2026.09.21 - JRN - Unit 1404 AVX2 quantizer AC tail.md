# Unit 1404 — AVX2 quantizer AC tail

## Change

The x86-64 fixed-point quantizer now processes the supported coefficient
blocks in complete eight-lane AVX2 chunks starting at raster offset zero. It
keeps the separately-quantized DC result in locals and restores lane zero
after the vector loop. This removes the seven-element scalar AC tail without
changing the DC rounding path.

The change is committed as `84f85dec` (`perf(core): vectorize AVX2 quantizer
AC tail`).

## Correctness evidence

- `cargo fmt --all --check` and `git diff --check` passed.
- AArch64 local quantizer suite passed: 52 tests.
- x86-64 Crucible quantizer suite passed: 4 tests.
- A deliberate red mutation wrote the DC result to `qcoeff[1]`; the scalar
  equivalence oracle failed on `Tx4x4 qcoeff`. Restoring lane zero returned
  all four x86 tests to green.
- The candidate and control produced byte-identical IVF output for all three
  canonical clips in both timing screens.

## Crucible timing

The paired candidate/control corpus ratios were `0.990091x` and `0.993158x`
on two independent 3-pair, two-rotation screens. Each clip was faster in
both screens. The raw harness data and manifests are under
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-21-unit1404-screen/`.

Against pinned libvpx `12f3a2ac-libvpx`, the fresh relaxed screen measured
`1.613798x` overall (ducks `1.670395x`, intree `1.638377x`, oldtown
`1.535727x`). RVP9 remains materially slower than the reference; this unit is
an incremental RVP9 baseline gain, not parity.

The candidate is retained for its repeatable approximately 0.8–1.4% local
speed gain and is ready for integration.
