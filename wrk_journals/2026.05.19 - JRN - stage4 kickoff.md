### S4.07 — `write_compressed_header` + prob-diff writer (`4dc028b`)

Agent S4.07 dropped at 48 tool calls due to auth/quota; pre-report.
On-disk work was solid — supervisor fixed two small issues before
commit:
- `const _: u8 = DIFF_UPDATE_PROB;` outside test-module scope (the
  const was meant to suppress an unused-import lint that didn't
  actually exist; tests have their own scoped import).
- `#[allow(clippy::too_many_arguments)]` on
  `write_frame_reference_mode_probs` (8 args by design — three
  (pre, post) pairs gated by `reference_mode`).

Strategy B for the writer API:
`write_compressed_header(bw, pre_fc, post_fc, opts, target)`. Caller
owns both `pre_fc` (entry-bank state) and `post_fc` (target probs);
writer emits gates + deltas for any field where they differ. Force-
update-on-any-diff policy (no cost-benefit); the encoder above us
decides which probs to change. Symmetric `prob_diff_writer` module
mirrors the libvpx `vp9_subexp.c` algorithm verbatim (MAP_TABLE,
recenter_nonneg, remap_prob). rvp9-core 576 → 594.

### Open observations / supervisor-on-self notes

- The supervisor-and-team pattern works well when agents have well-
  bounded tasks (>200 ll, <800 ll). Below that, agent overhead
  dominates (~300 ll brief + 5-10 min wall vs ~10 min of supervisor
  direct writing). Direct writing is the right call for the small
  container pieces (S4.08, S4.09).
- Two agent drops this drive (S4.02 socket; S4.07 auth). Both
  recovered by supervisor inspecting on-disk work, running gates,
  fixing small issues, committing. The lesson holds: **agents
  produce durable artifacts; trust the disk, not the report**.
- Inter-frame ref-size inheritance is the only deliberate wire-format
  divergence so far in the encoder. Decode-equivalent but adds 32
  bits per inter frame. Can be backfilled without parser change.
- The `crates/rvp9-decoder/src/lib.rs` change in S4.05 (hoist
  `build_frame_mi_grid` out of LF branch + publish via accessor) is
  a per-frame allocation on the decode hot path. Negligible for
  validation use; if a benchmark surfaces it, the next-frame mi_grid
  can reuse the previous Vec capacity via `clear()` + reserve.
