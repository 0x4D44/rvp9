## 2. Root-cause map (from Phase-0 + 5 investigations, all evidence-backed)

- **C1 — Keyframe over-allocation (FIXED, pending land).** Uncapped first-KF went near-lossless (q≈4, 152k bits) at low bitrate, starving the GOP. Budget-ratio-gated cap re-instated (caps only when `target_bpm < bits_per_mb(Key, best_quality)`): +0.43 dB @100k, **zero** high-bitrate regression (preserves f971c4e). Implemented, validated, uncommitted → finishing via review process.
- **C2 — Partition 16×16 cap: NOT a lever (rejected).** Relaxing `variance_hint_bounds` shifts the histogram toward libvpx but quality is flat/worse at saturated q (A7 trap). Do not touch.
- **Lever 3 — Inter-coding RD inefficiency (THE prize, ~6 dB). ROOT-CAUSED:**
  - **3a (highest leverage) — picker/emit skip decoupling (H2, CONFIRMED).** Picker's skip tie-break (`inter.rs:2656-2666`) prices 98.9% of leaves at the ~2-bit skip rate with prediction-only distortion; the emit independently re-decides skip via `leaf_total_eob==0` (`lib.rs:5263`) and actually codes residual 97% of the time (mean 78 nonzero coeffs/leaf the picker assumed away). At high q the residual self-quantizes → total ZEROMV/skip bias (90%) → reconstruction ≈ stale LASTFRAME → the gap. The rate model is calibrated (ratio≈1.0) and the buffer is healthy, so the high inter-q (224–255 vs libvpx 114–160) is a *symptom* of this inefficiency, not a controller bug.
  - **3b — RD calibration (coef_rate/lambda).** Q-mode RD curves: rvp9 spends more bits AND gets lower PSNR at matched q (curve shifted right/down). `coef_rate` K1 scale (live=2 vs recommended=4) + `compute_rd_mult` must be calibrated *as a pair* — but only meaningful once 3a lands (the skip tie-break currently discards `coef_rate`). Likely required to realize the full 6 dB.
  - REFUTED: NEWMV disabled (it runs), MV-cost over-charged (0.00 dB), active_worst q-floor (inert at fixed bitrate), rate-model miscalibration (ratio≈1.0).
- **Speed — 13.6× gap: UNPROFILED.** Needs a profiling pass before any HLD.
