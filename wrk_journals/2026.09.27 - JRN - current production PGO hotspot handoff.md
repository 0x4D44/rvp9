# Current production PGO hotspot handoff

The dual-TX chroma residual-reuse prototype was exact but slower in two paired screens; its code stayed out of `origin/main`. The earlier post-chroma instruction trace had used a plain-release binary, so its hotspot ranking was only exploratory. I built the current integrated source on Crucible with the production 28-row PGO recipe, then captured exact Ducks, Tree, and Town WPR traces. The Tree smoke and all full IVFs matched canonical hashes; PE/PDB identity and zero ETW event/buffer loss were verified. [The PGO profile](../wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-27-current-pgo-ip/README.md) is the new attribution reference.

The PGO image inlines much of the Ducks work into `pick_and_simulate_inter_encode_partitions`: 5,200/16,158 direct samples in the broad closure, with 1,794 attached to the early chroma-proof call at `lib.rs:11766` and 1,036 to TX selection at `lib.rs:11818`. Motion search and filter evaluation remain large. These are source-label counts, not removable CPU. The unchanged direct speed baseline is 1.061826× pinned libvpx. A new hypothesis must identify the actual instructions or count a repeated operation, then pass exact-output differentials and paired PGO CPU.
