### INV-A result — f971c4e rationale → C1 has a CLEAN conditional fix shape

`f971c4e` (0x4D44, 2026-05-27, "fix(rc): remove KF active-quality cap — first Track-F win") deleted `KF_ACTIVE_QUALITY_BASIS_CAP=122` from the CBR KF arm. **It gained +1.17/+1.18 dB at 1200k/2000k** (KF was floored at q57/42.08 dB regardless of bitrate; uncapped it goes near-lossless, lifting the whole GOP since inter frames predict from it). **Its gain is entirely HIGH-bitrate** (large GOP buffer affords a near-lossless KF). The journal explicitly flagged the LOW-bitrate case ("buffer drains on the KF") as a **separate, unfixed, second-order issue** — which is exactly C1's regime.

⇒ **C1 and f971c4e do not conflict — different regimes.** The cap was always a stand-in for libvpx's content-aware `bits_per_mb` (which lands foreman KF at q57 organically because the KF target is only ~74 kbit; rvp9's enumerator under-predicts → KF runs to q4 unless bounded). **Recommended fix (option A): a budget-ratio-gated floor** in the first-frame KF arm (`cbr.rs:572`) — keep `rc.best_quality` (near-lossless) when the GOP/buffer can afford it (high bitrate, preserves f971c4e); apply `get_kf_active_quality(122)` floor when the KF target is large vs the GOP budget (low bitrate, restores C1's +1.09 dB). Self-scaling, faithful to libvpx (which spends proportionally less on the KF as bitrate drops). Do NOT gate on `rc_mode==Cbr` alone (re-breaks the 1200k CBR regime). **Tests at risk:** 2 byte-identity goldens (`w_buffer_pool.rs:217`, `w_gop_topology.rs:300`) — re-capture only if the gate moves their (~1 Mbps) fixtures' KF q. **Uncertainty:** 250k–1200k band unmeasured → validate gate behaves (gain low, no-regression high) before committing.
