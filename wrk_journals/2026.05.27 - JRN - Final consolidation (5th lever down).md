## The earned, durable principle

**Non-PGO and isolated microbenchmarks systematically overstate gaps.**
The warm out-of-order core + PGO's indirect-call promotion + i-cache
locality erase call-envelope and indirection costs that cold-path benches
loudly advertise. Five times, four wrong. The infamous "intra kernels are
10-25× slower than libvpx" was a cold-call mirage — in the real hot loop
our predictors run at ~1ns ≈ libvpx's 0.6ns.

**Only PGO end-to-end A/B with interleaved drift-cancelling iterations on
the actual gate fixture is trustworthy.** This is now baked into every
sign-off condition for future work.

abstraction / memory).

## Paths beyond autonomous reach

Two genuine options remain, both warranting strategic + tooling decisions:

1. **Within-tile row-MT *decode* port** (libvpx `row_mt=1`). ~400-700 LOC,
   architectural, prior Wave-2 row-MT regressed 1.5×. Attacks parallelism
   at the tile-internal level. Could plausibly reclaim ~10-15% if sync
   overhead stays below idle reclaimed — but the overlap-lever miss
   suggests the threading inefficiencies may already be PGO/scheduler-
   captured similarly. Multi-day commitment with real regression risk.
2. **VTune-guided diffuse-work reduction.** VTune Profiler not installed;
   needs an interactive 1-2GB install + admin driver. Microarch view
   would localize the ~2-3× diffuse cost (cache misses, branch mispredicts,
   port utilization) the inline-blob PGO profile cannot decompose.
3. **Retarget "match vpxdec"** to inter-heavy real-world content where
   the kernel-audit workstream's *real* (PGO-confirmed-needed-but-not-yet)
   convolve8 / idct32x32 levers apply — different fixture, different gap
   composition.
