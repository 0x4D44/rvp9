# Unit 1388 — static A4 descent picker policy rejected

## Decision

Reject and do not retain the code change. The candidate hoisted frame- and
superblock-invariant policy values out of the A4 speculative descent callback.
It preserved output bytes, but two exact Crucible screens disagreed: the first
showed a 0.991327× paired corpus ratio and the repeat showed 1.006296×. The
candidate code was reverted, leaving only this audit record and its evidence.

## Why this candidate

The integrated x86-64 profile at `a94c8ce8` attributes 12.543% of 19,597,341
weighted RVP9 user samples to the A4 picker descent closure
`pick_and_simulate_inter_encode_partitions::closure$10`. The callback rebuilt
several frame- and SB-invariant values on each speculative block. This unit
computed those values once per SB and reused them without changing picker
choices or final-leaf setup.

## Correctness evidence

- `cargo fmt --all -- --check` and `git diff --check` passed.
- The focused policy-oracle test
  `descent_static_picker_config_preserves_frame_and_sb_policies` passed.
- The full local encoder library passed: 780 tests passed and one was ignored.
- The x86-64 cross-test build (`x86_64-apple-darwin`) passed.
- Deliberate red run: changing `allow_golden_ref: !force_disable_golden_ref`
  to the inverted policy failed the focused oracle with the expected
  `allow_golden_ref` mismatch. Restoring the policy returned it to green.
- Crucible release builds completed for both candidate and control.
- All three candidate/control streams were hash-exact on both screens, with
  one distinct hash per arm and clip. The result is therefore speed-only.

## Crucible screens

Both screens used CPU-used 6, CBR 1260 kbps, 30 fps, one thread, zero tile
columns, AQ 0, zero noise sensitivity, 60 frames, KF interval 60, one warmup,
15 alternating pairs, two reversed clip-order rotations, and no `--max-load1`
cap. Ratios are candidate/control; lower is faster.

| screen | clip | separate | paired | candidate ms/frame | control ms/frame |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| A | ducks | 1.006888× | 1.001815× | 20.3500 | 20.2108 |
| A | in-to-tree | 0.982364× | 1.006811× | 13.1790 | 13.4156 |
| A | old-town-cross | 0.971672× | 0.965861× | 30.9362 | 31.8381 |
| A | three-clip corpus | 0.986865× | 0.991327× | — | — |
| B | ducks | 1.008248× | 1.009011× | 19.1007 | 18.9445 |
| B | in-to-tree | 0.998933× | 1.004324× | 12.3364 | 12.3495 |
| B | old-town-cross | 1.009653× | 1.005559× | 11.2675 | 11.1598 |
| B | three-clip corpus | 1.005600× | 1.006296× | — | — |

Screen A recorded high unrelated host load (roughly 19–23 busy logical
cores), while screen B recorded a quiet host (roughly 0.5–3.0 busy cores).
That load difference and the opposite corpus results make the small apparent
gain non-reproducible. No pinned-libvpx comparison was warranted.
