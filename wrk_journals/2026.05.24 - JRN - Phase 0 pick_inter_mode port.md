## What's done

8 commits, ~2000 net LOC added. The picker is restructured to match
libvpx's RT flow: RT_INTER_MODES interleaved iteration, model_rd
variance-domain RD as the primary cost model, adaptive TX-size
selection, skip_txfm short-circuit, intra-in-inter evaluation,
unconditional filter search, unconditional skip tie-break, no area
gates, mv_pred SAD seeding.
