# Unit 1375 — A4 descent quantizer cache (rejected)

## Hypothesis

The A4 descent callback rebuilt `init_plane_quantizers` and
`plane_dequant_pair` for every block-size visit, even though the effective
qindex is fixed for each segment during one superblock descent. Caching those
values per segment should remove repeated setup work without changing the
selected tree or the final per-leaf path.

## Correctness

The candidate compiled and passed these release tests on the AArch64 host:

- `fixed_tree_hint_segment_grid_matches_point_lookup`
- `realtime_cached_full_pick_requires_context_and_non_newmv_winner`
- `descent_pick_cache_covers_complete_superblock_key_space`

The candidate and current-trunk control both built on Crucible x86. The
relaxed paired screen reported one distinct, stable IVF hash per arm and every
candidate hash matched its control hash:

- control SHA-256: `cd09379c323aabc967f56b13b83b9614727a84c6d29b9fa95b4cdaa58b572030`
- candidate SHA-256: `1c5236c3db4641380f8cf74f7a0ca2aa145c16abebfc71ebd5e6f8f9374e9827`

## Crucible screen

`encode-headtohead`, three 1280×720 clips, 60 frames, CPU-used 6, CBR 1260
kbps, one thread, two reverse rotations, one warmup, seven alternating pairs.
The strict host-load ceiling was omitted under the agreed Crucible relaxed-load
policy.

The candidate is 3.86% slower on the paired corpus and slower on all three
clips. The measured result is inside the 1.05× safety ceiling but fails the
Tesco requirement to retain only reproducible gains.

## Decision

Reject and do not integrate. The source experiment was reverted in this
worktree; this journal preserves the negative evidence for future selection.
