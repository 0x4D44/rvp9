# Unit 1370: picker evaluated-candidate counter rejected

The Unit 1366 Crucible profile made the inter-mode picker a reasonable place
for a small Tesco. I tested replacing the repeated `mode_checked` population
count with an incrementing `evaluated_candidates` counter, preserving the
existing `> 1` early-break condition.

The candidate passed the focused inter-mode suite on both architectures:
95/95 on local AArch64 and 95/95 on Crucible x86-64. A deliberate `> 0`
mutation did not trip that unit suite, but a real 60-frame ducks encode
changed its hash; that mutation was restored before timing.

Three relaxed-load, 20-pair plain-release screens were output-exact. Their
three-clip geometric means were 0.993341x, 0.997710x, and 1.024106x; the
aggregate across all nine clip pairs was 1.004961x. The first two apparent
gains were below the 1% retention bar and the third was a clear regression.

I rejected the candidate, removed the source change, and kept this evidence
so the next Tesco pass does not revisit the same bookkeeping shortcut.
