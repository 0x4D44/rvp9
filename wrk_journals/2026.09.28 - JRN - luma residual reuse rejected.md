The current integrated parity remains 1.057638× pinned libvpx equal-clip CPU, above the 1.05× target. A distinct emit-path experiment reused the TX selector's full-leaf luma residual instead of re-subtracting each selected TX tile. Exact local and Crucible checks passed, but the natural production-PGO candidate lost 3.1067% equal-clip CPU and 4.6940% Ducks CPU against the pinned integrated image in 15 pairs per clip. [Raw evidence and the unintegrated patch](../wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-28-luma-residual-reuse-rejected/README.md) are durable. Do not land `f9295dc1` or re-run this implementation from a hotspot label alone.

The next parity experiment needs a fresh operation-level hypothesis. The integrated Ducks profile remains broad in picker and TX-selection work, but prior coefficient forwarding, residual forwarding, NSTEP restarts, identity-scale setup, subpel bounds hoisting, and model16 variance variants all failed paired PGO gates. Count an exact redundant operation before another full-PGO build. Keep the relaxed Crucible load policy: paired process CPU is the decision metric; a perfectly idle host is not required.
