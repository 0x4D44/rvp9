# Unit 1414 — EOB1 coefficient cost

The integrated Unit 1412 profile put the inter TX-size selector callsite at a
3.09% directional whole-encoder ceiling. A temporary, feature-gated Crucible
histogram on the exact integrated source found EOB 1 in 700,679 of 6,471,286
selector quantized blocks (10.83%) across 59 inter frames of 1080p Ducks.
EOB 0 dominated (86.87%) but already had an early return. The diagnostic
output matched the integrated control bit for bit; its patch and raw histogram
are archived under `wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-23-unit1414-eob1-cost/` and
the diagnostic code was removed.

The bounded candidate adds an EOB1 branch to `cost_coeffs`: price the DC token,
its extra bits, and the band-1 EOB directly instead of constructing the band
walker. It preserves saturating addition and also matches the generic path for
an inconsistent EOB1/zero-DC call. The independent table-lookup differential
covers all four TX sizes, token categories/signs, plane/ref selectors, and
coefficient contexts. A deliberately wrong EOB band failed (`2869 != 4227`);
the restored test passed on AArch64 and x86 release. A 60-frame candidate
Ducks encode matched the exact integrated control IVF hash.

Two independent Crucible screens used the same exact control/candidate
binaries, three pinned 1080p clips, one warmup, five matched pairs, four
clip-order rotations, and no host-load ceiling. Their paired corpus ratios
were 0.988787 and 0.980597 (candidate faster by about 1.1% and 1.9%). Ducks
won 13/20 and 16/20 pairs; Tree and Town were near neutral, with 9–11 wins
of 20 per screen. The candidate/control hashes matched and were stable.
Individual pair ratios still had multi-fold stalls under 9–24 busy logical
cores out of 32, so the size of the gain is uncertain; the repeat supports
retaining the small exact change, not a claim of parity.

A fresh pinned-libvpx screen gave a 1.433202 paired RVP9/libvpx corpus ratio
(2.536224 Ducks, 1.177385 Tree, 0.985861 Town), above the 1.05 target. Its
Ducks candidate arm had three 6.87–18.37-second stalls while the reference
arm topped out at 2.98 seconds. That makes the direct number a poor trend
comparison with Unit 1410's 1.227734; both numbers leave parity unproven.
Full raw rows and manifests are archived beside the histogram.

Read-only follow-up audits found no justified narrow next edit in the x86
quantizer or 16×16 variance kernel. The quantizer's hottest EOB reduction is
already retained; nearby alternatives were rejected. The variance four-way
accumulator is already unrolled in optimized x86 code, and the earlier inline
hint regressed. The next unit should re-profile the integrated build or
discuss a larger selector/final-emission reuse design before changing it.
