# Unit 1295 — bounded source-line attribution and parity pause

## Outcome

The integrated trunk is unchanged at `e3e1e65a` (Unit 1294's default-path
audit). A bounded source-line pass over the retained Unit 1293 Crucible ETL
did not justify another micro-candidate. It gives one useful direction for a
future design pass: the emission-time transform selector and the mandatory
inter-mode evaluator deserve attribution with a trace built specifically for
those functions. No shipped code changed in this unit.

## Candidate disposition

The direct full-trace attribution still rules out the previously proposed
low-ceiling rewrites: `model_rd_for_sb_y_large` accounts for only 149,549
direct `rvp9_core` samples (`0.1454%`), and `subpel_variance_avx2` for 153,665
(`0.1494%`). A wrapper around either cannot clear the one-percent whole-encode
retention bar.

The current x86 `fdct32x32` dispatch remains scalar in
`crates/rvp9-core/src/transforms/forward.rs`; only AArch64 selects a vector
kernel. The source-line weight at the emission selector is enough to justify a
separate audit of the scalar 32×32 transform, but not enough to claim its
whole-encode ceiling. Any future SIMD attempt needs a design note, exact
scalar differential coverage, deliberate red-test evidence, and a full
production-PGO Crucible comparison before retention.

## Parity checkpoint

The accepted Unit-1293 timings remain the current evidence:

- plain candidate/control: `0.987768x` paired;
- production-PGO repeats: `0.984338x` and `0.981972x` paired;
- direct pinned-libvpx: `1.516455x` paired and `1.519847x` separate.

The current RVP9 frame times for ducks, in-tree, and old-town are
`22.333/13.788/13.080 ms`; pinned libvpx measures
`14.471/9.034/8.776 ms`. Reaching the `<=1.05x` target therefore needs about
30.8% less RVP9 runtime. That is a multi-unit kernel or algorithm project, not
a routine cache or dispatch cleanup. A looser “parity-ish” point such as 1.3×
would still require about 14.3% less runtime.

The safe resume point is a fresh x86 production profile with source-level
attribution around the emission selector and inter-mode evaluator, followed by
one bounded transform experiment only if its measured span supports a credible
whole-encode gain. The Crucible policy can remain relaxed: the recorded runs
already tolerated substantial background CPU without invalidating the paired
hash-stable comparisons.
