- vpxdec values shifted slightly between the two sweeps (e.g. 1080p60 t=8: 484.4 → 514.3 µs). This is unpinned-host noise on a short measurement (R=3); the underlying vpxdec binary is unchanged. The PGO row's higher vpxdec value happens to wash out PGO's rvp9 win for the headline fixture in the ratio column — look at the absolute p50 column or Δ% table for the cleaner read.
- `testsrc_720p30 t=1` no-PGO p50 (4843.8 µs) is essentially flat vs morning (4859.0 µs, −0.3%). Wave 1 and Wave 4 didn't help this fixture much; PGO is what moves it (−16.3%). This is consistent with this fixture being intra/loopfilter-dominated rather than IDCT/residual-allocator-dominated.
- Mean-vs-p50 spread is wide on the 1080p multi-thread rows (e.g. 1080p60 t=8 PGO: p50=3176, mean=3437, p95=6600) — the tail is what's killing those ratios. The 1080p60 t=4/t=8 mean is roughly 8% above p50 because of long-tail frames; investigating that tail is its own work item.
- The 2× gate is currently a 1-of-9 (no-PGO) / 2-of-9 (PGO) state. Closing the remaining seven needs more than PGO; the W1+W4 momentum on 1080p multi-thread (−17 to −30% Δ%) suggests the IDCT/residual-pool levers still have headroom and should be pushed next.
