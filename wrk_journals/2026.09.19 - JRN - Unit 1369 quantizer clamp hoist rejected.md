# Unit 1369 — quantizer clamp constants rejected

The Unit 1366 profile left the AVX2 fixed-point quantizer at 9.278% of
weighted x86 samples. I screened the smallest obvious loop cleanup: hoist the
two `_mm256_set1_epi32` clamp constants outside the AC loop.

The x86 differential passed 4/4, and a deliberate zero-ceiling mutation made
the scalar-equivalence test fail before restoration. A 20-pair alternating
Crucible screen then showed a 1.05936x three-clip geomean, with regressions on
ducks and in-tree despite identical output hashes. The source change is not
retained. The result is a useful warning that fewer apparent loop instructions
can lose to register pressure or scheduling in this kernel.
