## M.5 dispatched — bool-coder cursor bisect

Strongest hypothesis on inspection: the **per-bmi `get_y_mode_probs` neighbour-mode lookup** for sub-8 luma. The 4 bmi mode reads use neighbour-dependent prob tables; if our neighbour lookup picks a different prob row than libvpx, the decoded mode happens to match (because the bitstream just happens to yield Dc/H/Dc/Dc anyway) but **bits consumed differ** → cursor drift → sub-1's decode_coefs reads from wrong bitstream offset.
