# Unit 1420 — 16-lane AVX2 quantizer rejected

The current exact Crucible profile put the AVX2 quantizer at 6.85% of encoder
self-sample weight. A bounded 16-lane i16 prototype fused quantization and EOB
tracking, while preserving scalar DC and the old i32 fallback. The x86 release
differentials passed 8/8; mutating the DC/EOB condition produced a deliberate
red and restoration returned green. Both screens emitted byte-identical IVFs.

The initial six-pair Ducks screen reported 1.685x candidate/control, but its
raw pairs swung widely in both directions under host contention. It was not a
defensible rejection. A 30-pair, two-rotation Ducks screen reported 1.012607x
paired and 1.009047x separate candidate/control. That showed no gain, so the
prototype source was reversed after archiving its exact patch. No codec change
was integrated. Raw manifests, rows, hashes, and the patch are in
`wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-25-unit1420-quant16-screen/`.

This leaves the production parity baseline unchanged. The last direct pinned
libvpx comparison remains Unit 1415 at about 1.231x RVP9/libvpx; this unit
does not justify a fresh libvpx run.
