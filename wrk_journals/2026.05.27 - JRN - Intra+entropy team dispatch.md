## Update — intra HLD signed off, implementation dispatched

**Intra HLD (a6f99) landed and reviewed.** Excellent, honest work. Measured
root-cause: the 38% is structural per-block overhead, not slow kernels.
- R1: DC-4×4 kernel does ~2.3ns work but ~16-18ns through dispatch
  (indirection ~7.6-12.9ns/call, blocking inlining of a 2ns kernel).
  ~32,474 intra_predict calls/frame on tile_1x4; 58% are 4×4; DC is 33%
  of calls, 94% both-neighbours.
- R2: build_intra_predictors over-copies the `above` row (~9.7ns) vs
  libvpx's zero-copy `const_above_row = above_ref` (~1.6ns).
- Honest H1 correction: libvpx ALSO dispatches via fn-pointer. Our excess
  is a redundant SECOND indirection layer + blocked inlining — lever is
  collapsing layers + inlining, NOT removing dispatch.

**Signed off Phase 1** (DC-4×4-both inline fast path, ~30 LOC) with the
key condition: **MEASURE ON THE PGO BUILD.** My profiling was non-PGO
(clean symbols) but the headline ships PGO, and PGO's indirect-call
promotion may ALREADY capture the dispatch win — making manual inlining
moot. If the PGO delta is ≈0, STOP and report (clean finding, no dead
complexity). Methodological note: my "38% intra" composition is non-PGO;
the PGO-build fraction may be lower. Phase 1 PoC is the experiment to
settle it. Phase 2 (mode widening) + Phase 3 (Lever B zero-copy above,
start 4×4/8×8-only) gated on Phase 1's PGO result.
