# Unit 1371: AVX2 qcoeff signed pack rejected

I tested a small AVX2 quantizer Tesco from the Unit 1366 profile. The
qcoeff vector is already bounded to signed i16, so `_mm_packs_epi32` can
replace two masks and unsigned packing. I left dqcoeff unchanged because
its scalar cast intentionally keeps low-16-bit wrap semantics.

The candidate passed the x86 AVX2 differential suite (4/4) and the local
AArch64 forward-quantizer suite (24/24). A deliberate unsigned-pack mutation
failed the random negative-coefficient differential, proving the test catches
the relevant sign error. Full output hashes stayed identical.

Two relaxed Crucible screens measured 1.013804x and 1.029438x geometric
means; the six-pair aggregate was 1.021591x. This is a repeatable regression,
so I rejected the candidate, restored the source, and kept the evidence.
