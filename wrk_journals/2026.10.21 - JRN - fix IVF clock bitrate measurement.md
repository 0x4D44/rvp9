# RVP9-BUG-FLU-00058: IVF clock and frame cadence

The beta campaign exposed finite but false compression gains near -97%. Libvpx writes a 1000 Hz IVF timestamp clock with 33/34-tick frame spacing at30 fps; the benchmark assumed one tick per frame. A60-frame stream therefore appeared to last0.06 seconds instead of2 seconds.

The fix measures the complete CFR duration from each role's effective encoder FPS, honoring the last role-specific FPS override. It independently validates the IVF clock and all packet timestamps against that cadence, allowing floor/ceil tick quantization, coarse-clock repeated timestamps and a starting offset. Invalid/truncated timing fails before CSV publication. Source-Y4M cadence is not substituted. This changes benchmark evidence, not codec output.

Focused checks passed23 unit and5 integration tests, configured Clippy and formatting. Five retained original/mutant failures cover real millisecond clocks, restoring clock-based duration, disabled timestamp validation and ignored role overrides. A non-fixing reviewer found no Blocker/Issue; the lead independently reran the28 tests and actual two-scene rate replay. All24 retained real bitrate values match independent bytes/2-second arithmetic; pinned libvpx self-comparison reports+0.0000%. These diagnostics do not replace the complete beta matrix. Evidence lives in wrk_docs/evidence/2026-10-21-beta/bug58-fix/.

The fix is claimed by the lead and must land alone. Different-verifier closure and fresh full beta quality campaigns follow; no invalid campaign result is accepted.
