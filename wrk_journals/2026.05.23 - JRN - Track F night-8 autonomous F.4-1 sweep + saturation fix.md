## Discovery on review (night-8 first hour)

**The sweep was measuring the wrong code path.**

The picker DOES NOT call `block_yrd()` (the canonical reference
function in `mode_decision/block_yrd.rs`). It uses an **inlined
copy** of the math inside `reconstruct_via_round_trip` in
`mode_decision/inter.rs` (lines 1012-1184). The same `(2 +
VP9_PROB_COST_SHIFT)` shift sits at `inter.rs:1180`, with its own
u32::MAX saturation at `inter.rs:1182-1184`.

The sweep agent patched `block_yrd.rs:157` (the canonical reference)
but the picker's inlined math at `inter.rs:1180` was untouched. The
"identical K1 ∈ {0..4} results" wasn't a saturation diagnosis — it
was a **patches-had-no-effect** finding. The R1 escalation was
premature.
