# Chroma residual reuse prototype

The post-integration plain-release IP profile attributed 798/19,488 Ducks direct samples to the new early U/V chroma proof, 655 around its residual loop. That is attribution, not removable CPU. A bounded candidate reused each 8×8 source-minus-prediction residual for TX8 and the four TX4 quadrants, preserving the old per-quadrant zero skip. The local commit is `88cfdd57`; its old-shape differential was deliberately red under a wrong fourth-quadrant offset, then green after restoration. AArch64 and x86 focused checks passed.

Two independent paired production-PGO Crucible screens kept every exact-output encode but measured candidate/control equal-clip process CPU at 1.021189× (5 pairs) and 1.011443× (10 pairs). Ducks itself was 1.004831× and 1.003055×. The changed traversal and quadrant bookkeeping cost more than the avoided pixel subtraction in this form. The code is rejected and must not be integrated. Evidence, binary identities, training manifests, the paired runner, and all raw observations are in [the evidence directory](../wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-27-chroma-residual-reuse/README.md).

The integrated parity reading remains 1.061826× pinned libvpx, about 1.11% of current RVP9 CPU above the 1.05× bar. Next: profile the exact current PGO control rather than extend this plain-release-sample hypothesis; choose a distinct operation with a larger measured saving ceiling.
