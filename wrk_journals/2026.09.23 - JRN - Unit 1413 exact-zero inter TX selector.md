# Unit 1413 — exact-zero inter TX selector

The integrated Unit 1412 PDB attributes 3.09% of raw encoder samples to the
`choose_inter_luma_tx_size` callsite, a directional ceiling rather than a
saving. This candidate avoids its transform/quant/rate walk only when the
already-built luma prediction residual is exactly zero. It still chooses the
TX size by the same high-to-low candidate order, zero-EOB context-0 rate,
TX-size signaling rate, saturating RD score, and strict tie rule. Ordinary
nonzero residuals take the unchanged full path after a short-circuit scan.

The focused zero-residual differential compares the fast branch with a
const-disabled exhaustive selector across six leaf shapes, qindex edges,
both quantizers, both TX4 policies, and synthetic signaling costs. A
deliberately wrong Tx4 return failed (`Block8x8`, q0, exhaustive Tx8); the
restored test passed. Native AArch64 `inter_tx_size` tests passed 3/3; fmt
and whitespace checks passed. Native release x86 tests passed 3/3; the
candidate release encoder SHA-256 is
`a6db771f310887a120fcca472c8efc8e191ad256f9bcd769531845d61464d610`.
The control is the exact integrated `cdbf7a6a` release encoder, SHA-256
`adf2da0757c6d9a9f1f37f028159f476ddd5c3055629eb254b2490634935d677`;
the intervening commits through `2b1ae11f` changed docs only.

A separate read-only audit ruled out bypassing this selector from
`pick.skip_txfm`: that picker field is luma-only, while a nonzero chroma EOB
can still leave the inter leaf non-skip and require a signaled luma TX size.

The first complete paired Crucible screen used three pairs and two rotations
without a host-load ceiling. All candidate/control IVF hashes matched and
were stable. Its paired corpus ratio was 1.254033x (candidate slower), but
the raw data contain multi-fold stalls on both arms, so this short screen
alone cannot resolve a small effect. The completed five-pair/four-rotation
screen again had stable, matching IVF hashes, but its paired per-clip ratios
were 1.046531x Ducks, 0.990039x Tree, and 1.026938x Town; the corpus ratio
was 1.020899x, about 2.1% slower. Its separate-median corpus ratio was
0.977761x, an apparent gain that does not survive matched pairing under
load drift. The candidate won only 8/20, 11/20, and 9/20 pairs by clip.
All slow pairs remain in the raw CSV. With no repeatable gain, the candidate
was rejected and its exact patch archived; the source was restored. No new
libvpx comparison was warranted, so Unit 1410's integrated 1.227734x
RVP9/libvpx ratio remains the latest direct measure.
