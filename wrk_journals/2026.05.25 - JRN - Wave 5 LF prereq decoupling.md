## Why the predicted -15..-25% didn't materialise

1. **Different base.** The survey's prediction was for post-W1+W4,
   where the 1080p30 t=4 frame total is 3 256 us and LF mask build is
   estimated 280-400 us = ~10% of frame. On this worktree's pre-W1+W4
   base, frame total is ~4 635 us; the same mask build is roughly the
   same absolute cost but a smaller share of the larger frame. A
   ~150 us absolute saving (the W5 parallelism gain with 4 cores) is
   ~3% of 4 635 us — which is exactly what we measured.

2. **No decode overlap.** Without W2's C2 signal, the mask build
   runs *after* tile-decode joins, not overlapped with the slowest
   tile. The survey's higher-end prediction (~-25%) assumed the
   overlap captures the long-tail tile_decode P95 (3 354 us median
   vs 1 087 us / core). That gain isn't reachable on this base
   without re-porting W2.
