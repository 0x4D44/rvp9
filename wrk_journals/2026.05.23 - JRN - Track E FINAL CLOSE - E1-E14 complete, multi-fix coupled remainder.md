## Confounded gap → clean residual

**Before Track E:** foreman BD-rate **+395%** — a single confounded number.
**After Track E:** the gap is *decomposed* into:

- **+27 pp closed** by E2 (partition thresholds).
- Bitrate **overshoot eliminated** by E6/E7/E8 (KF lever + RC feedback bug). Inter bitrate 252 → 143.9 kbps vs libvpx 143.1 (0.6%).
- **−41% (then realistically −20%) inter coefficient bits** from the KF reference-quality cascade (E6 mechanism, E7 calibration).
- Full-pel diamond search **recovered +5.34 dB of NEWMV prediction PSNR** (E13) — the search now finds real motion (75.6% diamond-recovers-fraction, was 2%).
- **Residual gap: ~9 dB matched-bitrate prediction-quality deficit** (rvp9 31.5 dB recon vs libvpx 39.2 at q=120 / 142 kbps). BD-rate now "not measurable" — the curves don't overlap because rvp9's PSNR (24-27 dB across operating points) sits entirely below libvpx's (28-37 dB).

The "not measurable" is an honest consequence of fixing the overshoot:
rvp9 now operates at the *correct* bitrates with insufficient quality,
rather than overshooting with confounded quality. The BD-rate gate harness
should grow a matched-bitrate-PSNR fallback.

## The remaining gap — exact diagnosis

Five experiments (E5 RD-skip falsified, E11 partition refuted under
broken search, E13 search fixed alone, E14 partition refuted again under
fixed search) converge on **one mechanism**:

> rvp9's picker RD models **mode-rate + mv-rate but NOT coefficient bits**.
> So even when NEWMV finds a genuinely better motion-compensated
> prediction (E13), the picker can't see that NEWMV would save residual
> bits — only that it costs +5000 prob-cost in mv-rate. NEAREST
> (predicting (0,0) for free from the chicken-and-egg degenerate
> `find_mv_refs`) always wins the visible RD.

Three coupled fixes are needed; **no single one closes the gap**:

1. **`block_yrd` / transform-domain RD** — port libvpx's coefficient-bit-aware RD
   into the picker's `evaluate_candidate`, so NEWMV's residual savings
   become visible. (`vp9/encoder/vp9_rdopt.c:vp9_block_yrd` + the rate
   estimation in nonrd pickmode.) **The primary lever**, but bounded by
   per-block coef savings vs mv-rate at the partition granularity.
2. **`find_mv_refs` / NEAREST quality** — once block_yrd makes block_yrd
   makes *some* block pick NEWMV, the propagation closes the
   chicken-and-egg (NEAREST starts predicting from real motion for free
   in neighbours). Likely needs the temporal MV path working (A18
   prev_frame_mvs salvage is plumbed but currently no-op).
3. **Motion-aware partition decision** — libvpx's nonrd evaluates modes
   (incl. NEWMV with the working search + block_yrd) at each level and
   only splits when motion-comp RD doesn't justify the larger block.
   Replaces the current variance-of-ZEROMV-residual partitioner.

This is the libvpx `vp9_pick_inter_mode` realtime / nonrd pickmode
machinery in its entirety. Multi-day, possibly multi-week. The three
fixes interact: block_yrd alone may not flip NEWMV at 8×8 (per-block
coef savings still < mv-rate); but combined with motion-aware larger
partitions AND find_mv_refs propagation, the cluster closes.
