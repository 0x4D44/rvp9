# In-place large-model RD break

The integrated production-PGO CPU ratio remains 1.054068× pinned libvpx across Ducks, Tree, and Town, just outside the ≤1.05× target. The wrapper-based large-model TX-cell early exit was exact but slower. An in-place version tested whether the wrapper caused that regression. It preserved the production call shape and used a test-only full-loop oracle with a deliberate red visit assertion. AArch64 and native x86 focused tests passed.

Two paired Crucible production-PGO screens of the in-place candidate were also slower: 1.031453× control over five pairs per clip and 1.022915× over 15 pairs per clip. Every IVF hash matched. [Raw evidence](../wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-27-large-model-rd-in-place-pgo/README.md) is integrated; code commits `b46fcd8e` and `be8e88836` stay local and unpushed. This closes the wrapper hypothesis. Do not retry a TX-cell early break without evidence of a materially different operation or code-generation approach.

Next bounded candidate should target a demonstrably removable part of the Ducks hot path. The x86 AVX2 subpel checked-load machinery is one candidate from the instruction profile, but any prototype needs exact-width buffer safety, focused scalar differential and deliberate-red evidence, then paired production-PGO CPU improvement before integration.
