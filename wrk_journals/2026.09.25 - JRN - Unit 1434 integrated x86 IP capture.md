# Unit 1434 — integrated x86 IP capture

The prior one-clip Tree audit could not certify a new three-clip profile because its general extractor was missing. I rebuilt it as the checked-in `wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-25-unit1434-three-clip-ip-attribution/extract-ip.py`, replayed Unit 1432's archived raw sample and IP maps with zero per-IP content mismatches, and deliberately rejected an old-EXE/new-PDB pairing. The only archived mismatch was an incorrect derived `line_ok` flag for one numeric-zero IP; the numeric line and all counts matched. The extractor needed eager DbgHelp loading and the full module-info structure to verify the exact loaded PDB path.

I profiled the integrated `88644d86` x86 build on Crucible with one 60-frame WPR CPU-memory trace per canonical CPU6/CBR1260 clip. All Ducks, Tree, and Town IVFs matched their existing exact hashes. The three xperf `tracestats` reports each show zero lost buffers and zero lost events. Raw direct sample rows, per-IP maps, symbol/line summaries, capture logs, runner, and extractor are in [the evidence set](../wrk_docs/evidence/2026-09-25-unit1434-three-clip-ip-attribution/README.md). The ETLs stay in the Crucible task worktree's `.scratch` with their SHA-256 values in that README. Sampling is a hotspot map, not a new speed measurement; the last separate paired pinned-libvpx process-CPU ratio is 1.182520×.

Two tool edges surfaced and were fixed before trusting the maps. PowerShell `Get-Command wpr.exe` returned two applications; the first Ducks preflight tried to invoke their concatenated paths and stopped before WPR started. The runner now selects one application. xperf also printed malformed CSV quoting in an unrelated `Unknown` process row; the extractor now skips only malformed non-target rows while failing on malformed encoder rows. The saved Ducks ETL was re-extracted after that parser fix, so no repeat capture was needed.

The clip mix is not uniform. Tree and Town put about 13–15% each in `reconstruct_via_round_trip` and AVX2 sub-pixel variance; Ducks is led by inter-partition simulation, AVX2 quantization, and SAD4D. Source line `inter.rs:5590` is a macro-expansion attribution, not yet a proven single instruction or operation. Previous variants at both hot kernels regressed or were neutral, so a new experiment needs finer disassembly/work-mix evidence and the usual exact differential and paired process-CPU screen. No new encoder code was changed in this unit.
